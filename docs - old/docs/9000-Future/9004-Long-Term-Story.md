# 9004 — Long Term Story

## Purpose

This document contains the long-term direction of Project Elysium.

It defines how the world can continue growing through years of expansions, updates, and new adventures.

---

# Story Ages

## The First Age

The launch storyline.

Focus:

* Introducing the world
* Establishing factions
* Creating player identity
* Discovering the first major threats

---

## The Second Age

The first expansion era.

Focus:

* Expanding the known world
* Revealing ancient mysteries
* Introducing new civilizations

---

## The Third Age

The world begins changing permanently.

Focus:

* Major conflicts
* Fallen kingdoms
* Global events
* New alliances

---

## The Fourth Age

The story expands beyond the original world.

Focus:

* Other realms
* Ancient powers
* Unknown civilizations
* Greater threats

---

## The Final Age

The conclusion of a major story cycle.

Focus:

* The greatest conflicts
* The fate of the world
* The legacy of players

---

# Long-Term Principles

* The world should continue evolving.
* Previous events should have consequences.
* Player achievements should remain meaningful.
* Expansions should connect to existing lore.
* No update should make previous content irrelevant.

---

# The Endless Journey

Project Elysium is designed as a living world.

Every victory creates new questions.

Every discovery reveals something unknown.

Every legend becomes part of history.
