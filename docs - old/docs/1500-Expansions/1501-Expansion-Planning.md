## 1. Overview

Expansion Planning is the process that turns a future-direction idea into a scoped, scheduled expansion with clear pillars, content lists, and success criteria.

---

## 2. Steps

1. Graduate idea from Future Plans  
2. Define fantasy, continent/system focus, and level band  
3. Draft story, world, and feature outlines  
4. Estimate art, design, and engineering cost  
5. Place on roadmap when capacity exists  

---

## 3. Rules

Expansions must reinforce the vision and pillars. They expand the world; they do not discard prior content.
