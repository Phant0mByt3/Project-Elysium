## 1. Overview

Character Art covers the visual design and production of playable races, NPCs, and humanoid entities: proportions, silhouette, material treatment, and customisation ranges.

---

## 2. Principles

- Silhouette readability at combat distances.
- Racial identity preserved under customisation.
- Shared base meshes and texture approaches where possible for efficiency.
- Alignment with the grounded high-fantasy tone.

---

## 3. Scope

Player races, major NPC archetypes, and the customisation options exposed at character creation and through later cosmetics.
