## 1. Overview

Cinematics art direction covers camera language, staging, lighting, and performance style for scripted story sequences. The goal is emotional clarity and world immersion, not Hollywood length or complexity.

---

## 2. Principles

- Cameras serve the story and character faces/actions.
- Lighting and environment reinforce regional identity.
- Performances match the animation style and tone of the game.
- Sequences are paced for the medium (in-engine, skippable, relatively short).

---

## 3. Collaboration

Cinematics are a joint product of Narrative, Animation, and Environment Art, implemented through the client cutscene tools.
