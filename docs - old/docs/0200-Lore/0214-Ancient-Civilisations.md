## 1. Overview

Ancient civilisations are the cultures that existed during the Age of Concord and earlier. Their ruins, artefacts, and lost knowledge form a major exploration and narrative pillar of Elysium. Players rediscover them; the world does not explain them all at once.

---

## 2. Known Ancient Civilisations

### The Concord Civilisation
The dominant multi-racial society of the Age of Concord. Its capital, Aethercrest, was destroyed in the Sundering and now lies beneath the Shattered Cairns (launch raid). Concord architecture, roads, and magical infrastructure still underpin many modern settlements.

### The Pre-Concord Kingdoms
Fragmentary records speak of independent human, elven, and dwarven realms that existed before the Circle’s full influence unified the continent. Some landmarks and deep ruins pre-date Concord styling.

### The Forge Clans of Terravox
Ancestral dwarven culture that later became the Ironpeak Holds. Their deepest halls and runic forges are still being rediscovered.

### The Star-Singers (High Elven)
An early high-elven scholarly tradition focused on celestial magic and the mapping of the gods’ will. Many mage academies claim descent from them.

### The Beastkin Ancestors
Nomadic cultures that once ranged across what is now the Kharzul Wastes and parts of Vethmoor. Their stone circles and buried cities will become more prominent in Expansion 02.

### The Sundered Remnants
Not a civilisation in the conventional sense, but the scattered, twisted survivals of peoples caught at the epicentre of Kaelgorath’s attack. Closely tied to the origin of the Revenant race and the future Nightreach continent.

---

## 3. Design Use

- Ruins and landmarks should feel layered: Concord over Pre-Concord, modern reuse over ancient foundations.
- Loot, relics, and legendary items frequently carry “of the Old Concord” or similar flavour.
- The Cartographer’s League and Ashen Circle are the primary in-world organisations studying these civilisations.
- Future expansions deliberately open older layers that were only hinted at in launch content.

---

## 4. Rules for Writers & Builders

1. Never present a complete, tidy history of any ancient civilisation at launch; leave room for later revelation.
2. Visual language (architecture, motifs, materials) should be consistent enough that players can learn to recognise “Concord-era” versus “pre-Concord” versus “Sundered”.
3. New ruins introduced in later content should feel like natural extensions of the foundations already laid here and in [0105-Landmarks.md](../0100-World/0105-Landmarks.md).
