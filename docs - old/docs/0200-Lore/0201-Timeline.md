# 0201 — Timeline

Chronological history of Elysium. See [0206-History.md](0206-History.md) for expanded detail on individual events, and [0200-Lore.md](0200-Lore.md) for the thematic overview.

## Age of Dawn *(mythic era)*
The Elysian Circle — seven gods (see [0202-Gods.md](0202-Gods.md)) — shape Elysium from the void and seed it with the founding races ([0204-Races.md](0204-Races.md)).

## Age of Concord *(~1,200 – 800 years ago)*
A golden age of cooperation between the mortal races under the loose guidance of the Circle. The great pre-Sundering capitals are built; magic and craftsmanship flourish. Kaelgorath, the eighth god, grows resentful of mortal free will and is cast out of the Circle's councils.

## The Sundering *(800 years ago)*
Kaelgorath attacks the Circle directly, attempting to unmake Elysium. The ensuing divine battle tears the world's single continent into fragments, silences the gods, and kills an uncountable number of mortals. Entire kingdoms vanish overnight. This single event divides Elysium's history into "before" and "after."

## Age of Ash *(800 – ~100 years ago)*
Civilization collapses into survivalist city-states. Old knowledge is lost. The first Revenants — mortals twisted by lingering Sundering energy — appear during this era ([0204-Races.md](0204-Races.md)). The Dawnbound Concord and Duskward Pact both trace their founding to the latter half of this age, as rival philosophies for how mortals should rebuild.

## Age of Reclamation *(present day)*
Continents have drifted close enough to be reachable again. Old magic is resurfacing. Ruins from the Age of Concord are being rediscovered (and looted) faster than they can be studied. Player characters begin their journey here — see [0207-Main-Story.md](0207-Main-Story.md) for how the current story picks up.

## Timeline System

Elysium uses historical phases to preserve major events without deleting old versions of the world.

**Example:**
```
Aurelia Timeline

Era of Origin
        |
        |
Collision Event
        |
        |
Era of Fracture
```

Players can experience different points in history depending on progression.
