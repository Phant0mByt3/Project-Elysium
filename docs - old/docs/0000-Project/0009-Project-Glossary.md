# 0009 — Project Glossary

**Project:** Elysium MMORPG  
**Category:** Project  
**Status:** Living Document  
**Related:** [0006-Documentation-Guide.md](0006-Documentation-Guide.md)

---

A living reference of terms used throughout the Elysium documentation. New terms should be added here when introduced in any design document.

---

## A

**Age of Ash** — The centuries following the Sundering during which civilisation collapsed into survivalist city-states. See [0201-Timeline.md](../0200-Lore/0201-Timeline.md).

**Age of Concord** — The golden age before the Sundering when the mortal races cooperated under the guidance of the Elysian Circle.

**Age of Reclamation** — The present era in which the continents are reconnecting and players begin their journeys.

**Aethercrest** — The lost original capital of Elysium, destroyed during the Sundering; its ruins form the basis of the launch raid “The Sunken Concord.”

**Ashka Vor** — Capital city of the Duskward Pact, carved into a volcanic caldera in Vethmoor.

**Aurum** — Primary soft currency of Elysium. See [1001-Currency.md](../1000-Economy/1001-Currency.md).

---

## C

**Concord / Dawnbound Concord** — One of the two player factions. Believes the world should be rebuilt in the image of the Age of Concord. See [0203-Factions.md](../0200-Lore/0203-Factions.md).

**Core Pillars** — The six non-negotiable design principles that every feature must support. See [0002-Core-Pillars.md](0002-Core-Pillars.md).

---

## D

**Duskward Pact** — The rival player faction. Believes the old order failed and survival requires strength and new alliances.

---

## E

**Elysian Circle** — The seven gods who shaped the world. Silent since the Sundering. See [0202-Gods.md](../0200-Lore/0202-Gods.md).

**Elysium** — Both the name of the game and the name of the world.

---

## G

**GDD** — Game Design Document. The entire `docs/` directory is the living GDD for Project Elysium.

---

## I

**Instance** — A private or semi-private server process hosting a dungeon, raid, or event. See [1209-Instance-System.md](../1200-Technical/1209-Instance-System.md).

**Ironpeak Hold** — Dwarven capital city in Vethmoor.

---

## K

**Kaelgorath** — The Fallen God, once the eighth member of the Elysian Circle. His attack caused the Sundering. Fragments of his influence remain the primary endgame threat.

---

## R

**Revenant** — A playable race of mortals permanently marked by lingering Sundering energy. See [0204-Races.md](../0200-Lore/0204-Races.md).

---

## S

**Solmere** — Capital of Aurelia and seat of the Dawnbound Concord.

**Sundering** — The cataclysm 800 years ago that tore the single continent of Elysium into fragments and silenced the gods.

---

## V

**Vethmoor** — Second launch continent (levels 25–50). Highland, volcanic, and contested between dwarves and orcs.

---

## W

**WorldPainter** — External terrain-authoring tool used to create the base heightmaps and biome layouts before hand-detailing. See [1210-World-Management.md](../1200-Technical/1210-World-Management.md).

---

*This glossary is expanded whenever a new proper noun or system term is introduced in the documentation.*
