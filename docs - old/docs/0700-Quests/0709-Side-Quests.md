## 1. Overview

Side Quests are optional narrative and gameplay content that enrich the world without gating the Main Quest. They give secondary NPCs their own arcs, deepen regional conflicts, and reward exploration and roleplay.

---

## 2. Design Principles

- Every side quest (or short chain) has a clear beginning, middle, and end.
- Side quests should never be required to understand the Main Story.
- Regional side quests tie into the “local conflict” defined for that region.
- Longer, multi-region side stories exist for players who want deeper lore or character arcs.

---

## 3. Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Regional** | Contained within one region; supports the local story |
| **Character-driven** | Focused on a single NPC or small cast across multiple steps |
| **Exploration** | Unlocked or advanced by discovering landmarks or secrets |
| **Faction** | Available or coloured by faction allegiance |
| **Profession / Reputation** | Gated or rewarded through profession or rep progress |

---

## 4. Rewards

Side quests grant experience, Aurum, gear, reputation, and occasionally unique cosmetics or recipes. Rewards are tuned to feel worthwhile without overshadowing Main Quest or dungeon progression.
