# 0307 — Elements

## Elemental Damage Types
* **Fire** — associated with Ignareth; applies Burning (damage over time).
* **Frost** — associated with the Mage's Frostweaver spec; applies Chilled/Frozen (slow/root).
* **Nature** — associated with Aeloria; applies Poisoned (damage over time, reduces healing received).
* **Shadow** — associated with Nyxara/Voranthe; applies Weakened (reduced outgoing healing and damage).
* **Holy** — associated with Solthar; primarily a healing-amplifying and undead-damage-bonus type rather than a damage-over-time source.
* **Arcane** — unaligned with the Circle; associated with raw Mage burst damage, no default status effect.
* **Physical** — the default damage type for melee/ranged weapon attacks, mitigated by Armor rather than Resistance ([0304-Stats.md](0304-Stats.md)).

## Interactions
Certain elemental combinations produce bonus effects when applied to the same target in sequence (e.g. Frost followed by a Fire hit "shatters" for bonus damage), giving multi-class groups a reason to coordinate application order in dungeons and raids.

## Resistances
Enemy types have thematic resistances/weaknesses (undead resist Shadow and are weak to Holy; fire elementals resist Fire and are weak to Frost) documented per-enemy as bestiary content is developed alongside regions ([0102-Regions.md](../0100-World/0102-Regions.md)).

## Balance
Elemental damage bonuses and resistances are tuned as part of the ongoing balance process — see [0309-Balance.md](0309-Balance.md).
