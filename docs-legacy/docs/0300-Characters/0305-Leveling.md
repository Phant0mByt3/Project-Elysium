# 0305 — Leveling

## Level Range
Launch level cap: **50**. Character progression is intentionally front-loaded with skill unlocks ([0302-Skills.md](0302-Skills.md)) and back-loaded with talent choices ([0303-Talent-Trees.md](0303-Talent-Trees.md)), so early levels teach the class and late levels let players build it.

## Experience Sources
* Quest completion (primary source, especially through the 1–30 range).
* Dungeon clears ([0106-Dungeons.md](../0100-World/0106-Dungeons.md)).
* Exploration and landmark discovery ([0105-Landmarks.md](../0100-World/0105-Landmarks.md)).
* Minor experience from kills, intentionally secondary to avoid mindless grinding, per Pillar 5 in [0002-Core-Pillars.md](../0000-Project/0002-Core-Pillars.md).

## Pacing
The leveling curve is tuned against the regional level ranges in [0102-Regions.md](../0100-World/0102-Regions.md) so that a player completing a region's available quests naturally arrives at the next region already at or near its minimum level — no filler grinding required to "catch up."

## Rewards at Key Levels
* **Level 10** — Specialization choice ([0301-Specializations.md](0301-Specializations.md)).
* **Level 15** — First mount ([0901-Mounts.md](../0900-Player-Systems/0901-Mounts.md)).
* **Level 25** — Cross-continent travel unlocked (Aurelia → Vethmoor, per [0207-Main-Story.md](../0200-Lore/0207-Main-Story.md) Act I conclusion).
* **Level 50** — Level cap; dungeon Heroic mode, raid, and endgame reputation grinds unlock ([0707-Factions-Reputation.md](../0700-Quests/0707-Factions-Reputation.md)).

Future expansions will raise the level cap per their own documents (e.g. [1500-Expansion-01.md](../1500-Expansions/1500-Expansion-01.md)).
