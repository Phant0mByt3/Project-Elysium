# 0208 — Side Stories

Optional questlines and character-driven narratives that run parallel to the main story ([0207-Main-Story.md](0207-Main-Story.md)), enriching the world without gating critical-path progress.

## Design Principles
* Every side story should have a clear beginning, middle, and end — no "quest chain that trails off."
* Side stories should give secondary NPCs ([0209-NPCs.md](0209-NPCs.md)) their own arcs, rather than existing purely to serve the main story's cast.
* Regional side stories tie into that region's "local conflict" field (see [0102-Regions.md](../0100-World/0102-Regions.md) template); continent- or faction-spanning side stories may cross multiple regions.

## Launch Side Stories (examples)

**"The Miller's Debt"** *(Southern Shires)* — a low-level chain following a Millhaven family's struggle against a predatory moneylender, resolving in either a peaceful buyout or the moneylender's exposure as a bandit financier, connecting into the Wildwood Reach bandit questline.

**"Echoes of Aethercrest"** *(cross-continent, levels 20–45)* — an optional chain for lore-focused players, following a Cartographer's League researcher piecing together fragments of the lost capital's fate; culminates just before the main story's Act III, adding context but not required for it.

**"Clanbreaker"** *(Ashenclaw Tundra)* — a Duskward-only chain about a disgraced orc warrior seeking to reclaim their clan name, offering a very different tonal take on Vethmoor's conflict than the Dawnbound-framed main story beats.
