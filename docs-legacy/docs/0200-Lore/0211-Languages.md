## 1. Overview

Languages in Elysium exist primarily for flavour, immersion, and occasional puzzle or reputation gating. They are not a full gameplay system that requires players to “learn” languages to progress the main story. Instead they enrich dialogue, item names, inscriptions, and cultural identity.

---

## 2. Primary Languages

| Language | Speakers | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| **Common** | All playable races | The shared trade and diplomatic tongue. Virtually every NPC can converse in Common. |
| **Aurelian** | Humans of Aurelia | Formal, slightly archaic variant of Common used in Concord documents and high society. |
| **High Elven** | High Elves | Elegant, precise; many ancient texts and mage academies still use it. |
| **Dwarven** | Dwarves of the Ironpeak Holds | Practical, rune-heavy; strongly associated with craft and stonework. |
| **Orcish** | Orc clans of Ashenclaw | Harsh, direct; clan-specific dialects exist. |
| **Beastongue** | Beastkin | A family of related dialects; more prominent once Kharzul Wastes opens. |
| **Revenant Cant** | Revenants | Soft, measured speech with occasional archaic or “echoed” phrasing. Rarely taught to outsiders. |
| **Old Concord** | Scholars, relics | The formal language of the Age of Concord. Appears on ancient inscriptions, Aethercrest ruins, and certain legendary items. |

---

## 3. Design Use

- NPC dialogue can include occasional non-Common words or titles for flavour; the Dialogue System ([0210-Dialogue-System.md](0210-Dialogue-System.md)) always provides a Common translation or context.
- Inscriptions in dungeons and landmarks may require a reputation or profession perk (e.g. Cartographer’s League or Archaeology-style profession) to fully decipher.
- Item names and flavour text occasionally use authentic linguistic flavour without blocking understanding.
- No language barrier ever prevents acceptance or completion of a main-story or critical side quest.

---

## 4. Future Expansion

Additional languages (Sylvaneth wood-elven dialects, Nightreach corrupted tongues, etc.) are introduced with their respective continents and documented here when they become player-facing.
