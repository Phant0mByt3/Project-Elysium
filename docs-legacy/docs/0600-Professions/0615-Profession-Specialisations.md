# 0615 — Profession Specialisations

## 1. Overview

Profession specialisations allow a player who has reached a high skill threshold to focus on a particular branch of their profession (e.g. armour vs weapons for Blacksmithing, potions vs flasks for Alchemy). Specialisation deepens identity and creates economic niches without locking players out of the rest of the profession.

---

## 2. Structure

- Specialisation is chosen (or unlocked) at a defined skill gate, typically in the Expert or Artisan range.
- Each profession offers 2–3 specialisations.
- Specialisation grants access to exclusive recipes, passive bonuses, or improved outcomes for its focus area.
- Respeccing is possible for a cost so that players can adapt to market or personal preference changes.

---

## 3. Design Rules

1. Specialisations should feel different in play and in the market, not just numerically better at the same crafts.
2. A non-specialised crafter can still produce baseline goods; specialisation is an advantage, not a hard requirement for participation.
3. Specialisation choices are visible on the character or profession sheet so that other players can find the right crafter.

---

## 4. Examples (Illustrative)

- **Blacksmithing**: Weaponsmith / Armoursmith / Specialist Tools
- **Alchemy**: Combat Potions / Utility & Flasks / Transmutations
- **Leatherworking**: Combat Gear / Utility & Bags / Decorative & Cosmetic

Exact lists are finalised per profession during Phase 2 implementation.
