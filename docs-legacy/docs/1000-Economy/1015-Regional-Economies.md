## 1. Overview

Regional Economies recognise that different parts of Elysium produce different goods and have different needs. Materials, crafted items, and even some vendor stock reflect the geography, culture, and conflict of each region.

---

## 2. Expression

- Gathering nodes and mob drops are region- and biome-appropriate.
- Local vendors may offer unique or cheaper access to regional specialties.
- Quest and event rewards sometimes use local materials or currency flavours.
- Flavour text and NPC dialogue reinforce what a region is known for producing or lacking.

---

## 3. Design Rules

1. Regional flavour should be noticeable without making logistics painful (players can still move goods via Auction House and travel).
2. No region is designed to be economically isolated to the point of frustration.
3. Future continents will introduce new regional specialties that feed into the broader economy.
