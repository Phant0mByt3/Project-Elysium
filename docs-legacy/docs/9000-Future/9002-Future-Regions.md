# 9002 — Future Regions

## Purpose

This document contains planned continents, regions, dimensions, and locations that are not currently available.

These areas may be referenced through:

* NPC dialogue
* Ancient texts
* Maps
* Quests
* Hidden discoveries

---

## Region Structure

Every region should contain:

* Name
* Location
* Environment
* History
* Civilizations
* Major Locations
* Enemies
* Resources
* Dungeons
* Future Content

---

# Region Categories

## Hidden Continents

Large unexplored lands beyond the known world.

Examples:

* Lost kingdoms
* Forgotten civilizations
* Unknown territories

---

## New Realms

Locations outside normal reality.

Examples:

* Elemental realms
* Shadow dimensions
* Celestial worlds
* Void regions

---

## Changed Regions

Existing locations transformed by future events.

Examples:

* War zones
* Corrupted lands
* Rebuilt kingdoms

---

# Design Rule

Future regions should expand the world, not replace it.

Old locations should continue having importance even after new content releases.
