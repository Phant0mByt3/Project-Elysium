# 9001 — Future Characters

## Purpose

This document contains unresolved mysteries, forgotten secrets, hidden locations, and unexplained events within the world of Elysium.

These mysteries are designed to create curiosity, exploration, theories, and long-term discoveries for players.

Some mysteries may remain unanswered for years before their true purpose is revealed.

---

## Mystery Structure

Every mystery should contain:

* Name
* Location
* First Appearance
* Current Known Information
* Hidden Truth
* Future Reveal
* Connected Storylines

---

# Mystery Categories

## Lost Civilizations

Ancient societies that disappeared or were forgotten.

Examples:

* Lost kingdoms
* Destroyed cities
* Ancient technologies
* Forgotten cultures

---

## Sealed Locations

Areas that exist but cannot currently be accessed.

Examples:

* Locked temples
* Forbidden ruins
* Ancient gates
* Hidden dimensions

---

## Unknown Entities

Characters or beings whose true identity is hidden.

Examples:

* Mysterious travellers
* Forgotten gods
* Ancient creatures
* Unknown forces

---

## Historical Events

Past events that shaped the world but remain unexplained.

Examples:

* Ancient wars
* Lost battles
* Catastrophic events
* Forgotten ages

---

# Design Rule

Every mystery should eventually have a purpose.

A mystery can remain hidden for a long time, but it should always connect to future world development.
