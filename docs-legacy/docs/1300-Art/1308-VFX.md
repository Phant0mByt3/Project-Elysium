## 1. Overview

VFX (visual effects) cover spell effects, combat impacts, environmental magic, and status visuals. They reinforce elemental identity and combat readability.

---

## 2. Principles

- Elemental colours and shapes stay consistent with [0307-Elements.md](../0300-Characters/0307-Elements.md) and the colour palette.
- Telegraphs for dangerous abilities are unmistakable.
- Density is controlled so that raids and world events remain legible.
- Style matches the grounded high-fantasy direction of the project.

---

## 3. Production

VFX are authored to budgets defined with Performance and Client Optimisation. Shared libraries of beams, bursts, and auras are preferred over one-off effects where possible.
