## 1. Overview

Iconography is the broader visual language of symbols used across UI, maps, factions, and world signage. It extends the specific icon production standards in [1304-Icons.md](1304-Icons.md).

---

## 2. Principles

- Strong silhouettes and limited detail for small sizes.
- Consistent metaphor language (e.g. how “danger”, “quest”, “vendor” are expressed).
- Faction and elemental symbols remain distinct under colourblind simulation.
- Reuse and systematic variation over one-off invention where possible.
