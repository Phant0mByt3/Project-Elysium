# 36 — History

Detailed historical events referenced throughout the game's quests and lore. See [031-Timeline.md](031-Timeline.md) for the high-level chronology this document expands on.

## The Fall of the First Capital
During the Sundering, the original united capital of Elysium — **Aethercrest** — was the epicenter of Kaelgorath's attack and was torn apart mid-collapse. Its ruins now lie beneath the Shattered Cairns in Vethmoor, forming the basis of the launch raid, [017-Raids.md](017-Raids.md)'s "The Sunken Concord."

## The Founding of the Dawnbound Concord
Roughly 150 years after the Sundering, a coalition of human and elven survivor settlements formalized the Dawnbound Concord, explicitly naming itself after the lost Age of Concord as a statement of intent to rebuild it. Their founding charter is a referenced document in several Aurelia quest chains.

## The Ashenclaw Accords
Orc clans, historically fractious, were united into the Ashenclaw Warbands roughly a generation after the Concord's founding, partly in response to Concord expansion into Vethmoor. The Duskward Pact formalized from this alliance shortly after.

## The First Revenant Sighting
The earliest documented Revenant, a Concord soldier who survived a direct brush with Sundering energy at the Aethercrest ruins, is recorded roughly 200 years post-Sundering — the origin point for the Revenant race ([034-Races.md](034-Races.md)).

## Recent History (last 20 years)
Continents have begun drifting measurably closer, reopening travel between Aurelia and Vethmoor for the first time in centuries — this reopening is the inciting event for [037-Main-Story.md](037-Main-Story.md) and the reason player characters' journeys begin now.
