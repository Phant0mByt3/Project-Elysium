# 130 — Art Style

## Overview
Elysium's overall visual identity — the reference point every other art document ([131-UI-Style.md](131-UI-Style.md) through [136-Models.md](136-Models.md)) should be checked against.

## Direction
A grounded high-fantasy aesthetic — closer to classic MMORPG concept art than either Minecraft's blocky vanilla look or a hyper-realistic style unsuited to the engine. Silhouette and readability take priority over fine detail, since combat clarity (Pillar in [44-Combat.md](44-Combat.md)) depends on instantly recognizing enemies, allies, and effects at a glance.

## Regional Identity
Each continent has a distinct visual language reflecting its dominant culture:
* **Aurelia** — warm, golden, pastoral; Concord architecture uses pale stone and gold trim, echoing Solthar ([32-Gods.md](32-Gods.md)).
* **Vethmoor** — cold, angular, weighty; dwarven architecture uses dark stone and iron, orc architecture uses bone, hide, and fire-blackened wood.
