# 102 — Vendors

## Overview
NPC vendors provide a baseline, always-available marketplace independent of player supply — selling reagents, basic gear, repair services, and recipes.

## Vendor Types
* **General Goods Vendors** — basic consumables, repair services, found in every city and village ([13-Cities.md](13-Cities.md), [14-Villages.md](14-Villages.md)).
* **Class Trainers** — teach new skills as characters level ([42-Skills.md](42-Skills.md)); functionally vendors of ability unlocks rather than items.
* **Profession Trainers & Recipe Vendors** — teach profession recipes ([60-Professions.md](60-Professions.md)).
* **Reputation Quartermasters** — faction/reputation-gated vendors selling tier-exclusive rewards ([76-Reputation.md](76-Reputation.md), [77-Factions-Reputation.md](77-Factions-Reputation.md)).
* **Sundered Shard Vendors** — endgame catch-up gear and cosmetics ([101-Currency.md](101-Currency.md)).

## Design Rules
Vendor stock and prices should never directly undercut the player economy's Auction House ([103-Auction-House.md](103-Auction-House.md)) for tradeable goods — vendors exist as a reliability floor, not a replacement for player trading.
