A holding area for long-term ideas that are agreed upon in direction but not yet scheduled on the [0003-Roadmap.md](0003-Roadmap.md). Once an idea here is scoped and prioritized, it graduates into an actual expansion document (see [1501-Expansion-Planning.md](../1500-Expansions/1501-Expansion-Planning.md) and onward). Raw, unscoped brainstorming lives in [9999-Ideas.md](../9000-Future/9999-Ideas.md) instead — this file is for ideas the team has already committed to in principle, even if the "when" is undecided.

---

## How an Idea Gets Here

1. Raised as a raw idea in [9999-Ideas.md](../9000-Future/9999-Ideas.md) or a discipline-specific future document.
2. Discussed against the [0002-Core-Pillars.md](0002-Core-Pillars.md) and the current [0001-Vision.md](0001-Vision.md).
3. If the team agrees the idea belongs in Elysium's future *in principle*, it moves here.
4. When it's time to schedule it, it graduates to an expansion document under [1500-Expansions/](../1500-Expansions/) and is added to the Roadmap.

## Confirmed Future Directions

### World

* **Continent 3 — Sylvaneth**: the elven forest continent, planned as the first major post-launch expansion.
* **Continent 4 — Kharzul Wastes**: a desert continent centered on beastkin nomad culture and ancient ruins.
* **Continent 5 — Nightreach**: the corrupted continent tied to Kaelgorath, the Sundered One — intended as an endgame-focused expansion (see [0206-History.md](../0200-Lore/0206-History.md)).
* **Player Ships**: naval travel and ship customization, extending [0110-Travel.md](../0100-World/0110-Travel.md).

### Social & Player Systems

* **Player Housing Neighborhoods**: guild-owned housing districts, expanding on the housing system in [0900-Player-Systems/](../0900-Player-Systems/).
* **Cross-Faction Events**: limited-time events where the Dawnbound Concord and Duskward Pact cooperate against a shared threat.
* **A Third Playable Faction**: a neutral, mercenary-aligned faction, gated behind reputation rather than race at character creation.

### Systems

* **Expanded Profession Specializations**: deeper mastery tiers for existing professions once the base economy is proven stable (see [0600-Professions/](../0600-Professions/)).
* **Player-Driven Contracts**: an in-game bounty/contract board layered on top of the auction house economy.
* **Guild Wars / Territory Control**: a structured PvP system where guilds contest control of specific world zones for economic bonuses.

## Explicitly Deferred (Not Rejected)

These ideas have support but are deliberately deferred until specific prerequisites are met — they are not in the same category as items in [9012-Rejected-Ideas.md](../9000-Future/9012-Rejected-Ideas.md), which are ideas the team decided *against*.

| Idea | Deferred Until |
| --- | --- |
| Player ships / naval travel | Continent geography supports meaningful sea routes (post Continent 3) |
| Guild territory control | Guild systems and world PvP are proven stable at launch scale |
| Third faction | Two-faction balance is validated over at least one full content season |

## Review Cadence

This document should be revisited at the start of every expansion planning cycle, alongside [0004-Version-History.md](0004-Version-History.md), to decide what graduates onto the active roadmap. The Creative Director and the relevant discipline Leads (see [0007-Team-Structure.md](0007-Team-Structure.md)) jointly own this review.

An idea should not sit in this document indefinitely without review — if it has not been revisited in over a year of active development, it should be re-evaluated against the current Vision and either recommitted to, formally deferred with a clearer trigger condition, or moved to [9012-Rejected-Ideas.md](../9000-Future/9012-Rejected-Ideas.md).
