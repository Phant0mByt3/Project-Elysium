# 17 — Raids

## Overview
Raids are Elysium's endgame group content — larger instances (10–20 players) with multi-phase boss encounters, designed for coordinated guild play. See [80-Guilds.md](80-Guilds.md) and [82-Raiding.md](82-Raiding.md) for the social/organizational systems around raiding.

## Design Principles
* Every boss encounter must have a **mechanic identity** — a phrase players can use to describe it in one sentence (e.g. "the boss that splits into three").
* Raids should tell a complete story arc, usually the climax of a region's or continent's narrative.
* Difficulty tiers: **Normal**, **Heroic**, **Mythic** (guild-only, hardest tuning, cosmetic-only bonus rewards).
* Weekly lockouts per raid, tracked server-side (see [121-Database.md](121-Database.md)).

## Launch Raid — The Sunken Concord *(Vethmoor, Level 50)*
A drowned pre-Sundering Concord fortress beneath the Shattered Cairns, sealed since the cataclysm. The raid's story reveals what really happened to the original Concord leadership during the Sundering, tying directly into [36-History.md](36-History.md) and setting up the first major expansion.

**Planned Wings:**
1. **The Flooded Antechamber** — 3 bosses, introductory mechanics.
2. **The Drowned Court** — 3 bosses, includes a former Concord general as a boss.
3. **The Sundering Vault** — 2 bosses + final encounter against a fragment of Kaelgorath's influence (see [32-Gods.md](32-Gods.md)).
