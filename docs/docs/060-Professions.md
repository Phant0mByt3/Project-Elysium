# 60 — Professions

## Overview
Professions are Elysium's crafting and gathering progression systems, existing alongside — not competing with — combat leveling ([46-Leveling.md](46-Leveling.md)). Every character may learn up to two professions.

## Categories
* **Gathering Professions** — Mining ([61-Mining.md](61-Mining.md)), Woodcutting ([62-Woodcutting.md](62-Woodcutting.md)), Fishing ([63-Fishing.md](63-Fishing.md)), Herbalism ([64-Herbalism.md](64-Herbalism.md)).
* **Production Professions** — Alchemy ([65-Alchemy.md](65-Alchemy.md)), Blacksmithing ([66-Blacksmithing.md](66-Blacksmithing.md)), Cooking ([67-Cooking.md](67-Cooking.md)), Jewelcrafting ([68-Jewelcrafting.md](68-Jewelcrafting.md)), Tailoring ([69-Tailoring.md](69-Tailoring.md)).
* **Universal Profession** — Enchanting ([59-Enchanting.md](59-Enchanting.md)), which every character can learn as an add-on regardless of their two main profession slots.

## Two-Profession Rule
Most players pair one gathering and one production profession (e.g. Mining + Blacksmithing) for self-sufficiency, though pure-gathering or pure-production pairings are also viable, feeding directly into the Auction House economy ([103-Auction-House.md](103-Auction-House.md)).

## Progression
Each profession has its own skill level, capped alongside the character level cap ([46-Leveling.md](46-Leveling.md)), trained through use and recipe/technique unlocks from trainers found in cities and villages ([13-Cities.md](13-Cities.md), [14-Villages.md](14-Villages.md)).
