# 60 — Professions

## Overview
Professions are Elysium's crafting and gathering progression systems, existing alongside — not competing with — combat leveling ([046-Leveling.md](046-Leveling.md)). Every character may learn up to two professions.

## Categories
* **Gathering Professions** — Mining ([061-Mining.md](061-Mining.md)), Woodcutting ([062-Woodcutting.md](062-Woodcutting.md)), Fishing ([063-Fishing.md](063-Fishing.md)), Herbalism ([064-Herbalism.md](064-Herbalism.md)).
* **Production Professions** — Alchemy ([065-Alchemy.md](065-Alchemy.md)), Blacksmithing ([066-Blacksmithing.md](066-Blacksmithing.md)), Cooking ([067-Cooking.md](067-Cooking.md)), Jewelcrafting ([068-Jewelcrafting.md](068-Jewelcrafting.md)), Tailoring ([069-Tailoring.md](069-Tailoring.md)).
* **Universal Profession** — Enchanting ([059-Enchanting.md](059-Enchanting.md)), which every character can learn as an add-on regardless of their two main profession slots.

## Two-Profession Rule
Most players pair one gathering and one production profession (e.g. Mining + Blacksmithing) for self-sufficiency, though pure-gathering or pure-production pairings are also viable, feeding directly into the Auction House economy ([103-Auction-House.md](103-Auction-House.md)).

## Progression
Each profession has its own skill level, capped alongside the character level cap ([046-Leveling.md](046-Leveling.md)), trained through use and recipe/technique unlocks from trainers found in cities and villages ([013-Cities.md](013-Cities.md), [014-Villages.md](014-Villages.md)).
