# 0709 — Side Quests

**Category:** Quests
**Status:** Living Document
**Related:** [0700-Quests.md](0700-Quests.md) · [0208-Side-Stories.md](../0200-Lore/0208-Side-Stories.md)

---

## 1. Overview

Side quests are optional content tied to a region's local conflict, an individual NPC's personal arc, or a piece of world-building not central to the Main Quest ([0708-Main-Quest.md](0708-Main-Quest.md)).

## 2. Categories

* **Regional Side Quests** — support the local conflict defined in a region's design brief ([0102-Regions.md](../0100-World/0102-Regions.md)).
* **Character Vignettes** — short, self-contained quests focused entirely on developing a single NPC, often with no combat objective at all.
* **Exploration Side Quests** — reward discovering a landmark or hidden area ([0105-Landmarks.md](../0100-World/0105-Landmarks.md)) rather than combat.
* **Side Story Chains** — longer, connected side content documented as full side stories in [0208-Side-Stories.md](../0200-Lore/0208-Side-Stories.md).

## 3. Design Standards

* Side quests should never feel mandatory, but should be interesting enough that players choose to do them anyway.
* Every side quest should answer "why does this NPC need my specific help" rather than defaulting to generic errand framing.
* Side quests contribute meaningfully to the region's total quest density target described in [0700-Quests.md](0700-Quests.md), Section 6.

## 4. Reward Philosophy

Side quest rewards are generally comparable to Main Quest rewards for the same level range, ensuring players aren't mechanically punished for pursuing optional content, while the most narratively significant side stories may offer unique items unavailable elsewhere.

## 5. Examples

**The Miller's Debt** (Millhaven) — a short vignette where a player helps a struggling miller pay off a debt to a traveling merchant, with multiple possible resolutions depending on how the player chooses to intervene.

**The Lost Journal** (Sunspire Hills) — an exploration side quest triggered by finding a hidden journal near the Sundered Spire landmark, rewarding a piece of lore and a cosmetic item.

## 6. Writing Standard

Side quests follow the same writing guide as Main Quest content ([1403-Quest-Writing-Guide.md](../1400-Development/1403-Quest-Writing-Guide.md)) but have more latitude for tonal variety — humor, melancholy, and small-scale personal stories are especially well-suited to this category.
