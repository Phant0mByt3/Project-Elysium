# 74 — Achievements

## Overview
Achievements are optional, permanently-tracked accomplishments rewarding exploration, mastery, and dedication beyond the core leveling/gearing loop — directly supporting Pillar 1 in [0002-Core-Pillars.md](../0000-Project/0002-Core-Pillars.md).

## Categories
* **Exploration** — discovering every landmark in a region ([0105-Landmarks.md](../0100-World/0105-Landmarks.md)), fully revealing a continent's map.
* **Quests & Story** — completing every quest chain in a region, finishing the main story ([0207-Main-Story.md](../0200-Lore/0207-Main-Story.md)).
* **Combat & PvE** — dungeon/raid clears on specific difficulties, defeating world bosses ([0108-World-Bosses.md](../0100-World/0108-World-Bosses.md)).
* **PvP** — arena and territory control milestones ([0805-Arenas.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0805-Arenas.md), [0806-Territory-Control.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0806-Territory-Control.md)).
* **Collection** — mounts, pets, cosmetics collected ([0901-Mounts.md](../0900-Player-Systems/0901-Mounts.md), [0902-Pets.md](../0900-Player-Systems/0902-Pets.md), [0903-Cosmetics.md](../0900-Player-Systems/0903-Cosmetics.md)).
* **Profession** — reaching max skill in a profession, crafting rare recipes.

## Rewards
Achievements should primarily reward titles ([0705-Titles.md](0705-Titles.md)) and cosmetics rather than power, keeping them a prestige/completionist system rather than a stealth power progression track.

## Design Rules
Every major system added to the game should be reviewed for at least one achievement tie-in as part of its design, rather than achievements being bolted on after the fact.
