## 1. Overview

Achievements track account-wide accomplishments across every category of play — exploration, combat, professions, quests, and social milestones — providing long-term goals independent of gear progression.

## 2. Achievement Categories

* **Exploration** — discovering landmarks ([0105-Landmarks.md](../0100-World/0105-Landmarks.md)), fully mapping regions.
* **Combat** — dungeon and raid clears, difficulty-tier completions, world boss kills.
* **Quests** — completing quest chains, side stories, faction storylines.
* **Professions** — reaching mastery tiers ([0616-Profession-Mastery.md](../0600-Professions/0616-Profession-Mastery.md)).
* **Social** — guild milestones, group content participation.
* **Collection** — mounts, pets, transmog appearances ([0907-Collections.md](../0900-Player-Systems/0907-Collections.md)).

## 3. Rewards

Achievements grant achievement points (a meta-progression score), and select achievements grant titles ([0705-Titles.md](0705-Titles.md)), cosmetics, or rare mounts/pets unavailable through any other means.

## 4. Design Rules

* Achievements should recognize genuine accomplishment, not just time spent — a "kill 1000 rabbits" style achievement is avoided in favor of achievements tied to skill, exploration, or dedication.
* Hidden achievements are used sparingly for landmark secrets ([0105-Landmarks.md](../0100-World/0105-Landmarks.md)) to preserve discovery surprise.
* Meta-achievements (completing a set of related achievements) reward a capstone title or cosmetic, giving completionists a clear long-term structure.

## 5. UI and Tracking

Achievements are tracked through a dedicated panel accessible from the character menu, organized by category with clear progress bars for multi-step achievements.

## 6. Post-Launch Expansion

Each expansion and major content update introduces new achievement categories tied to new content, ensuring achievement hunters always have fresh goals — see [2001-Updates.md](../2000-Operations/2001-Updates.md).
