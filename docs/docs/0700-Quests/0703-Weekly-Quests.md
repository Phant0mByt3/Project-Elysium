# 0703 — Weekly Quests

**Category:** Quests
**Status:** Living Document
**Related:** [0702-Daily-Quests.md](0702-Daily-Quests.md) · [0107-Raids.md](../0100-World/0107-Raids.md)

---

## 1. Overview

Weekly quests are larger-scope repeatable objectives resetting every 7 days, typically tied to group content completion (dungeons, raids, world bosses) rather than solo hub errands.

## 2. Design Rules

* Weekly quests should encourage engaging with group content the player might otherwise skip, without being mandatory for progression.
* Rewards should be meaningfully better than daily quest rewards, reflecting their larger time investment and coordination requirement.
* Weekly quest objectives should never require a specific difficulty tier beyond Normal, so players of all skill levels can complete them, though bonus rewards may scale with higher difficulty completion.

## 3. Examples

**Weekly Bounty: The Ember Foundry** — requires clearing the Ember Foundry dungeon at any difficulty, rewarding bonus currency and a chance at a bonus loot roll.

**Weekly World Boss Tribute** — requires participating in any world boss kill during the week, rewarding reputation with the relevant regional faction.

## 4. Reset Timing

Weekly quests reset on a fixed schedule aligned with raid lockouts (see [0107-Raids.md](../0100-World/0107-Raids.md) and [2011-Server-Restarts.md](../2000-Operations/2011-Server-Restarts.md)), giving players a predictable weekly rhythm.

## 5. Catch-Up Design

Weekly quests are deliberately generous in their completion window (a full week rather than a tight daily reset) to accommodate players who can only play a few times per week, avoiding the pressure of a daily-login-required system.

## 6. Post-Launch Expansion

As with dailies, new weekly quest objectives are introduced alongside major content updates, typically tied to new dungeons, raids, or seasonal events ([2010-Seasonal-Events.md](../2000-Operations/2010-Seasonal-Events.md)).
