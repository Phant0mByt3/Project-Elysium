## 1. Overview

Major cities are safe hubs containing vendors, trainers, the auction house, bank, and quest hubs. Unlike villages ([0104-Villages.md](0104-Villages.md)), cities are large, faction-aligned, and serve as respawn/recall points.

## 2. Solmere — Capital of Aurelia

Seat of the Dawnbound Concord and the game's primary starting hub. Built around the **Sunspire Cathedral**, a monument to the god Solthar. Contains the Grand Auction House ([1003-Auction-House.md](../1000-Economy/1003-Auction-House.md)), the Adventurer's Guildhall (class trainers), and the Concord Council chambers, a recurring location in the main story ([0207-Main-Story.md](../0200-Lore/0207-Main-Story.md)).

**Districts:**
* **The Sunspire Quarter** — cathedral, temple district, healers.
* **The Merchant Row** — auction house, bank, general vendors.
* **The Guildhall District** — class trainers, guild registration.
* **The Council Ward** — Concord political center, main story hub.
* **The Docks** — departure point for Vethmoor sea routes.

## 3. Ashka Vor — Orc Clan Capital, Vethmoor

Duskward Pact stronghold carved into a volcanic caldera. Home to the Warcouncil of Clans and the primary Duskward class trainers for levels 25+.

**Districts:**
* **The Warcouncil Hall** — clan leadership, faction storyline hub.
* **The Ember Markets** — vendors and auction house access.
* **The Trial Grounds** — orc combat trial content, tied to class trainers.

## 4. Ironpeak Hold — Dwarven Capital, Vethmoor

A fortress-city built into the mountain itself, home to the dwarven forgemasters and the continent's primary blacksmithing and enchanting trainers ([0606-Blacksmithing.md](../0600-Professions/0606-Blacksmithing.md), [0509-Enchanting.md](../0500-Items/0509-Enchanting.md)).

**Districts:**
* **The Forgehalls** — profession trainers, crafting stations.
* **The Deep Bank** — bank and auction house.
* **The High Council Chamber** — dwarven political hub, story content.

## 5. City Design Requirements

* Must contain: bank, auction house access, class trainers for local factions, flight/fast-travel point ([0111-Fast-Travel.md](0111-Fast-Travel.md)), and a guild hall.
* Must be built with distinct architecture reflecting the [1300-Art-Style.md](../1300-Art/1300-Art-Style.md) guide and the dominant race's culture.
* Must tie into at least one main story or faction questline.
* Must be a designated safe zone — no hostile NPC spawns, no open-world PvP unless explicitly flagged.

## 6. City Population and Life

Cities are populated with a mix of functional NPCs (vendors, trainers) and ambient/flavor NPCs whose only purpose is to make the city feel alive — see [0209-NPCs.md](../0200-Lore/0209-NPCs.md) for the NPC writing standard. A city with only functional NPCs and no ambient population reads as a lobby rather than a place, which works against the immersion goals in [0008-Development-Philosophy.md](../0000-Project/0008-Development-Philosophy.md).

## 7. Future Cities

Additional cities are added as continents expand — see [0101-Continents.md](0101-Continents.md) for the full continent list. Sylvaneth, the Kharzul Wastes, and Nightreach will each receive at least one major capital city documented here once world-building for those continents begins.
