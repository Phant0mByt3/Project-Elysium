# 0105 — Landmarks

**Category:** World
**Status:** Living Document
**Related:** [0002-Core-Pillars.md](../0000-Project/0002-Core-Pillars.md) · [0704-Achievements.md](../0700-Quests/0704-Achievements.md)

---

## 1. Purpose

Landmarks are non-quest-hub points of interest — ruins, monuments, natural wonders, and secrets — that reward exploration for its own sake, directly supporting Pillar 1 in [0002-Core-Pillars.md](../0000-Project/0002-Core-Pillars.md).

## 2. Design Rule

Every region must contain at least one landmark that is **not** required by any quest chain. Landmarks may still contain optional lore items, achievements ([0704-Achievements.md](../0700-Quests/0704-Achievements.md)), or hidden vendors.

## 3. Examples

**The Sundered Spire** (Aurelia, Sunspire Hills) — a half-collapsed tower jutting out of the hills at an impossible angle, frozen mid-fall since the Sundering. Climbing it reveals a lore journal fragment ([0201-Timeline.md](../0200-Lore/0201-Timeline.md)) and a hidden achievement.

**The Weeping Hollow** (Aurelia, Greywater Fens) — a sunken grove said to be the site of a pre-Sundering battle. At night, spectral duelists can be seen replaying their final fight — a purely atmospheric encounter with no combat.

**The Emberfall Overlook** (Vethmoor, Ember Deeps) — a cliffside view over the volcanic mines, popular as an informal player gathering and screenshot spot.

**The Cairn of Names** (Vethmoor, Shattered Cairns) — a field of standing stones, each engraved with the name of someone lost in the Sundering; players can add commemorative names via a future community feature (see [0005-Future-Plans.md](../0000-Project/0005-Future-Plans.md)).

**The Old Millhaven Well** (Aurelia, Southern Shires) — a small, easily missed well behind Millhaven that, when interacted with, triggers a short optional flavor vignette about the village's founding.

## 4. Categories

* **Ruins** — remnants of the Age of Concord, tying into [0214-Ancient-Civilisations.md](../0200-Lore/0214-Ancient-Civilisations.md).
* **Natural Wonders** — unique geography with no combat purpose.
* **Secrets** — hidden, unmarked locations found only through exploration or hints, often requiring the class movement abilities described in [0400-Game-Mechanics.md](../0400-Gameplay/0400-Game-Mechanics.md).
* **Memorials** — locations tied directly to lore events in [0206-History.md](../0200-Lore/0206-History.md).

## 5. Reward Structure

Landmarks should never be purely decorative if reasonably avoidable. Each should offer at least one of:

* A lore item contributing to [0209-NPCs.md](../0200-Lore/0209-NPCs.md) or the timeline.
* A cosmetic-only reward (title, transmog piece).
* An achievement contributing to account-wide progression ([0704-Achievements.md](../0700-Quests/0704-Achievements.md)).
* A hidden vendor or recipe, tying into [0600-Professions/](../0600-Professions/).

## 6. Discovery and Map Integration

Landmarks appear on the map ([0112-Maps.md](0112-Maps.md)) only once discovered, encouraging organic exploration rather than checklist-following. A small number of landmarks per region (roughly 10–15%) are deliberately left off any hint system entirely, rewarding players who explore without external guides.

## 7. Placement Process

Landmarks are placed during the "key locations" stage of the region-building pipeline described in [0102-Regions.md](0102-Regions.md), Section 5, alongside cities and villages, ensuring they're considered as part of the region's identity rather than bolted on afterward.
