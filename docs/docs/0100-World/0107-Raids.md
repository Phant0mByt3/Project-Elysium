## 1. Overview

Raids are the highest-tier instanced group content in Elysium — larger group sizes, longer encounters, and the best available gear outside of world-first or seasonal events. Where dungeons ([0106-Dungeons.md](0106-Dungeons.md)) teach mechanics, raids test mastery of them at scale.

## 2. Structure

* **Group size:** 10 players at launch, with the architecture designed to support a 20-player format post-launch if warranted by testing.
* **Wing structure:** raids are divided into "wings" of 3–5 bosses, allowing partial-lockout progression rather than requiring a full clear in one sitting.
* **Lockout:** weekly per-boss lockout, resetting on a fixed schedule (see [2011-Server-Restarts.md](../2000-Operations/2011-Server-Restarts.md)).
* **Difficulty tiers:** Normal, Heroic, and Mythic, mirroring the dungeon tier structure but with a steeper difficulty curve.

## 3. Launch Raid — The Sunken Concord

The launch raid is set within the ruins of **Aethercrest**, the lost original capital of Elysium, destroyed during the Sundering (see [0009-Project-Glossary.md](../0000-Project/0009-Project-Glossary.md)). It is accessed from the Shattered Cairns in Vethmoor once a player reaches the level cap.

**Wing 1 — The Drowned Approach:** three bosses guarding the ruin's flooded entrance halls, themed around water and pressure mechanics.

**Wing 2 — The Concord Archives:** two bosses within the ruined archive halls, themed around information and memory — one encounter requires players to correctly recall a mechanic pattern shown earlier in the fight.

**Wing 3 — The Sundering Scar:** the final wing, culminating in a boss directly tied to the Sundering event itself, serving as the launch raid's narrative and mechanical climax.

## 4. Design Philosophy

Raid encounters are built around the "one new idea per boss" principle: every boss should teach or test one primary mechanic clearly, layered with secondary mechanics as the fight escalates through phases. This keeps encounters legible even at high complexity — see [0403-Boss-Mechanics.md](../0400-Gameplay/0403-Boss-Mechanics.md).

## 5. Loot and Progression

Raid loot is tuned to sit clearly above Heroic dungeon and Mythic dungeon gear, with Mythic raid difficulty producing the best gear available at launch (see [0504-Loot-Tables.md](../0500-Items/0504-Loot-Tables.md)). Loot distribution options (personal loot, master loot) are covered in [0800-Guilds.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0800-Guilds.md) raid coordination tools.

## 6. Post-Launch Raid Cadence

New raid tiers or wings ship roughly once per content season (see [2010-Seasonal-Events.md](../2000-Operations/2010-Seasonal-Events.md)), with major new raids accompanying each continent expansion described in [0101-Continents.md](0101-Continents.md).

## 7. Accessibility Considerations

While raiding is designed as premium group content, Normal difficulty is tuned to be achievable by an organized but non-elite guild, in keeping with Pillar 4 (Multiplayer Encourages Cooperation) from [0002-Core-Pillars.md](../0000-Project/0002-Core-Pillars.md) — raiding should feel aspirational, not exclusionary.
