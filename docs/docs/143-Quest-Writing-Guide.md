# 143 — Quest Writing Guide

## Overview
Standards for writing quest text, ensuring every quest ([070-Quests.md](070-Quests.md)) reads as part of a coherent world rather than generic MMO filler.

## Tone
Grounded high fantasy, consistent with the lore's themes of reconstruction and fragmentation ([030-Lore.md](030-Lore.md)) — sincere rather than jokey, though individual NPCs may have distinct comedic or dry voices per [144-NPC-Writing-Guide.md](144-NPC-Writing-Guide.md).

## Structure
* **Hook** — the first line should establish why the NPC needs the player's help, not just what to do.
* **Objective Text** — clear, concise, in the NPC's voice where possible rather than generic system text.
* **Completion Text** — should acknowledge what the player did, not just hand over a reward blankly.
* **No unmotivated fetch quests** — every "kill X" or "collect Y" objective needs a stated reason tied to the region's local conflict ([012-Regions.md](012-Regions.md)).

## Consistency Checks
* Cross-reference [039-NPCs.md](039-NPCs.md) before introducing a new named character.
* Cross-reference [030-Lore.md](030-Lore.md) through [036-History.md](036-History.md) for any historical or mythological claim made in quest text.
* Main story quests must align with the act structure in [037-Main-Story.md](037-Main-Story.md); side quests should never contradict it.
