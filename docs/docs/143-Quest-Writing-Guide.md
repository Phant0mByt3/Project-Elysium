# 143 — Quest Writing Guide

## Overview
Standards for writing quest text, ensuring every quest ([70-Quests.md](70-Quests.md)) reads as part of a coherent world rather than generic MMO filler.

## Tone
Grounded high fantasy, consistent with the lore's themes of reconstruction and fragmentation ([30-Lore.md](30-Lore.md)) — sincere rather than jokey, though individual NPCs may have distinct comedic or dry voices per [144-NPC-Writing-Guide.md](144-NPC-Writing-Guide.md).

## Structure
* **Hook** — the first line should establish why the NPC needs the player's help, not just what to do.
* **Objective Text** — clear, concise, in the NPC's voice where possible rather than generic system text.
* **Completion Text** — should acknowledge what the player did, not just hand over a reward blankly.
* **No unmotivated fetch quests** — every "kill X" or "collect Y" objective needs a stated reason tied to the region's local conflict ([12-Regions.md](12-Regions.md)).

## Consistency Checks
* Cross-reference [39-NPCs.md](39-NPCs.md) before introducing a new named character.
* Cross-reference [30-Lore.md](30-Lore.md) through [36-History.md](36-History.md) for any historical or mythological claim made in quest text.
* Main story quests must align with the act structure in [37-Main-Story.md](37-Main-Story.md); side quests should never contradict it.
