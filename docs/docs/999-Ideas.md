# 999 — Ideas

A sandbox for brainstorming. Unlike [05-Future-Plans.md](05-Future-Plans.md), nothing here is committed to — this is where raw, unscoped ideas go before they're refined enough to graduate.

## Gameplay Ideas
* A "photo mode" style camera tool for capturing screenshots at landmarks ([15-Landmarks.md](15-Landmarks.md)), with a light social-sharing hook.
* Player-run market stalls in cities as a cosmetic/vanity alternative to the Auction House ([103-Auction-House.md](103-Auction-House.md)).
* A limited player-housing "open visiting" mode, letting friends browse a housing plot without full guild-neighborhood infrastructure ([90-Housing.md](90-Housing.md)).
* Difficulty-scaling "Mythic+"-style dungeon keys for repeatable, ever-scaling endgame dungeon content beyond the three fixed difficulty tiers in [16-Dungeons.md](16-Dungeons.md).

## Lore/World Ideas
* A hidden sixth continent, never fully explained, hinted at only through Cartographer's League ([33-Factions.md](33-Factions.md)) rumors.
* A player-facing "Codex" UI collecting all discovered lore fragments across landmarks and quests into one browsable in-game document.
* Seasonal cosmetic recolors tied to which god is being celebrated that season ([87-Seasons.md](87-Seasons.md), [32-Gods.md](32-Gods.md)).

## Technical Ideas
* A companion mobile app for Auction House browsing and guild chat, extending the external API idea in [125-API.md](125-API.md).
* A built-in in-game screenshot-to-website gallery pipeline for the community site.

## Process
Ideas here should be reviewed periodically; promising ones move to [05-Future-Plans.md](05-Future-Plans.md) once the team commits to them in principle.
