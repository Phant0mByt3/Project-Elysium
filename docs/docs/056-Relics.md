# 56 — Relics

## Overview
Relics are the rarest item tier in Elysium — genuine Age of Concord artifacts, predating the Sundering itself, distinct from Legendary items ([055-Legendary-Items.md](055-Legendary-Items.md)) in that they are tied to the Elysian Circle directly rather than to mortal history.

## Design Rules
* Relics should be exceedingly rare — a handful per raid tier at most, not a per-boss drop pool.
* Each relic's flavor text and acquisition should tie directly to one of the seven gods ([032-Gods.md](032-Gods.md)) or, for corrupted relics, to Kaelgorath.
* Relics may have evolving effects — growing stronger as the player completes related content, rather than being static on acquisition (design pattern to be finalized during Phase 2, [003-Roadmap.md](003-Roadmap.md)).

## Launch Relic (example)

**Shard of the Sunspire** — a fragment of the original, pre-Sundering Sunspire Cathedral, said to still hold a whisper of Solthar's light. Drops from the final boss of "The Sunken Concord" raid on Mythic difficulty only; its passive grows stronger as the player's Concord reputation ([077-Factions-Reputation.md](077-Factions-Reputation.md)) increases.

## Narrative Role
Relics as a category are expected to play a central role in the Nightreach expansion's story ([152-Expansion-03.md](152-Expansion-03.md)), where Kaelgorath's corrupted counterparts to these artifacts will be introduced.
