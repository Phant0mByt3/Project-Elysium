# 84 — PvP

## Overview
Player-vs-player combat exists in two primary forms: open-world contested-territory PvP ([0806-Territory-Control.md](0806-Territory-Control.md)) and structured Arena combat ([0805-Arenas.md](0805-Arenas.md)).

## Open World PvP
Enabled by default in designated contested zones (initially most of Vethmoor's frontier regions, see [0102-Regions.md](../0100-World/0102-Regions.md)), opt-in elsewhere via a PvP flag toggle. Aurelia's core regions are PvP-safe by default to keep the early leveling experience approachable.

## Faction Basis
All PvP is faction-based — Dawnbound Concord vs. Duskward Pact ([0203-Factions.md](../0200-Lore/0203-Factions.md)). There is no free-for-all or same-faction PvP mode at launch.

## Balance Considerations
* PvP damage/healing may apply separate modifiers from PvE where needed to keep burst damage and crowd control ([0306-Status-Effects.md](../0300-Characters/0306-Status-Effects.md)) fair in player-vs-player contexts, reviewed under [0309-Balance.md](../0300-Characters/0309-Balance.md).
* Gear itemization includes a PvP-specific resilience-style stat consideration to prevent pure PvE raid gear from completely dominating PvP, to be finalized during Phase 2 balance work.

## World Bosses & Contested Content
Faction tagging conflicts at shared world content (world bosses, [0108-World-Bosses.md](../0100-World/0108-World-Bosses.md)) are governed by the same contested-zone rules as open-world PvP.
