# 0806 — Territory Control

**Status:** Living Document

## 1. Overview

Territory Control is Elysium's large-scale, objective-based PvP system where organized groups contest control of specific world zones, primarily along Vethmoor's Dawnbound/Duskward border, for economic and reputation bonuses.

## 2. Structure

Contested zones feature capturable objectives (outposts, resource nodes, strategic points) that grant the controlling faction bonus gathering yield, vendor discounts, or reputation gains in the surrounding region for as long as they hold control.

## 3. Participation

Territory Control is open-world and does not require a queue — any player flagged for PvP in a contested zone can contribute to capturing or defending an objective, giving it a more organic, large-scale feel than instanced Arena content.

## 4. Faction Balance

Server-side population and win-rate monitoring inform periodic balance adjustments (buff to the underdog faction's capture speed, bonus rewards) to prevent one faction from permanently dominating a given contested zone, preserving the tug-of-war fantasy over the long term.

## 5. Rewards

Sustained participation in Territory Control grants a dedicated reputation and currency track, plus faction-flavored cosmetic rewards distinct from Arena and dungeon rewards.

## 6. Narrative Integration

Territory Control outcomes have light narrative acknowledgment — regional NPC dialogue and vendor availability may shift slightly based on current territory state, reinforcing that the faction conflict established in [0203-Factions.md](../0200-Lore/0203-Factions.md) is an active, ongoing part of the world rather than purely historical.

## 7. Future Expansion

A full guild-level Territory Control system with persistent guild ownership of contested zones is a confirmed future direction — see [0005-Future-Plans.md](../0000-Project/0005-Future-Plans.md).
