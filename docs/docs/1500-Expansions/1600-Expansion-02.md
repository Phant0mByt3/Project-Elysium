# 1600 — Expansion 02

## Overview
The second planned expansion, introducing the fourth continent, the **Kharzul Wastes** — a scorched desert continent dominated by beastkin nomad clans and buried pre-Sundering ruins ([0101-Continents.md](../0100-World/0101-Continents.md)).

## New Content
* **Continent** — Kharzul Wastes, level range 65–80.
* **Race Focus** — Beastkin ([0204-Races.md](../0200-Lore/0204-Races.md)) become a first-class cultural presence with their own capital city and starting experience for new Beastkin characters.
* **New Systems** — a desert-specific survival-flavored mechanic (heat/exposure management while exploring, without becoming a full survival-game system, staying consistent with [0001-Vision.md](../0000-Project/0001-Vision.md)'s MMORPG-first philosophy).
* **New Dungeons & Raid** — themed around buried Age of Concord excavation sites, tying into the ongoing "ancient ruins rediscovery" thread established in [0206-History.md](../0200-Lore/0206-History.md).

## Narrative Hook
Kharzul's ruins are revealed to hold some of the only surviving records of Kaelgorath's original fall from the Circle, foreshadowing the Nightreach expansion ([1700-Expansion-03.md](1700-Expansion-03.md)).

## Status
Planned; not yet scheduled on the active roadmap. See [0005-Future-Plans.md](../0000-Project/0005-Future-Plans.md).
