# 1900 — Expansion 05

## Overview
Placeholder for the fifth major expansion. As with [1800-Expansion-04.md](1800-Expansion-04.md), scope is intentionally undefined this early in development.

## Purpose of This Placeholder
Reserving a numbered slot in the documentation structure ([Doc-Structure.md](../Doc-Structure.md)) signals the team's intent to support Elysium as a multi-year live service (per [0001-Vision.md](../0000-Project/0001-Vision.md) and [0003-Roadmap.md](../0000-Project/0003-Roadmap.md)) without prematurely committing to specific content this far in advance.

## Status
Unscoped. Ideas that could eventually inform this expansion should be captured in [9999-Ideas.md](../9000-Future/9999-Ideas.md) or, once more concrete, [0005-Future-Plans.md](../0000-Project/0005-Future-Plans.md).

## Placeholder Philosophy

Reserving this slot is a deliberate signal, not an empty formality — it commits the documentation structure itself to supporting a multi-year content roadmap, so that when the team is ready to scope Expansion 05, there's already a defined place for it in the numbered structure ([Doc-Structure.md](../Doc-Structure.md)) rather than needing to renumber or restructure the GDD to accommodate it.

## What Would Trigger Scoping

Expansion 05 planning would realistically begin once Expansion 04 ([1800-Expansion-04.md](1800-Expansion-04.md)) has shipped and reached a stable live-service state, following the same capacity-based scheduling principle described in [1501-Expansion-Planning.md](1501-Expansion-Planning.md), Section 4.

## Long-Term Narrative Considerations

By this point in Elysium's live service life, the original Sundering/Kaelgorath narrative arc (resolved in Expansion 03) will have been settled for two full expansion cycles. Expansion 05 planning should consider whether to open an entirely new long-term narrative arc, drawing on the long-term story framework in [9004-Long-Term-Story.md](../9000-Future/9004-Long-Term-Story.md), rather than continuing to reference a resolved conflict.

## Status

Unscoped. Ideas that could eventually inform this expansion should be captured in [9999-Ideas.md](../9000-Future/9999-Ideas.md) or, once more concrete, [0005-Future-Plans.md](../0000-Project/0005-Future-Plans.md).
