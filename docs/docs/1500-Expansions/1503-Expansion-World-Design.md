## 1. Overview

Expansion World Design covers how new continents and major zones are scoped: size, biomes, hubs, traversal, and integration with existing travel and instance systems.

---

## 2. Rules

- Handcrafted, purposeful spaces only  
- Level bands and travel times match pacing goals  
- Distinct visual and cultural identity  
- Technical instance plan agreed early  


## 3. Continent Sizing Guidelines

New continents follow the same regional structure and pacing established at launch (see [0102-Regions.md](../0100-World/0102-Regions.md)) — roughly 4–5 regions per continent, each scoped to a 3–6 hour quest content window at its intended level range.

## 4. Instance and Travel Integration

Every new continent's dungeon and raid entrances integrate with the existing instance architecture ([1209-Instance-System.md](../1200-Technical/1209-Instance-System.md)), and its connection to the existing world map is planned early in the process (sea route, portal network extension, or story-gated access) rather than left as a late-stage technical afterthought.

## 5. Visual and Cultural Distinctiveness Requirement

Per [1300-Art-Style.md](../1300-Art/1300-Art-Style.md), no new continent may reuse an existing continent's material and color language wholesale — each must be reviewed by the Art Director for genuine visual distinctiveness before world-building begins in earnest.
