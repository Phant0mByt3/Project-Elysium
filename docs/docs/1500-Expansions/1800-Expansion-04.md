## Why This Stays Unscoped For Now

Per the capacity-based scheduling principle in [1501-Expansion-Planning.md](1501-Expansion-Planning.md), Section 4, committing to Expansion 04's specifics before Expansion 03 is far enough along would risk locking in decisions before the team knows what Expansion 03's reception and remaining threads actually call for. Nightreach's narrative choice point ([1700-Expansion-03.md](1700-Expansion-03.md)) in particular may meaningfully shape which of the candidate directions below makes the most sense.

## Evaluation Criteria (Once Scoping Begins)

When this document is filled in, it should be evaluated against the same framework as every other expansion:

* Does it reinforce at least one Core Pillar ([0002-Core-Pillars.md](../0000-Project/0002-Core-Pillars.md))?
* Does it have a clear narrative hook from prior content, per [1502-Expansion-Story-Structure.md](1502-Expansion-Story-Structure.md)?
* Is it additive to the existing world rather than requiring existing content to be discarded?

## Candidate Direction Detail

* **Naval Exploration** — would most naturally follow Sylvaneth and Kharzul Wastes' coastal geography, giving players a new way to interact with previously unreachable ocean regions between existing continents rather than requiring an entirely new landmass.
* **Guild Housing Neighborhoods** — the lowest-risk candidate, extending existing systems ([0900-Housing.md](../0900-Player-Systems/0900-Housing.md), [0800-Guilds.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0800-Guilds.md)) rather than requiring new world-building, making it attractive if the team wants a lighter-scope expansion after Nightreach's heavy raid-focused cycle.
* **Third Faction** — the highest-risk candidate given its potential ripple effects across PvP balance ([0804-PvP.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0804-PvP.md)) and territory control ([0806-Territory-Control.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0806-Territory-Control.md)); would require its own dedicated balance validation period before release.
