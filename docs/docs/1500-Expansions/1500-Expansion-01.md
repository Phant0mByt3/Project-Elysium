# 1500 — Expansion 01

## Overview
The first planned post-launch expansion, introducing the third continent, **Sylvaneth** — an ancient elven forest continent built around a single, colossal world-tree canopy ([0101-Continents.md](../0100-World/0101-Continents.md)).

## New Content
* **Continent** — Sylvaneth, level range 50–65 (raising the level cap, see [0305-Leveling.md](../0300-Characters/0305-Leveling.md)).
* **Race Update** — introduces Wood Elves as a distinct cultural branch of the existing Elf race lore ([0204-Races.md](../0200-Lore/0204-Races.md)), native to Sylvaneth.
* **New Dungeons & Raid** — a full new dungeon set and raid tier themed around the world-tree's canopy and roots.
* **New Profession Materials** — unique Sylvaneth timber and herbs extending Woodcutting and Herbalism ([0602-Woodcutting.md](../0600-Professions/0602-Woodcutting.md), [0604-Herbalism.md](../0600-Professions/0604-Herbalism.md)).
* **Story** — follows directly from the main story's Act III resolution ([0207-Main-Story.md](../0200-Lore/0207-Main-Story.md)), with Sylvaneth's elves revealed to hold knowledge about Aeloria's silence since the Sundering ([0202-Gods.md](../0200-Lore/0202-Gods.md)).

## Faction Impact
Sylvaneth introduces new faction outposts for both the Dawnbound Concord and Duskward Pact as they compete to form alliances with Sylvaneth's insular elven communities, extending [0203-Factions.md](../0200-Lore/0203-Factions.md) and [0806-Territory-Control.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0806-Territory-Control.md) into a new continent.

## Status
Planned; not yet scheduled on the active roadmap. See [0005-Future-Plans.md](../0000-Project/0005-Future-Plans.md) for graduation criteria.

* **Existing Areas** — Aurelia and Vethmoor remain fully accessible and unchanged; no existing content is removed or invalidated, per the "expand, don't discard" rule in [1501-Expansion-Planning.md](1501-Expansion-Planning.md).
* **New Travel Links** — a new sea route from Solmere's Docks and a Vethmoor coastal port both open direct passage to Sylvaneth, integrated into [0110-Travel.md](../0100-World/0110-Travel.md) and [0111-Fast-Travel.md](../0100-World/0111-Fast-Travel.md).
* **Phased Content** — none; Sylvaneth is purely additive to the existing world map.

## Design Pillars for This Expansion

Following the framework in [1504-Expansion-Feature-Planning.md](1504-Expansion-Feature-Planning.md), Expansion 01 is built around: introducing genuine verticality to world traversal (canopy paths, tree-lift networks), deepening the Wood Elf cultural identity within the broader Elf race lore, and resolving the Aeloria silence plot thread seeded at launch.

## Success Criteria

The expansion is considered successful if it: raises the level cap smoothly without leveling-curve gaps (see [0305-Leveling.md](../0300-Characters/0305-Leveling.md)), delivers a raid tier that at least matches launch raid engagement metrics, and meaningfully advances the Aeloria narrative thread without fully resolving the larger Kaelgorath arc reserved for Expansion 03.
