## 1. Overview

Expansion Release Strategy covers PTR/beta plans, staggered unlocks, communication, and post-launch support windows for major content drops.

---

## 2. Rules

- Test environments before live  
- Clear player communication of dates and expectations  
- Capacity and support staffing scaled for launch week  
- Hotfix readiness for day-one issues  


## 3. Public Test Realm (PTR)

Each expansion goes through a public test period on a dedicated test server before live release, allowing broad community feedback and stress-testing beyond what internal QA ([1406-Testing.md](../1400-Development/1406-Testing.md)) alone can surface, particularly for balance and encounter design.

## 4. Staggered Feature Unlocks

Rather than unlocking every new system simultaneously, some expansion content (particularly raid tiers) may unlock on a staggered schedule post-launch to give the community time to absorb new zones and story content before the hardest endgame content becomes available, mirroring successful patterns from established live-service MMORPGs.

## 5. Communication Timeline

Release dates, new feature previews, and any significant system changes are communicated on a consistent cadence in the lead-up to release through the channels described in [2004-Community-Management.md](../2000-Operations/2004-Community-Management.md), avoiding both under-communication (surprising the community) and over-promising features that may still be cut per Section 5 of [1504-Expansion-Feature-Planning.md](1504-Expansion-Feature-Planning.md).
