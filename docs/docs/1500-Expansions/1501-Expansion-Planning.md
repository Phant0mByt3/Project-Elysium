# 1501 — Expansion Planning

**Project:** Elysium MMORPG  
**Category:** Expansions  
**Status:** Design Complete — Implementation Pending  
**Related:** [0003-Roadmap.md](../0000-Project/0003-Roadmap.md) · [0005-Future-Plans.md](../0000-Project/0005-Future-Plans.md) · [1500-Expansion-01.md](1500-Expansion-01.md)

---

## 1. Overview

Expansion Planning is the process that turns a future-direction idea into a scoped, scheduled expansion with clear pillars, content lists, and success criteria.

---

## 2. Steps

1. Graduate idea from Future Plans  
2. Define fantasy, continent/system focus, and level band  
3. Draft story, world, and feature outlines  
4. Estimate art, design, and engineering cost  
5. Place on roadmap when capacity exists  

---

## 3. Rules

Expansions must reinforce the vision and pillars. They expand the world; they do not discard prior content.


---

## 4. Capacity-Based Scheduling

An expansion is only placed on the active roadmap ([0003-Roadmap.md](../0000-Project/0003-Roadmap.md)) once the team has genuine development capacity for it — typically once the current live content season's obligations are stable and the prior expansion has been live long enough to be in steady-state maintenance rather than active firefighting.

## 5. Cross-Team Estimation

Cost estimation in Step 4 involves the Lead Game Designer (systems/features), World Director (zone scope), Art Director (visual production cost), and Technical Lead (engineering dependencies) providing independent estimates that are then reconciled, avoiding the common pitfall of a single discipline's optimistic estimate driving the whole schedule.

## 6. Idea Graduation Review

The Step 1 graduation from [0005-Future-Plans.md](../0000-Project/0005-Future-Plans.md) is a formal review, not an informal decision — the Creative Director and relevant discipline Leads jointly confirm an idea is ready to move from "confirmed direction" to "actively being planned" before Step 2 begins.
