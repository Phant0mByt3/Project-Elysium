## 1. Overview

Expansion Story Structure defines how each expansion’s campaign is organised: acts, hooks from prior content, and setup for the next era.

---

## 2. Principles

- Continuity with launch lore and unresolved threads  
- Clear beginning, middle, and climax  
- Optional side stories that enrich without gating  
- Player choice where it matters, without exploding production scope  


## 3. Standard Structure

Following the pattern established by the Main Quest ([0708-Main-Quest.md](../0700-Quests/0708-Main-Quest.md)), each expansion's story is organized into 3–5 acts:

* **Act 1 — Arrival:** introduces the new continent/system and its central conflict.
* **Act 2 — Escalation:** deepens the conflict, introduces key new NPCs and factions.
* **Act 3 — Climax:** resolves the expansion's central story, typically culminating in its signature raid encounter.

## 4. Hooks and Payoffs

Every expansion must open with at least one hook drawn from unresolved threads in prior content ([0208-Side-Stories.md](../0200-Lore/0208-Side-Stories.md), prior expansion documents) and close with at least one new hook seeding the next expansion, keeping the long-term story described in [9004-Long-Term-Story.md](../9000-Future/9004-Long-Term-Story.md) feeling like one continuous narrative rather than disconnected content drops.

## 5. Side Story Integration

Optional side stories introduced within an expansion follow the same design rules as launch side quests ([0709-Side-Quests.md](../0700-Quests/0709-Side-Quests.md)) — they enrich the expansion's world without gating access to its critical path content.
