## 1. Overview

Expansion Story Structure defines how each expansion’s campaign is organised: acts, hooks from prior content, and setup for the next era.

---

## 2. Principles

- Continuity with launch lore and unresolved threads  
- Clear beginning, middle, and climax  
- Optional side stories that enrich without gating  
- Player choice where it matters, without exploding production scope
