# 1001 — Currency

## Aurum
The primary in-game currency, earned from quests, vendoring loot, dungeon/raid completion, and professions. Used for repairs, fast travel, consumables, crafting materials, and most Auction House transactions ([1003-Auction-House.md](1003-Auction-House.md)).

## Sundered Shards
A rarer secondary currency, obtained from Heroic/Mythic dungeon and raid completion, world bosses, and select achievements. Used to purchase high-end cosmetics ([0903-Cosmetics.md](../0900-Player-Systems/0903-Cosmetics.md)), catch-up gear vendors, and rare recipes — a controlled, non-tradeable currency to keep endgame reward pacing consistent regardless of Auction House market conditions.

## Design Rules
* Aurum should never be purchasable directly with real money, to avoid pay-to-win perception and keep the player-driven economy ([1000-Economy.md](1000-Economy.md)) meaningful.
* Sundered Shards are account-bound and non-tradeable, distinguishing "farmed" endgame currency from the open Aurum market.
* Currency caps and sink tuning are reviewed as part of the ongoing economy health process described in [1000-Economy.md](1000-Economy.md).


## Currency Acquisition Pacing

Aurum income is tuned to roughly track a player's leveling pace and repair/consumable expenses, ensuring most players stay comfortably solvent without either currency scarcity blocking basic play or currency surplus trivializing the economy's sinks.

## Future Currency Types

Additional specialized currencies (PvP honor, seasonal event tokens — see [0807-Seasons.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0807-Seasons.md)) exist alongside Aurum and Sundered Shards, each scoped tightly to their specific reward system rather than being freely convertible, keeping the overall currency landscape legible rather than fragmented into confusing parallel economies.
