# 55 — Legendary Items

## Overview
Legendary items are unique, named pieces of equipment with a distinct passive or active effect beyond raw stats, tied narratively to the world's lore ([30-Lore.md](30-Lore.md)) rather than being generic drops.

## Design Rules
* Every legendary must have a name, a short lore blurb connecting it to a specific historical figure or event ([36-History.md](36-History.md)), and a mechanically unique effect.
* Acquisition should involve more than a single drop roll — typically a quest chain, a rare drop plus a crafting/completion step, or a multi-boss collection requirement.
* Legendaries should be powerful but not mandatory — content should be balanced around Epic-tier gear as the baseline ([53-Loot.md](53-Loot.md)).

## Launch Legendaries (examples)

**Aldric's Last Stand** *(Two-Handed Sword)* — said to have belonged to a Concord general who held the line during the Sundering itself; drops from the final boss of "The Sunken Concord" raid ([17-Raids.md](17-Raids.md)), requiring a follow-up quest to fully awaken its passive effect.

**The Warden's Seed** *(Druid-only Trinket)* — grown from a cutting believed to be one of the last living pieces of Aeloria's original grove; acquired through a long exploration-and-collection questline across Aurelia's regions, rewarding thorough explorers rather than pure raiders.
