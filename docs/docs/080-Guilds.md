# 80 — Guilds

## Overview
Guilds are persistent player organizations, the primary social structure for coordinated group content ([82-Raiding.md](82-Raiding.md)) and long-term community building — directly supporting Pillar 4 in [02-Core-Pillars.md](02-Core-Pillars.md).

## Core Features
* **Guild Creation** — requires a minimum founding member count and an Aurum cost, purchasable from any city's guild registrar.
* **Guild Ranks** — customizable permission tiers (invite, kick, bank access, officer notes).
* **Guild Bank** — shared storage and Aurum pool with per-rank withdrawal limits.
* **Guild Chat & Roster** — persistent communication and online-status tracking.
* **Guild Perks** — passive bonuses (Aurum find, reputation gain, reduced fast-travel cost) that scale with guild level, earned through collective member activity.

## Guild Housing
Guilds can eventually claim a shared housing plot — see [90-Housing.md](90-Housing.md) for the individual housing system this extends from, and [05-Future-Plans.md](05-Future-Plans.md) for the planned guild neighborhood expansion.

## Design Rules
Guild perks should encourage participation across all guild members, not just top raiders — e.g. perk progress should account for dungeon runs, professions, and PvP, not raid attendance alone.

## Relationship to Factions
Guilds are single-faction only — a guild's members must all share the same faction allegiance ([33-Factions.md](33-Factions.md)), reinforcing the faction-vs-faction structure of PvP and territory content.
