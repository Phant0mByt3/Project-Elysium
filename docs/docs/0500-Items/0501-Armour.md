## 1. Overview

Armor is the primary determinant of a character's survivability and, through secondary stats, contributes meaningfully to damage and healing output as well.

## 2. Armor Categories

| Category | Base Armor | Stat Focus | Primary Classes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cloth | Lowest | Intellect | Arcanist |
| Leather | Low-moderate | Agility | Shade, Wayfarer (secondary) |
| Mail | Moderate | Agility / balanced | Wayfarer, Warden (secondary) |
| Plate | Highest | Strength / Stamina | Vanguard, Oathkeeper |

Warden splits across Leather and a hybrid nature-focused stat profile depending on specialization ([0301-Specializations.md](../0300-Characters/0301-Specializations.md)).

## 3. Slots

Head, Shoulders, Chest, Hands, Waist, Legs, Feet, Cloak, plus two Accessory slots covered in [0502-Accessories.md](0502-Accessories.md).

## 4. Set Bonuses

Armor from the same dungeon or raid tier shares a themed visual set. Equipping multiple pieces from the same set grants a bonus effect (2-piece and 4-piece thresholds), reinforcing the value of running content repeatedly rather than mixing loot at random — see [0512-Item-Sets.md](0512-Item-Sets.md) and [0504-Loot-Tables.md](0504-Loot-Tables.md) for how sets are distributed.

## 5. Progression

Armor item level scales through: quest rewards (early leveling) → dungeon drops (mid-level) → crafted gear ([0508-Crafting.md](0508-Crafting.md), tailoring/blacksmithing/leatherworking) → raid drops (endgame). This mirrors the leveling curve in [0305-Leveling.md](../0300-Characters/0305-Leveling.md).

## 6. Visual Standards

Each armor class (Cloth/Leather/Mail/Plate) should be silhouette-distinct even at a distance, so a player's role is readable in group content without opening a character sheet — see [1300-Art-Style.md](../1300-Art/1300-Art-Style.md). Faction-aligned armor sets additionally carry Dawnbound Concord or Duskward Pact visual motifs where appropriate.

## 7. Durability

Armor is subject to the durability system described in [0515-Item-Durability.md](0515-Item-Durability.md), requiring periodic repair through vendors or the Blacksmithing/Leatherworking professions.

## 8. Binding

High-value armor pieces (Rare and above) generally bind on pickup or on equip — see [0514-Item-Binding.md](0514-Item-Binding.md) for the full binding rules and their economic implications.
