# 0506 — Relics

**Category:** Items
**Status:** Living Document

## 1. Overview

Relics are the rarest item tier in Elysium — genuine Age of Concord artifacts, predating the Sundering itself, distinct from Legendary items ([0505-Legendary-Items.md](0505-Legendary-Items.md)) in that they are tied to the Elysian Circle directly rather than to mortal history.

## 2. Design Rules

* Relics should be exceedingly rare — a handful per raid tier at most, not a per-boss drop pool.
* Each relic's flavor text and acquisition should tie directly to one of the seven gods ([0202-Gods.md](../0200-Lore/0202-Gods.md)) or, for corrupted relics, to Kaelgorath.
* Relics may have evolving effects — growing stronger as the player completes related content, rather than being static on acquisition (design pattern to be finalized during Phase 2, [0003-Roadmap.md](../0000-Project/0003-Roadmap.md)).

## 3. Launch Relic (example)

**Shard of the Sunspire** — a fragment of the original, pre-Sundering Sunspire Cathedral, said to still hold a whisper of Solthar's light. Drops from the final boss of "The Sunken Concord" raid on Mythic difficulty only; its passive grows stronger as the player's Concord reputation ([0707-Factions-Reputation.md](../0700-Quests/0707-Factions-Reputation.md)) increases.

## 4. Relic Evolution Concept

Rather than being static, relics are envisioned to unlock additional passive tiers as the player completes specific milestones — raid clears, reputation thresholds, or lore-collection achievements. This turns a relic from a single power spike into a long-term account-level progression goal, distinguishing it clearly from a Legendary item's more contained acquisition arc.

## 5. Narrative Role

Relics as a category are expected to play a central role in the Nightreach expansion's story ([1700-Expansion-03.md](../1500-Expansions/1700-Expansion-03.md)), where Kaelgorath's corrupted counterparts to these artifacts will be introduced, giving the endgame a direct, tangible connection back to the Sundering that started the entire setting.

## 6. Ownership and Review

Because relics sit at the absolute top of the power and rarity curve, every proposed relic effect requires sign-off from both the Lead Game Designer and the Narrative Lead ([0007-Team-Structure.md](../0000-Project/0007-Team-Structure.md)) before entering implementation, ensuring mechanical and lore consistency stay tightly aligned at this tier.
