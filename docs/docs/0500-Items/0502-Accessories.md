# 0502 — Accessories

**Category:** Items
**Status:** Living Document
**Related:** [0501-Armour.md](0501-Armour.md) · [0301-Specializations.md](../0300-Characters/0301-Specializations.md)

---

## 1. Overview

Accessories are the itemization slots least tied to armor class restrictions, giving them the most freedom to introduce build-defining effects independent of class or role.

## 2. Slot Types

* **Rings** (x2) — pure stat items, no armor class restriction.
* **Necklace** — pure stat item, occasionally carries a minor proc effect.
* **Trinkets** (x2) — the primary source of unique on-use or passive proc effects; the most build-defining accessory slot.
* **Cloak** — technically an armor slot but functions closer to an accessory in itemization variety (see [0501-Armour.md](0501-Armour.md)).

## 3. Design Philosophy

Trinkets in particular should feel like discovering a build-enabling toy, not just a stat stick — a trinket that synergizes with a specific specialization's mechanic (see [0301-Specializations.md](../0300-Characters/0301-Specializations.md)) is preferred over a generic stat trinket whenever possible.

## 4. Trinket Categories

* **On-Use** — an activated effect on a cooldown, giving the player an additional tactical decision in combat.
* **Passive Proc** — triggers automatically on a combat event (critical hit, taking damage below a threshold, healing cast).
* **Conditional Passive** — always active but scales its value based on a build or situational condition (e.g. bonus effect while above a health threshold).

## 5. Acquisition

Accessories drop from quests, dungeons, and raids like other gear ([0504-Loot-Tables.md](0504-Loot-Tables.md)), with select craftable rings/necklaces available through Jewelcrafting ([0608-Jewelcrafting.md](../0600-Professions/0608-Jewelcrafting.md)).

## 6. Balance Considerations

Trinket proc effects are reviewed under the same cadence as class balance ([0309-Balance.md](../0300-Characters/0309-Balance.md)) since a strong enough trinket can distort specialization balance on its own. New trinket effects are cross-checked against existing talent tree mechanics ([0303-Talent-Trees.md](../0300-Characters/0303-Talent-Trees.md)) to avoid redundant or degenerate stacking.

## 7. Rarity and Power Curve

Like other equipment, accessories follow the standard rarity progression in [0510-Item-Rarity.md](0510-Item-Rarity.md), with Legendary and Relic-tier trinkets ([0505-Legendary-Items.md](0505-Legendary-Items.md), [0506-Relics.md](0506-Relics.md)) reserved for the most build-defining, narratively significant effects in the game.
