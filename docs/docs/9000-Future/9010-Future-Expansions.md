### 3.1 A Fully Player-Governed City

**Status:** *Under Consideration*

**Concept:** A speculative future city where meaningful policy/economy decisions are influenced by player voting or guild influence, beyond the cosmetic-only territory control described in [0806-Territory-Control.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0806-Territory-Control.md).

**Notes:** High complexity and high risk of engagement imbalance (a small dedicated group dominating decisions for the whole server); would need extensive prototyping before being seriously scoped into any numbered expansion.

### 3.2 A Non-Combat "Explorer's Path" Progression Track

**Status:** *Under Consideration*

**Concept:** A dedicated long-term progression track built entirely around exploration and discovery rather than combat power, expanding the achievement/collection systems in [0907-Collections.md](../0900-Player-Systems/0907-Collections.md) into a first-class parallel progression path.

**Notes:** Strong thematic fit with Pillar 1 (Exploration is Always Rewarding) in [0002-Core-Pillars.md](../0000-Project/0002-Core-Pillars.md); a promising lower-risk expansion-adjacent feature that could ship alongside any future expansion rather than requiring its own dedicated one.

### 3.3 Cross-Game Legacy Rewards

**Status:** *Under Consideration*

**Concept:** If Anthropic-unrelated future Elysium-branded projects were ever built, speculative cross-title cosmetic rewards for players active across both.

**Notes:** Extremely speculative and entirely dependent on business decisions well outside the current single-game scope; recorded here only as a long-range idea, not an active consideration.
