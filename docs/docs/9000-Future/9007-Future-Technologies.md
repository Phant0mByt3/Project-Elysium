### 3.1 Cloud-Streamed Play

**Status:** *Under Consideration*

**Concept:** Support for cloud-streaming Elysium to lower-spec devices, extending reach beyond the minimum hardware targets in [1105-Shaders.md](../1100-Client/1105-Shaders.md) and [1208-Performance.md](../1200-Technical/1208-Performance.md).

**Notes:** Dependent on third-party cloud gaming platform partnerships and infrastructure cost, not something the current team scope can evaluate seriously before launch.

### 3.2 Voice-to-Text Accessibility Integration

**Status:** *Under Consideration*

**Concept:** Real-time voice-to-text transcription for in-client voice communication ([0812-Voice-Communication.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0812-Voice-Communication.md)), improving accessibility for deaf and hard-of-hearing players in group content.

**Notes:** A strong accessibility win aligned with [1106-Accessibility.md](../1100-Client/1106-Accessibility.md); technical feasibility and cost need evaluation once voice communication infrastructure is fully built.

### 3.3 Procedural Ambient Dialogue Variation

**Status:** *Under Consideration*

**Concept:** Lightweight generative variation for ambient/idle NPC barks (not quest-critical dialogue), reducing repetition fatigue in high-traffic city hubs without requiring fully bespoke writing for every variation.

**Notes:** Would need strict guardrails to preserve the hand-authored voice consistency required by [1404-NPC-Writing-Guide.md](../1400-Development/1404-NPC-Writing-Guide.md) — any such system would only touch flavor barks, never quest or story-critical dialogue.
