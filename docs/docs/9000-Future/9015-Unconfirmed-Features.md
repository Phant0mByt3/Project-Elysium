### 3.1 Player-Officiated In-Game Weddings/Ceremonies

**Status:** *Unconfirmed*

**Concept:** A lightweight social ceremony system letting players hold formal in-game events (guild inductions, roleplay ceremonies) with scripted animations and a shareable commemorative item.

**Notes:** Pure social/roleplay feature with no gameplay impact, aligned with the social pillars in [0002-Core-Pillars.md](../0000-Project/0002-Core-Pillars.md); low priority relative to core systems but a pleasant potential addition once social features ([0810-Social-Features.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0810-Social-Features.md)) are mature.

### 3.2 Cross-Faction Marriage/Alliance Cosmetic Track

**Status:** *Unconfirmed*

**Concept:** A cosmetic-only system marking a persistent in-game "bond" between two players regardless of faction, unlocking shared cosmetic rewards.

**Notes:** Interesting long-tail social retention idea; would need careful narrative framing to avoid undermining the Dawnbound/Duskward conflict's stakes established in [0203-Factions.md](../0200-Lore/0203-Factions.md).

### 3.3 In-Game Spectator Broadcasting Tools

**Status:** *Unconfirmed*

**Concept:** Built-in tools for community members to broadcast/spectate raid and Arena content directly through the client rather than relying entirely on third-party streaming tools.

**Notes:** Related to the spectator mode already noted as a future consideration in [0805-Arenas.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0805-Arenas.md); would benefit from being scoped together with that feature rather than as a separate, disconnected system.
