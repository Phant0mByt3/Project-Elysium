### 3.1 Guild vs. Guild Scheduled Scrimmages

**Status:** *Community-Suggested, Under Review*

**Concept:** A formal system for scheduling guild-vs-guild Arena scrimmages ([0805-Arenas.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0805-Arenas.md)) outside of ranked matchmaking, for practice and inter-guild rivalry events.

**Notes:** Low technical complexity, strong community-requested feature; a good candidate for an early post-launch quality-of-life update per [2001-Updates.md](../2000-Operations/2001-Updates.md).

### 3.2 Transmog Appearance Preview Outside of Owned Items

**Status:** *Community-Suggested, Under Review*

**Concept:** Let players preview any discovered (not just owned) transmog appearance ([0513-Transmog-System.md](../0500-Items/0513-Transmog-System.md)) on their character before deciding whether to pursue it.

**Notes:** Strong quality-of-life suggestion with minimal design risk; likely to be adopted into the base transmog system rather than staying purely speculative.

### 3.3 In-Client LFG Voice Rooms

**Status:** *Community-Suggested, Under Review*

**Concept:** Temporary, automatically-created voice rooms for Dungeon Finder groups ([0803-Dungeon-Finder.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0803-Dungeon-Finder.md)), reducing the friction of coordinating external voice chat for pickup groups.

**Notes:** Builds directly on the voice communication infrastructure in [0812-Voice-Communication.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0812-Voice-Communication.md); technical feasibility needs evaluation against server infrastructure cost at scale.
