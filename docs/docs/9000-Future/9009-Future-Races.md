### 3.1 Constructs (Living Automata)

**Status:** *Under Consideration*

**Concept:** A playable race of sentient dwarven-forged automata, tying into Ironpeak Hold's forge-construct lore already established via the Ember Foundry dungeon ([0106-Dungeons.md](../0100-World/0106-Dungeons.md)).

**Notes:** Would require dedicated character art and animation work distinct from every existing humanoid race base rig ([1306-Models.md](../1300-Art/1306-Models.md)), making it a significant production investment better suited to a future expansion than a quick addition.

### 3.2 Fey-Touched (Sylvaneth-Native Race)

**Status:** *Under Consideration*

**Concept:** A playable race native to Sylvaneth, distinct from Wood Elves, representing fey creatures bound to the world-tree itself.

**Notes:** Natural fit for Expansion 01 ([1500-Expansion-01.md](../1500-Expansions/1500-Expansion-01.md)) if the team wants a second new race alongside Wood Elves; currently unscoped pending Sylvaneth's full lore development.

### 3.3 Playable Undead

**Status:** *Under Consideration*

**Concept:** A playable undead race, tonally distinct from the Revenant race already planned for Nightreach ([0204-Races.md](../0200-Lore/0204-Races.md)).

**Notes:** Risks narrative redundancy with the Revenant race's planned reveal in Expansion 03 ([1700-Expansion-03.md](../1500-Expansions/1700-Expansion-03.md)); would need a clearly distinct narrative hook to justify alongside Revenants rather than in place of them.
