# Future Threats

## Purpose

This document contains future enemies, disasters, conflicts, and dangers that will shape upcoming expansions.

Future threats should be planned early so they can be slowly introduced through the world.

---

## Threat Structure

Every threat should contain:

* Name
* Origin
* First Foreshadowing
* Current Status
* Goals
* Powers
* Followers
* Major Events
* Final Encounter

---

# Threat Categories

## World-Level Threats

Threats capable of affecting the entire world.

Examples:

* Ancient gods
* Cosmic entities
* Reality-changing forces

---

## Regional Threats

Threats focused on specific locations.

Examples:

* Enemy armies
* Corrupted kingdoms
* Powerful monsters

---

## Hidden Threats

Threats that remain unknown for years.

Examples:

* Secret organisations
* Forgotten creatures
* Unknown civilizations

---

# Design Rule

Future threats should feel like consequences of previous events.

The story should evolve naturally instead of resetting with every expansion.
