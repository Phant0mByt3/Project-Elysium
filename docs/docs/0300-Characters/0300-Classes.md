# 0300 — Classes

## Overview

Elysium launches with six major classes, each representing a distinct combat tradition within Elysian society. Their identities are shaped by the cultures, philosophies, and gods of Elysium rather than following conventional fantasy class archetypes.

Each class is designed around a clear combat identity while allowing meaningful build variation through specializations and talent choices.

The six launch classes are:

* Vanguard
* Arcanist
* Shade
* Warden
* Oathkeeper
* Wayfarer

Class specializations further define each class's role and playstyle. See [0301-Specializations.md](0301-Specializations.md).

# Class Identities

## Vanguard

The Vanguard represents the disciplined front line of Elysium.

Vanguards are trained to survive where others cannot. They master a wide variety of weapons and can adapt their fighting style to the battlefield.

Their traditions descend from warriors, soldiers, clan champions and forge-trained fighters.

**Primary roles:**

* Tank
* Damage

**Core fantasy:**

* Weapon mastery
* Heavy armour
* Frontline combat
* Battlefield control
* Physical endurance

**Associated influence:** Ignareth

---

## Arcanist

Arcanists study the forces of magic itself.

Unlike classes whose powers are derived from divine traditions, Arcanists rely primarily on their understanding of arcane forces. Their magic can manipulate elements, energy and the fundamental forces that shape Elysium.

The oldest Arcanist traditions descend from the scholars of the Age of Concord.

**Primary role:**

* Damage

**Core fantasy:**

* Arcane magic
* Elemental manipulation
* Spell combinations
* High magical damage
* Battlefield control

**Associated influence:** Independent arcane tradition

---

## Shade

The Shade is a master of secrecy, precision and deception.

Rather than overpowering an enemy, Shades manipulate positioning and timing to strike where an opponent is weakest.

Nyxara's traditions strongly influenced the development of Shade techniques, although not every Shade follows her faith.

**Primary role:**

* Damage

**Core fantasy:**

* Stealth
* Assassination
* Mobility
* Precision attacks
* Deception

**Associated influence:** Nyxara

---

## Warden

Wardens are protectors of the natural world.

Their traditions combine healing, primal magic and shapeshifting. Some Wardens fight directly through physical transformation, while others remain in their natural form and manipulate the battlefield through nature magic.

The tradition is strongly associated with Aeloria.

**Primary roles:**

* Healer
* Damage

**Core fantasy:**

* Nature magic
* Shapeshifting
* Restoration
* Primal forces
* Environmental control

**Associated influence:** Aeloria

---

## Oathkeeper

Oathkeepers are warriors bound by sacred vows.

Their strength comes from discipline, conviction and divine power. Rather than simply serving as soldiers of Solthar, Oathkeepers interpret their vows personally and may swear themselves to the protection of a person, community, cause or ideal.

Their traditions form the foundation of many Dawnbound military orders.

**Primary roles:**

* Tank
* Healer

**Core fantasy:**

* Sacred combat
* Protective magic
* Divine vows
* Defensive abilities
* Restoration

**Associated influence:** Solthar

---

## Wayfarer

Wayfarers are explorers, hunters and wilderness specialists.

They travel beyond settled territory, surviving in places where roads and civilization disappear. Their combat style focuses on ranged weapons, mobility, traps and knowledge of the environment.

The Wayfarer tradition is not restricted to any single culture. Humans, elves, dwarves, orcs and Beastkin have all developed their own interpretations of the path.

**Primary role:**

* Damage

**Core fantasy:**

* Ranged combat
* Exploration
* Survival
* Tracking
* Traps
* Wilderness mastery

**Associated influence:** Aeloria and independent wilderness traditions

---

# Class Philosophy

Elysium's classes should feel like established traditions within the world rather than generic RPG labels.

A character should be recognizable as a Vanguard, Arcanist, Shade, Warden, Oathkeeper or Wayfarer through their:

* Equipment
* Weapons
* Combat animations
* Abilities
* Clothing
* Visual effects
* NPC interactions
* Class-specific lore

The class name should communicate the character's identity without relying on conventional fantasy terminology.

---

# Combat Roles

Every class has a defined combat purpose.

### Tank

Designed to withstand enemy attacks and control enemy attention.

**Vanguard**

* Physical frontline tanking

**Oathkeeper**

* Defensive and protective tanking

### Healer

Designed to restore allies and support the group.

**Warden**

* Nature-based healing

**Oathkeeper**

* Sacred and defensive healing

### Damage

Designed to defeat enemies through direct or specialized offensive abilities.

**Vanguard**

* Melee weapon damage

**Arcanist**

* Arcane and elemental damage

**Shade**

* Precision and burst damage

**Warden**

* Nature and shapeshifting damage

**Wayfarer**

* Ranged and tactical damage

# Race and Class

Race does not restrict class selection.

Any playable race may follow any class tradition, regardless of their cultural or faction background.

This reflects Elysium's broader theme that identity is not predetermined by birth.

A dwarf can become a Shade.

An orc can become an Arcanist.

A Revenant can become an Oathkeeper.

A human can become a Warden.

The world may react differently to unusual combinations, but the player is never mechanically prevented from choosing them.

See [0204-Races.md](../0200-Lore/0204-Races.md).

---

# Class and Faction

Classes are independent of the two major player factions.

A Vanguard may serve the Dawnbound Concord or the Duskward Pact.

A Shade may work for either side.

An Oathkeeper may interpret their oath in ways that conflict with Solthar's traditional followers.

Class identity and faction identity should therefore remain separate systems.

---

# Design Goal

The six classes should feel like traditions that could only exist in Elysium.

They are not simply renamed versions of familiar fantasy classes.

The goal is for players to eventually hear a name such as:

**"Vanguard."**

**"Arcanist."**

**"Shade."**

**"Warden."**

**"Oathkeeper."**

**"Wayfarer."**

and immediately understand what kind of character they are dealing with.
