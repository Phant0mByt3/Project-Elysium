# 0301 — Specializations

## Overview

Each of Elysium's six launch classes (0300-Classes.md) offers two specializations, unlocked at level 10.

Specializations define the character's primary combat role and significantly reshape their abilities, talent tree, equipment priorities, and combat identity.

A specialization is not simply a stat adjustment. Each should feel like a different way of playing the same class.

# Vanguard

## Guardian

**Role:** Tank

Guardians turn the Vanguard's weapon mastery toward protecting allies and controlling the battlefield.

They rely on heavy armour, defensive positioning and sustained enemy control.

**Core identity:**

* Heavy defensive combat
* Damage mitigation
* Threat generation
* Crowd control
* Ally protection

---

## Berserker

**Role:** Damage

Berserkers abandon defensive discipline in favour of overwhelming aggression.

They build momentum through combat and become increasingly dangerous as fights continue.

**Core identity:**

* High melee damage
* Rage and momentum
* Aggressive combos
* Execute abilities
* Sustained pressure

---

# Arcanist

## Frostweaver

**Role:** Damage / Control

Frostweavers manipulate cold and arcane energy to control the battlefield.

Their strength comes from slowing enemies, creating zones and maintaining distance.

**Core identity:**

* Frost magic
* Crowd control
* Slows and roots
* Area denial
* Sustained spellcasting

---

## Pyromancer

**Role:** Damage / Burst

Pyromancers specialize in destructive elemental magic.

Their abilities build toward powerful bursts of fire damage.

**Core identity:**

* Fire magic
* Burst damage
* Burning effects
* Area damage
* High-risk spell combinations

---

# Shade

## Shadowblade

**Role:** Damage / Burst

Shadowblades are specialists in assassination and precision.

They rely heavily on stealth, positioning and powerful attacks against vulnerable targets.

**Core identity:**

* Stealth
* Critical strikes
* Assassination
* Mobility
* Single-target burst

---

## Trickster

**Role:** Damage / Utility

Tricksters rely on deception rather than pure damage.

They manipulate enemies through illusions, traps, distractions and unusual combat techniques.

**Core identity:**

* Deception
* Mobility
* Utility
* Illusions
* Enemy disruption

---

# Warden

## Wildshaper

**Role:** Damage / Tank

Wildshapers embrace the physical side of nature magic.

They transform into powerful creatures and use their forms to survive and deal damage.

**Core identity:**

* Shapeshifting
* Primal combat
* High durability
* Melee damage
* Transformation abilities

---

## Grovekeeper

**Role:** Healer

Grovekeepers focus on restoration and maintaining the health of their allies.

Their healing comes through nature magic, regeneration and protective growth.

**Core identity:**

* Nature healing
* Regeneration
* Healing over time
* Protective effects
* Area healing

---

# Oathkeeper

## Sentinel

**Role:** Tank

Sentinels embody the defensive side of the Oathkeeper tradition.

They use sacred power to protect allies and withstand attacks that would destroy ordinary warriors.

**Core identity:**

* Heavy defence
* Sacred barriers
* Ally protection
* Damage mitigation
* Defensive cooldowns

---

## Lightbringer

**Role:** Healer

Lightbringers channel Solthar's traditions into restorative magic.

They combine healing with defensive support and limited offensive holy abilities.

**Core identity:**

* Sacred healing
* Light magic
* Protective blessings
* Emergency healing
* Support abilities

---

# Wayfarer

## Marksman

**Role:** Damage / Precision

Marksmen specialize in ranged weapon mastery.

They maintain distance from enemies and reward careful positioning and accurate attacks.

**Core identity:**

* Ranged weapons
* Precision
* Critical hits
* Long-range attacks
* Weak-point mechanics

---

## Beastmaster

**Role:** Damage / Companion

Beastmasters form powerful bonds with creatures of the wild.

Their companions fight alongside them while the Wayfarer supports them from range.

**Core identity:**

* Beast companions
* Ranged combat
* Companion commands
* Tracking
* Coordinated attacks

# Specialization Progression

Specializations unlock at **level 10**.

Players choose their initial specialization after completing a short class-specific initiation.

The initiation introduces the philosophy and combat style of the specialization rather than simply presenting a menu.

For example:

```text
Vanguard

Level 10

Choose your path:

Guardian
"Stand where others fall."

Berserker
"Let nothing stand before you."
```

---

# Respecialization

Players can freely change between their class's two specializations outside of combat.

Respecialization should be accessible in major cities and through appropriate class-related NPCs.

The system is designed to encourage experimentation rather than punish players for choosing the "wrong" specialization.

Players should be able to maintain different equipment and talent configurations for each specialization.

---

# Specialization Identity

Each specialization must meaningfully change how its class plays.

Differences should include:

* Abilities
* Talent trees
* Resource interactions
* Rotation or ability priority
* Equipment preferences
* Combat animations
* Visual effects
* Defensive mechanics
* Utility
* Group role

Two specializations should never feel like:

```text
Same class
+
10% more damage
```

Instead, they should feel like:

```text
Same class fantasy
+
completely different combat expression
```

---

# Role Flexibility

Not every specialization needs to fit perfectly into a single role.

Some specializations may combine roles.

Examples:

**Wildshaper**

* Damage
* Secondary tanking

**Trickster**

* Damage
* Strong utility

**Lightbringer**

* Healing
* Limited damage

These hybrid identities should be intentional rather than accidental.

---

# Class Identity vs Specialization Identity

The class represents the character's broader tradition.

The specialization represents how they practice that tradition.

For example:

```text
Warden
│
├── Wildshaper
│   └── Become the predator.
│
└── Grovekeeper
    └── Become the protector of life.
```

Both are Wardens.

They simply express the Warden tradition differently.

---

# Design Rules

1. Every launch class has exactly two specializations.
2. Specializations unlock at level 10.
3. Both specializations must be mechanically distinct.
4. Specializations must share the core identity of their class.
5. Neither specialization should be a strict upgrade over the other.
6. Players can freely respecialize outside combat.
7. Specializations should have distinct talent trees.
8. Hybrid roles are allowed when they have a clear purpose.
9. Specializations should have distinct visual and mechanical identities.
10. Specialization balance is documented in [0309-Balance.md](0309-Balance.md).
11. Full ability design is documented in [0302-Skills.md](0302-Skills.md).
12. Talent progression is documented in [0303-Talent-Trees.md](0303-Talent-Trees.md).
13. No specialization should exist purely to fill a missing role.
14. The specialization should change how the player actually plays.
15. Class identity should remain recognizable regardless of specialization.
