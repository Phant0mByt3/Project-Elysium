# 43 — Talent Trees

## Overview
Talent trees are the primary build-customization system, letting players specialize further within their chosen specialization ([0301-Specializations.md](0301-Specializations.md)) rather than just picking bigger numbers.

## Structure
Each specialization has one talent tree with three sub-paths (e.g. a Berserker Warrior might lean into sustained damage, burst windows, or self-sufficiency). Points are earned roughly one per level from 10 onward, letting players make a meaningfully different build by max level than someone who chose differently at every tier.

## Design Rules
* No "trap" talents — every choice should be viable in some context, even if not optimal for every content type.
* Capstone talents (the final tier of each sub-path) should be build-defining, not just bigger passive bonuses.
* Full respec should always be available for a modest Aurum cost ([1001-Currency.md](../1000-Economy/1001-Currency.md)), to encourage build experimentation without punishing mistakes.

## Relationship to Other Systems
Talent trees interact directly with the skill unlocks in [0302-Skills.md](0302-Skills.md), the stat formulas in [0304-Stats.md](0304-Stats.md), and are the primary lever for ongoing balance work in [0309-Balance.md](0309-Balance.md). Full tree layouts are designed and iterated during Phase 2 ([0003-Roadmap.md](../0000-Project/0003-Roadmap.md)).
