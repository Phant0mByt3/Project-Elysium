# 45 — Stats

## Primary Stats
* **Strength** — increases physical damage for melee classes.
* **Intellect** — increases magic damage and healing.
* **Agility** — increases physical damage for finesse-based classes (Rogue, Ranger) and critical strike chance.
* **Stamina** — increases maximum health.

## Secondary Stats
* **Critical Strike Chance** — chance for an attack to deal bonus damage.
* **Haste** — reduces ability cooldowns and cast/attack speed.
* **Mastery** — a per-specialization stat that amplifies each spec's defining mechanic (exact effect defined per spec in [0301-Specializations.md](0301-Specializations.md)).
* **Versatility** — a flat damage/healing and damage-taken-reduction stat, providing a baseline power increase for any build.
* **Armor** — reduces physical damage taken.
* **Resistance** — reduces elemental damage taken, tied to [0307-Elements.md](0307-Elements.md).

## Formula Philosophy
Stat formulas should scale smoothly across the leveling range ([0305-Leveling.md](0305-Leveling.md)) without requiring hard stat squishes at expansion boundaries. Diminishing returns apply to secondary stats past certain thresholds to prevent single-stat itemization dominance — exact curve values are a Phase 1/2 engineering task ([0003-Roadmap.md](../0000-Project/0003-Roadmap.md)).

## Gear Interaction
Stats are the primary lever through which itemization ([0500-Weapons.md](../0500-Items/0500-Weapons.md) onward) differentiates gear beyond raw item level. See [0309-Balance.md](0309-Balance.md) for how stat weights are reviewed per patch.
