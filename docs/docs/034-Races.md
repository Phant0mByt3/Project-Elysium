# 34 — Races

## Playable Races

**Humans** — the most numerous race, originating in Aurelia. Adaptable, with no strong racial combat bonus but flexible class access. Predominantly Dawnbound.

**High Elves** — long-lived scholars and mages descended from Age of Concord nobility. Strong affinity for arcane classes. Predominantly Dawnbound, though a minority hold Duskward sympathies over the Concord's perceived nostalgia.

**Dwarves** — mountain-dwelling artisans of Vethmoor's Ironpeak Holds, patrons of Terravox. Strong affinity for melee and crafting professions ([066-Blacksmithing.md](066-Blacksmithing.md)). Dawnbound-aligned.

**Orcs** — clan-based warrior culture of Vethmoor's Ashenclaw Tundra, patrons of Ignareth. Strong affinity for melee combat and endurance. Duskward-aligned.

**Beastkin** — a broad group of animalistic humanoid clans, currently a minor presence in Aurelia and Vethmoor but the dominant culture of the future Kharzul Wastes continent ([151-Expansion-02.md](151-Expansion-02.md)). Duskward-aligned, strong affinity for ranged and nature-based classes.

**Revenants** — mortals marked by lingering Sundering energy, first appearing during the Age of Ash. Not undead, but permanently changed — pale, cold to the touch, with a natural affinity for death-adjacent magic (necromancer, some cleric specs). Associated with Voranthe. Duskward-aligned, viewed with suspicion by parts of the Concord.

## Design Notes
Race selection affects starting continent, available racial passive traits (documented alongside [045-Stats.md](045-Stats.md) once finalized), and faction default — though faction can be freely chosen regardless of race at creation. Non-playable races and cultures (Sylvaneth wood elves, Nightreach's corrupted peoples) are introduced with their respective continents; see [011-Continents.md](011-Continents.md).
