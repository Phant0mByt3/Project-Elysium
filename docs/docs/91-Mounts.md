# 91 — Mounts

## Overview
Mounts are the primary speed-boosting travel tool, unlocked starting at level 15 ([46-Leveling.md](46-Leveling.md)), and a major collection/cosmetic goal at all levels ([74-Achievements.md](74-Achievements.md)).

## Acquisition
* **Starter Mounts** — purchasable from any city stable once level-eligible.
* **Faction Mounts** — reputation-gated, Revered-tier rewards ([77-Factions-Reputation.md](77-Factions-Reputation.md)).
* **Rare/World Boss Mounts** — low-drop-chance rewards from world bosses ([18-World-Bosses.md](18-World-Bosses.md)) and raid bosses ([17-Raids.md](17-Raids.md)).
* **Achievement Mounts** — earned through exploration or completionist achievements ([74-Achievements.md](74-Achievements.md)).

## Mount Types
Ground mounts only at launch (see [20-Travel.md](20-Travel.md)); flying mounts are explicitly out of scope for launch to preserve the value of hand-built ground travel and world exploration, revisited as a future direction ([05-Future-Plans.md](05-Future-Plans.md)).

## Design Rules
Mount speed should be standardized (no pay-for-speed or grind-for-speed disparity) so that mount collecting remains purely cosmetic/prestige, never a power or convenience treadmill — consistent with [93-Cosmetics.md](93-Cosmetics.md)'s philosophy.
