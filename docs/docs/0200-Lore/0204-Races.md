# 0204 — Races

## Playable Races

**Humans** — the most numerous race, originating in Aurelia. Adaptable, with no strong racial combat bonus but flexible class access. Predominantly associated with the Dawnbound Concord, though human regional factions may hold different political positions.

**High Elves** — long-lived scholars and mages descended from Age of Concord nobility. Strong affinity for arcane classes. Predominantly associated with the Dawnbound Concord, though a minority hold Duskward sympathies due to opposition to the Concord's focus on restoring the old order.

**Dwarves** — mountain-dwelling artisans of Vethmoor's Ironpeak Holds, patrons of Terravox. Strong affinity for melee and crafting professions ([0606-Blacksmithing.md](../0600-Professions/0606-Blacksmithing.md)). Predominantly associated with the Dawnbound Concord, particularly through their established alliances with human and high elf territories.

**Orcs** — clan-based warrior culture of Vethmoor's Ashenclaw Tundra, patrons of Ignareth. Strong affinity for melee combat and endurance. Predominantly associated with the Duskward Pact, whose values of independence, strength, and new alliances align closely with traditional orcish clan structures.

**Beastkin** — a broad group of animalistic humanoid clans, currently a minor presence in Aurelia and Vethmoor but the dominant culture of the future Kharzul Wastes continent ([1600-Expansion-02.md](../1500-Expansions/1600-Expansion-02.md)). Predominantly associated with the Duskward Pact, with strong affinity for ranged and nature-based classes. Individual clans may maintain their own political identities and relationships with other factions.

**Revenants** — mortals marked by lingering Sundering energy, first appearing during the Age of Ash. Not undead, but permanently changed, often pale and cold to the touch, with a natural affinity for death-adjacent magic. Associated with Voranthe. Predominantly associated with the Duskward Pact, though individual Revenants may hold different political beliefs. Viewed with suspicion by parts of the Dawnbound Concord.

## Racial and Faction Alignment

Race does not determine a player's faction.

Each race has cultural and historical ties to one of the two major factions, but players may freely choose between the Dawnbound Concord and Duskward Pact during character creation.

This allows characters to exist outside the political expectations of their race.

For example:

* A human may join the Duskward Pact.
* An orc may join the Dawnbound Concord.
* A high elf may reject the Concord's restorationist ideology.
* A Revenant may seek acceptance within the Concord.
* A Beastkin character may follow a faction independent of their clan's traditional allegiance.

These choices may influence character dialogue, racial reactions, regional reputation, and certain questlines, but do not prevent a player from joining either major faction.

## Design Notes

Race selection affects starting continent, available racial passive traits (documented alongside [0304-Stats.md](../0300-Characters/0304-Stats.md) once finalized), racial starting conditions, and cultural relationships.

Faction selection is separate from race selection and can be freely chosen at character creation.

Regional factions may have stronger relationships with specific races. These relationships can affect reputation, dialogue, quests, city access, and other interactions without permanently restricting the player.

Non-playable races and cultures, including Sylvaneth wood elves and the corrupted peoples of Nightreach, are introduced with their respective continents. See [0101-Continents.md](../0100-World/0101-Continents.md).
