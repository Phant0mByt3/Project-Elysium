# 0205 — Kingdoms

## Overview

Kingdoms are the major political, cultural, and territorial powers of Elysium.

They are distinct from the two overarching player factions described in [0203-Factions.md](../0200-Lore/0203-Factions.md).

A kingdom or regional power governs a specific territory and develops its own culture, laws, leadership, military, and political interests.

The **Dawnbound Concord** and **Duskward Pact** are ideological alliances that operate across these political borders.

A kingdom may align with either major faction, remain neutral, or change its allegiance over time.

This distinction allows Elysium to contain many independent powers without reducing the world to two governments.

---

# Aurelia

Aurelia is one of the oldest surviving centers of human civilization and one of the strongest regions of the Dawnbound Concord.

Its cities contain many surviving structures from the Age of Concord, making the continent one of the primary centers of reclamation.

## The Concord Dominion

The primary human political power in Aurelia.

The Concord Dominion is governed from **Solmere** by the Concord Council.

It is not a monarchy.

The Council consists of representatives from major cities and territories, with each region maintaining a degree of local autonomy.

The Dominion's political structure reflects the Dawnbound belief that Elysium's future should be built through cooperation rather than centralized rule.

The Dominion is strongly aligned with the **Dawnbound Concord**.

Its major priorities include:

* Rebuilding the old human territories
* Restoring trade routes
* Recovering Age of Concord knowledge
* Protecting human settlements
* Maintaining alliances with dwarven and high elven powers
* Expanding access to previously unreachable regions

The Dominion's relationship with regional powers is not always peaceful.

Some territories believe the Concord Council has too much influence over their internal affairs.

---

## The Sylvaria Enclave

A semi-autonomous high elven territory within Aurelia.

Sylvaria contains some of the last intact archives from the Age of Concord.

Its scholars possess knowledge that was lost elsewhere during the Age of Ash.

The Enclave maintains close relations with the Concord Dominion but retains control over its own government, military, and magical institutions.

Sylvaria is generally **Dawnbound-aligned**, although some of its scholars question whether restoring the old world is actually possible.

Its archives are considered among the most valuable historical sites in Elysium.

---

## Silverwatch

A powerful regional faction within Aurelia.

Silverwatch is aligned with the Dawnbound Concord but maintains its own political identity and territorial interests.

Unlike the Concord Dominion, Silverwatch is primarily defined by its regional authority rather than its role in the wider Concord government.

The faction controls its own territory, settlements, military forces, and local administration.

Silverwatch is an example of how a regional faction can exist within a major faction without becoming part of its central government.

Its relationship with the Concord is generally cooperative, but political disagreements are possible.

Silverwatch may become involved in conflicts involving:

* Territorial borders
* Local security
* Trade routes
* Bandit activity
* Ancient ruins
* Regional succession
* Concord military policy

Further Silverwatch information should be documented in regional and faction-specific files.

# Vethmoor

Vethmoor is a harsh continent dominated by mountains, tundra, and ancient settlements.

Its political structure is primarily divided between dwarven holds and orc clans.

The two cultures have developed very different ideas about governance and civilization.

---

## The Ironpeak Holds

A confederation of dwarven clan-holds located throughout the Ironpeak mountains.

Each hold maintains its own leadership, laws, military forces, and internal traditions.

The holds unite under a rotating **High Forgemaster** when continent-wide decisions must be made.

The High Forgemaster is responsible primarily for diplomacy, defense, and coordination between the holds rather than ruling them directly.

The Ironpeak Holds are formally aligned with the **Dawnbound Concord**.

The dwarves see the restoration of trade, engineering, and old-world infrastructure as essential to Elysium's recovery.

Their major priorities include:

* Protecting the mountain holds
* Recovering lost dwarven technology
* Reopening ancient mines
* Restoring old roads and tunnels
* Maintaining relations with the Concord Dominion
* Preserving dwarven traditions

The Holds maintain strong religious ties to **Terravox** and **Ignareth**.

---

## The Ashenclaw Warbands

The primary orc political structure of the Ashenclaw Tundra.

The Ashenclaw are not a single kingdom.

They consist of numerous independent clans organized around a **Warcouncil of Clans**.

Each clan maintains its own territory, leadership, traditions, and military forces.

The Warcouncil exists to coordinate matters that affect the clans as a whole.

The Ashenclaw are formally aligned with the **Duskward Pact**, although individual clans differ significantly in their interpretation of Pact ideology.

Some clans believe the old world should be completely abandoned.

Others maintain traditions that originated during the Age of Concord.

Some clans maintain trade with Dawnbound territories despite the political divide.

The Warbands' major priorities include:

* Protecting clan territories
* Maintaining independence
* Preserving ancestral traditions
* Expanding access to hunting grounds
* Resisting outside political control
* Building alliances outside the old Concord structure

Ignareth is widely honored among the clans, particularly through sacred fires and forge traditions.

# Kingdom and Faction Relationships

Kingdoms and regional powers do not automatically share the same political goals as their associated major faction.

For example:

```text
Dawnbound Concord
│
├── Concord Dominion
│   └── Solmere
│
├── Sylvaria Enclave
│
├── Silverwatch
│
└── Ironpeak Holds
```

While:

```text
Duskward Pact
│
├── Ashenclaw Warbands
│
├── Duskward-aligned territories
├── Independent clans
└── Other regional powers
```

These relationships are political rather than absolute.

A kingdom can disagree with its major faction.

A regional faction can change allegiance.

A city can remain neutral.

A clan can refuse to participate in a larger war.

This allows Elysium's political landscape to change as the story progresses.

# Governance & Player Interaction

Kingdom politics primarily appear through:

* Faction reputation
* Regional reputation
* Questlines
* Territory control
* City access
* NPC relationships
* Trade
* Military conflicts
* World events

See [0707-Factions-Reputation.md](../0700-Quests/0707-Factions-Reputation.md) for reputation systems.

See [0806-Territory-Control.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0806-Territory-Control.md) for territory control.

Players do not directly rule kingdoms by default.

Instead, players influence political events through quests, reputation, faction allegiance, territory conflicts, and major story decisions.

Certain future storylines may allow players to significantly alter the leadership, territory, or political status of a kingdom.

# Political Structure

Elysium's political hierarchy can be summarized as:

```text
Elysium
│
├── Major Ideological Factions
│   ├── Dawnbound Concord
│   └── Duskward Pact
│
├── Kingdoms & Regional Powers
│   ├── Concord Dominion
│   ├── Sylvaria Enclave
│   ├── Silverwatch
│   ├── Ironpeak Holds
│   └── Ashenclaw Warbands
│
├── Neutral Organizations
│   ├── Wayfarer's Guild
│   ├── Cartographer's League
│   └── Ashen Circle
│
└── Independent / Hostile Powers
    ├── Local warbands
    ├── Cults
    ├── Bandit groups
    └── Other regional factions
```

The major factions determine the broad ideological conflict of Elysium.

Kingdoms and regional powers determine who actually controls the land.

Neutral organizations connect the world across political borders.

Hostile and independent groups create conflicts outside the major faction struggle.
