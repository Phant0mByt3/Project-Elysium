# 35 — Kingdoms

## Overview
Political entities within Elysium, distinct from the two overarching player factions ([0203-Factions.md](0203-Factions.md)) — kingdoms are cultural/national identities, while factions are ideological alliances that cross kingdom lines in places.

## Aurelia

**The Concord Dominion** — the primary human kingdom, governed by the Concord Council from Solmere. Not a monarchy; a council of regional representatives, reflecting the Dawnbound philosophy of cooperative rebuilding.

**The Sylvaria Enclave** — a smaller high-elf territory within Aurelia's borders, semi-autonomous, holding some of the last intact Age of Concord archives.

## Vethmoor

**The Ironpeak Holds** — a confederation of dwarven clan-holds, each independently governed but united under a rotating High Forgemaster for matters of continent-wide defense. Formally Dawnbound-aligned.

**The Ashenclaw Warbands** — the orc clan structure, organized around a Warcouncil of Clans rather than a single ruler. Formally Duskward-aligned, though individual clans vary in how strictly they follow Pact doctrine.

## Governance & Player Interaction
Kingdom politics surface primarily through faction reputation storylines ([0707-Factions-Reputation.md](../0700-Quests/0707-Factions-Reputation.md)) and territory control ([0806-Territory-Control.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0806-Territory-Control.md)) rather than direct player rulership. Kingdoms for future continents (Sylvaneth, Kharzul Wastes) will be documented as those continents are scoped — see [0101-Continents.md](../0100-World/0101-Continents.md).
