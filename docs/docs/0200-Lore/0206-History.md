# 0206 — History

Detailed historical events referenced throughout Elysium's quests, characters, locations, factions, and world lore.

See [0200-Lore.md](../0200-Lore/0200-Lore.md) for the thematic overview and [0201-Timeline.md](../0200-Lore/0201-Timeline.md) for the high-level chronology.

---

# The Founding of Aethercrest

### Age of Concord

Long before the Sundering, the civilizations of Elysium established a shared capital at the heart of the united world.

The city was named **Aethercrest**.

Unlike the later capitals of individual kingdoms, Aethercrest belonged to no single race.

Humans, high elves, dwarves, and other peoples maintained districts within the city.

The city became the greatest symbol of the Age of Concord.

Its central institutions included:

* The Concord Assembly
* The Grand Arcanum
* The Hall of Oaths
* The Aetherian Archives
* The Temple of the Elysian Circle
* The Great Forge
* The Wayfarer's Hall

Aethercrest became a center of diplomacy, magic, scholarship, trade, and religion.

It was here that many of the institutions later destroyed during the Sundering were first established.

---

# The Rise of the Great Civilizations

### Age of Concord

As the Age of Concord progressed, the major civilizations of Elysium reached unprecedented levels of prosperity.

Human kingdoms expanded across Aurelia.

The high elves established vast magical academies.

The dwarves expanded the Ironpeak Holds and developed advanced metallurgy.

Orc clans established territories across the northern regions.

Trade routes connected distant cultures.

The major class traditions developed into recognizable disciplines.

The six traditions that would eventually become the modern classes emerged during this period:

* Vanguard
* Arcanist
* Shade
* Warden
* Oathkeeper
* Wayfarer

Although their names and practices changed over time, their fundamental philosophies survived the Sundering.

---

# The Exclusion of Kaelgorath

### Late Age of Concord

Kaelgorath's disagreement with the other gods gradually became impossible to hide.

He believed that mortals had been given too much freedom.

The other gods believed that freedom was the foundation of mortal civilization.

The disagreement eventually reached the highest institutions of the Circle.

Kaelgorath was excluded from the Circle's councils.

His followers were increasingly isolated.

Some mortal kingdoms continued worshipping him.

Others abandoned him.

The exact circumstances surrounding his exclusion remain disputed.

The surviving historical records were written by mortals long after the event and often contradict one another.

---

# The Fall of Aethercrest

### The Sundering

Aethercrest became the epicenter of Kaelgorath's attack.

The city was not simply destroyed.

It was torn apart.

The ground beneath the capital collapsed as the divine conflict reached the mortal world.

Buildings were shattered.

Magical structures exploded.

The central districts disappeared beneath the earth.

Entire sections of the city were pulled into massive underground caverns.

The destruction of Aethercrest became one of the defining moments of the Sundering.

The city's surviving ruins eventually became known as the **Shattered Cairns**.

Centuries later, the remains would be rediscovered beneath Vethmoor.

The ruins now form the foundation of the launch raid:

**The Sunken Concord**

See [0107-Raids.md](../0100-World/0107-Raids.md).

# The Sundering

### ~800 years ago

The battle between Kaelgorath and the Elysian Circle shattered the old world.

The consequences extended far beyond the destruction of Aethercrest.

The original continent fractured.

Seas flooded ancient civilizations.

Mountain ranges shifted.

Magical currents became unstable.

The boundaries between life and death were damaged.

Millions of mortals died.

Entire kingdoms disappeared.

The surviving gods ultimately succeeded in stopping Kaelgorath.

But they could not destroy him.

Kaelgorath was scattered across Elysium.

The gods then disappeared from direct mortal contact.

The Age of Concord ended.

The Age of Ash began.

---

# The First Years of the Age of Ash

### ~800–700 years ago

The survivors of the Sundering abandoned the ruins of the old capitals.

Many settlements became isolated almost immediately.

Trade collapsed.

Long-distance travel became dangerous.

Ancient institutions stopped functioning.

The surviving populations focused almost entirely on food, shelter, defense, and survival.

The old political structure of Elysium ceased to exist.

No single government replaced it.

Hundreds of small communities emerged instead.

Some eventually became the foundations of modern kingdoms.

---

# The First Revenant Sighting

### ~600 years ago

The earliest documented Revenant appeared roughly two centuries after the Sundering.

The individual was a soldier exploring the ruins of Aethercrest.

During the expedition, the soldier came into direct contact with concentrated Sundering energy.

They survived.

But they returned changed.

Their body became unnaturally cold.

Their appearance altered.

Their connection to life and death became abnormal.

The soldier was initially believed to be undead.

Later examinations proved otherwise.

The individual was still alive.

The event became the earliest reliable record of what would eventually become known as the **Revenants**.

The phenomenon spread slowly.

Some affected individuals formed isolated communities.

Others were driven away by fearful settlements.

Over the following centuries, Revenants became a permanent part of Elysian society.

---

# The Lost Centuries

### Age of Ash

For several centuries, Elysium existed without a unified political structure.

Historical records from this period are extremely fragmented.

Many cultures developed independently.

Languages diverged.

Religious traditions changed.

The old Age of Concord became increasingly mythologized.

Some societies remembered it as a perfect golden age.

Others remembered it as a period of inequality and political control.

Very few people possessed enough surviving information to understand what had actually happened.

This period is now known as the **Lost Centuries**.

---

# The Rebuilding of Aurelia

### Middle Age of Ash

Human settlements in Aurelia gradually expanded.

Fortified villages became towns.

Towns became cities.

Trade routes began returning.

Several regional powers emerged.

Some attempted to recreate the political institutions of the Age of Concord.

Others rejected them completely.

This disagreement would eventually become one of the foundations of the modern Dawnbound and Duskward ideologies.

---

# The Rise of the Ironpeak Holds

### Middle Age of Ash

The dwarven settlements beneath Vethmoor became increasingly interconnected.

Individual clans began cooperating to protect mines, tunnels, and mountain settlements.

The Ironpeak Holds eventually formed a loose confederation.

Each hold retained its own leadership.

A rotating **High Forgemaster** was established to coordinate defense and diplomacy between the holds.

The Ironpeak system survived into the modern era.

The Holds later became one of the strongest supporters of the Dawnbound Concord.

---

# The Ashenclaw Accords

### ~150 years ago

The orc clans of the Ashenclaw Tundra had traditionally maintained independent territories.

As human and dwarven powers began expanding their influence into Vethmoor, several clans feared that the old pattern of isolation would leave them vulnerable.

The clans began negotiating.

The result was the **Ashenclaw Accords**.

The Accords established the **Warcouncil of Clans**, allowing the clans to coordinate military and political decisions without surrendering their independence.

The resulting Ashenclaw Warbands became one of the largest political powers in Vethmoor.

The Accords also created the political foundation for later Duskward cooperation.

---

# The Founding of the Dawnbound Concord

### ~150 years ago

Human and high elven survivor settlements had spent generations rebuilding their societies.

Many leaders believed that continued fragmentation would eventually lead to another collapse.

Representatives from several major settlements gathered to establish a permanent alliance.

The resulting organization became the **Dawnbound Concord**.

The name was deliberately chosen as a reference to the lost Age of Concord.

Its founding charter declared that Elysium could be rebuilt through cooperation between its surviving civilizations.

Solmere became the political center of the movement.

The Concord quickly gained support among human, high elven, and dwarven territories.

Regional powers retained their own governments while aligning themselves with the larger alliance.

The Concord's philosophy eventually became summarized as:

**"Restore what was lost."**

---

# The Ashenclaw-Concord Tensions

### ~140–120 years ago

The growth of the Dawnbound Concord created tension in Vethmoor.

Concord-aligned settlements expanded trade routes and military positions near Ashenclaw territory.

Some orc clans considered this normal trade expansion.

Others saw it as the beginning of another attempt to rebuild the old political order at their expense.

Diplomatic relations deteriorated.

Several border conflicts occurred.

None developed into a full continental war, but the events permanently changed relations between the two cultures.

---

# The Founding of the Duskward Pact

### ~120 years ago

The political alliance between the Ashenclaw clans and other groups opposed to Concord restoration gradually expanded.

Beastkin groups, independent territories, Revenant communities, and other powers began cooperating with the Ashenclaw.

Their shared belief was not necessarily hatred of the Dawnbound Concord.

Instead, they rejected the idea that the old world should simply be restored.

The **Duskward Pact** was eventually formalized.

Ashka Vor became its political center.

The Pact's philosophy became summarized as:

**"Build what comes next."**

The Pact was never intended to function as a single kingdom.

Its members retained their own cultures, governments, and territories.

---

# The Rise of Silverwatch

### Late Age of Ash

Silverwatch emerged as one of the most influential regional powers within Aurelia.

Its military and administrative structure allowed it to control a strategically important territory while maintaining considerable independence from the Concord Dominion.

Silverwatch formally aligned with the Dawnbound Concord.

Its relationship with the Concord became an example of how regional powers could participate in a major faction without becoming directly governed by it.

Silverwatch remains one of the Concord's most important regional allies.

---

# The Reopening of the Continents

### ~100 years ago

Explorers began recording strange changes in the world's geography.

The separated continents appeared to be moving.

At first, the changes were too small to notice.

Over several decades, the distances between major landmasses became measurably smaller.

Old sea routes became possible again.

Previously unreachable islands appeared along established trade paths.

The phenomenon became known as **the Reconnection**.

No definitive explanation has been found.

---

# The First Reclamation Expeditions

### ~80 years ago

Organized expeditions began entering territories that had been inaccessible since the Sundering.

Explorers found:

* Ancient ruins
* Lost roads
* Abandoned cities
* Magical artifacts
* Old weapons
* Forgotten libraries
* Previously unknown cultures
* Signs of Sundering corruption

The discovery of these sites created a new profession built around exploration and recovery.

The **Cartographer's League** expanded rapidly during this period.

The Wayfarer's Guild also established new routes between recovering settlements.

---

# The Return of Ancient Magic

### ~50 years ago

Magical activity began increasing across Elysium.

Ancient relics became active.

Long-dormant magical structures began functioning.

Creatures believed extinct began appearing.

Some Arcanists discovered spells that had not been practiced since the Age of Concord.

The cause remains unknown.

Several theories emerged.

Some scholars believe the continents' reconnection is restoring the world's magical balance.

Others believe the gods are returning.

The Ashen Circle began investigating a more disturbing possibility.

The fragments of Kaelgorath may be becoming active again.

---

# The Ashen Circle Investigation

### Recent decades

The Ashen Circle began documenting strange similarities between modern corruption and ancient accounts of the Sundering.

Their research suggests that areas containing strong traces of Kaelgorath's influence are becoming increasingly active.

The Circle's findings are controversial.

The Dawnbound Concord fears that public knowledge of the research could cause panic.

The Duskward Pact suspects that the Concord may be suppressing information.

The Circle itself refuses to release all of its findings.

Its members claim that some discoveries are too dangerous to make public.

---

# The Last Twenty Years

The pace of change has accelerated.

Aurelia and Vethmoor are now connected by routes that had been impossible for centuries.

Merchants have returned to previously abandoned roads.

Explorers are reaching ancient ruins before scholars can document them.

Treasure hunters are looting historical sites.

Regional powers are competing for newly accessible territory.

The Dawnbound Concord sees the new connections as an opportunity to restore civilization.

The Duskward Pact sees the same territories as opportunities to create new settlements outside the old political order.

Neutral organizations attempt to keep trade and exploration moving despite growing political tension.

At the same time, reports of corruption have increased.

---

# The Current Situation

### Present Day

Elysium stands at a turning point.

The continents are reconnecting.

Civilization is expanding.

Ancient knowledge is returning.

The Dawnbound Concord and Duskward Pact are competing for influence.

Regional powers are attempting to protect their independence.

Neutral organizations are operating across faction boundaries.

The old gods remain silent.

Kaelgorath remains scattered.

And the ruins of the old world are beginning to reveal things that were never meant to be remembered.

The player's journey begins during this period.

---

# The Sunken Concord

The ruins of Aethercrest beneath the Shattered Cairns are among the most important discoveries of the Age of Reclamation.

For centuries, the location was believed to be inaccessible.

Recent expeditions have uncovered entrances leading deep beneath Vethmoor.

The ruins contain surviving sections of the ancient capital.

They also contain evidence of what happened during the final moments of the Sundering.

The expedition eventually becomes the launch raid:

**The Sunken Concord**

See [0107-Raids.md](../0100-World/0107-Raids.md).

The raid is not simply a battle against ancient monsters.

It is an archaeological disaster waiting to happen.

Every chamber contains pieces of a history that could challenge what modern Elysium believes about the Sundering.

---

# Historical Mysteries

Several major questions remain unresolved.

### What was Aethercrest really built to protect?

The city contained enormous magical structures whose purpose has not been fully identified.

### Why did Kaelgorath attack the Circle?

The official explanation is resentment toward mortal free will.

Some surviving evidence suggests there may have been another reason.

### Where are the remaining fragments of Kaelgorath?

Known corrupted zones appear to contain only a fraction of his remaining influence.

### Why are the continents reconnecting?

No accepted theory explains the phenomenon.

### Why is ancient magic returning?

The timing appears connected to the Reconnection.

### What happened to the other civilizations of the Age of Concord?

Many cultures simply disappeared.

Some may have survived somewhere beyond the known world.

### What caused the first Revenants?

Sundering energy is the accepted explanation.

But the exact mechanism remains unknown.

### Why did the gods become silent?

The Circle has never explained what happened after the Sundering.

No confirmed divine communication has occurred since.

# Historical Continuity

Historical events should have visible consequences in the modern world.

The Sundering should not exist only in books.

Players should encounter its effects through:

* Ruins
* Architecture
* Languages
* Religious traditions
* Family histories
* Ancient weapons
* Lost roads
* Regional borders
* Political disputes
* Racial relationships
* Environmental corruption
* Magical anomalies
* NPC memories and oral traditions

The Age of Concord should likewise feel like something that genuinely existed rather than a fictional golden age described only through exposition.

Every surviving piece of the old world should tell part of its story.

The same applies to the Age of Ash.

Modern Elysium exists because generations of people survived, adapted, and rebuilt after the Sundering.

---

# Historical Design Principle

Elysium's history should follow one central principle:

**The past should remain visible.**

Cities should be built on ruins.

Political borders should follow ancient conflicts.

Religious traditions should preserve fragments of forgotten beliefs.

Characters should inherit grudges they did not create.

Ancient technology should influence modern engineering.

Old wars should still affect modern diplomacy.

The Sundering should still shape the world eight centuries later.

The history of Elysium is not finished.

The Age of Reclamation is creating the next chapter.
