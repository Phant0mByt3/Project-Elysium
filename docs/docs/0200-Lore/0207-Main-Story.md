# 0207 — Main Story

## Overview

The main campaign is the central narrative of Elysium's **Age of Reclamation**.

It begins when travel between **Aurelia** and **Vethmoor** becomes possible again for the first time since the Sundering.

What initially appears to be a geographic phenomenon quickly becomes something much larger.

Ancient roads are resurfacing.

Long-dormant magical structures are activating.

Ruins thought to be unreachable are becoming accessible.

And fragments of the power that caused the Sundering are beginning to stir.

The player becomes involved in the investigation while navigating the growing political conflict between the **Dawnbound Concord** and **Duskward Pact**.

The campaign eventually leads beneath the Shattered Cairns, into the ruins of **Aethercrest**, where the player discovers that the Sundering may not be as well understood as modern Elysium believes.

---

# Story Premise

For centuries, Aurelia and Vethmoor have been separated by the consequences of the Sundering.

Travel between the continents became impossible.

The civilizations that survived developed independently.

The old world became history.

Now the continents are moving closer together.

Nobody knows why.

At first, the change is treated as a miracle.

Trade routes reopen.

Explorers return to forgotten territories.

Ancient cities become accessible.

The Dawnbound Concord sees an opportunity to accelerate the restoration of civilization.

The Duskward Pact sees an opportunity to establish new territories beyond the control of the old powers.

Both sides begin sending expeditions into the newly accessible regions.

The player becomes involved in one such expedition.

What begins as an investigation into the Reconnection eventually reveals something far more dangerous.

The same forces causing ancient structures to awaken are also disturbing fragments of **Kaelgorath's remaining influence**.

---

# The Player's Role

The player is not a predetermined hero or chosen one.

They begin as an ordinary adventurer entering a world undergoing rapid change.

Their race, class, starting region, and faction influence how different characters react to them, but the central story remains accessible regardless of these choices.

The player gradually becomes important because of their actions.

They investigate.

They explore.

They make alliances.

They uncover information.

They survive situations that would have killed less experienced adventurers.

By the later stages of the campaign, the player becomes one of the few people capable of confronting threats connected to the Sundering.

---

# Faction Perspective

The main story can be experienced from either major faction.

## Dawnbound Concord

Dawnbound characters generally encounter the story through the perspective of restoration and preservation.

The Reconnection is seen as an opportunity to recover the lost world.

Their story emphasizes:

* Restoring ancient cities
* Recovering lost knowledge
* Protecting historical sites
* Rebuilding trade routes
* Preserving Age of Concord artifacts
* Preventing another catastrophe

The Concord tends to view the investigation as a matter of protecting civilization.

---

## Duskward Pact

Duskward characters generally encounter the story through the perspective of independence and caution toward the old world.

The Reconnection is seen as an opportunity to build something new.

Their story emphasizes:

* Preventing Concord expansion
* Protecting independent territories
* Recovering technology for new civilizations
* Challenging old historical narratives
* Preventing ancient powers from returning
* Ensuring the mistakes of the past are not repeated

The Pact tends to view the investigation as a matter of preventing history from repeating itself.

---

## Shared Story

Despite the different perspectives, both factions eventually discover the same central threat.

The Reconnection is not simply a natural phenomenon.

Something is changing beneath Elysium.

Ancient magical structures are responding.

Corrupted regions are becoming more active.

Fragments of Kaelgorath's influence are beginning to awaken.

The player eventually learns that neither the Concord nor the Pact fully understands what happened during the Sundering.

---

# Act I — Reconnection

### Levels 1–20 | Aurelia

The player begins in their chosen starting region of Aurelia.

The opening portion of the campaign is heavily connected to the player's local environment.

Regional questlines introduce:

* Local cultures
* Regional factions
* Major cities
* Local conflicts
* The player's class
* The player's race
* The Dawnbound Concord
* The Duskward Pact
* The current political situation

The main story gradually becomes visible through these local events rather than immediately presenting the player with the world's largest crisis.

---

## The First Signs

Strange events begin occurring throughout Aurelia.

Ancient ruins activate.

Old magical objects begin functioning.

Travelers report seeing structures that were previously unreachable.

Cartographers begin updating their maps.

Merchants begin discussing new routes.

The player initially encounters these events as isolated incidents.

Eventually, they realize the incidents are connected.

---

## The Reconnection

The player learns that the distance between Aurelia and Vethmoor has decreased significantly.

For the first time in centuries, ships and expeditions can travel between the continents.

The Dawnbound Concord wants to establish permanent routes.

The Duskward Pact fears that the Concord will use the opening to expand its influence.

Both factions begin sending expeditions.

The player becomes involved in the first major expedition toward Vethmoor.

---

## Act I Finale

The player completes the first major expedition across the reopened route.

During the journey, they encounter an ancient magical phenomenon.

The event contains energy resembling descriptions of the Sundering.

The player survives.

The discovery is reported to the relevant faction.

The expedition concludes with the player arriving in Vethmoor.

The story moves into its second act.

---

# Act II — Fracture Lines

### Levels 20–40 | Vethmoor

Vethmoor introduces the player to a continent shaped heavily by the consequences of the Sundering.

The player encounters the **Ironpeak Holds** and **Ashenclaw Warbands**.

The political situation becomes more complicated.

The Dawnbound Concord considers the Ironpeak Holds an important ally.

The Duskward Pact maintains strong relationships with the Ashenclaw.

Neither side fully trusts the other.

---

## The Ancient Ruins

Both major factions begin investigating ruins uncovered by the Reconnection.

These sites contain technology dating from before the Sundering.

Some appear to have been built during the Age of Concord.

Others appear significantly older.

The player discovers that several ruins contain the same strange energy encountered during the journey to Vethmoor.

---

## The Ironpeak Investigation

The player assists the dwarves in investigating ancient structures beneath the mountains.

The ruins contain:

* Ancient machinery
* Magical infrastructure
* Lost weapons
* Historical records
* Strange energy sources
* Evidence of Aethercrest's final days

The discoveries suggest that the people of the Age of Concord understood the Sundering differently than modern historians believe.

---

## The Ashenclaw Investigation

The player becomes involved with the Ashenclaw Warbands.

The orcs possess oral histories that survived the destruction of the old world.

Some of these stories contradict surviving Concord records.

The player begins discovering that history has been interpreted differently by different cultures.

The truth is buried somewhere between them.

---

# The Ashen Circle

During Act II, the player encounters the **Ashen Circle**.

The Circle is investigating the increasing magical activity surrounding the ancient ruins.

Its members believe the energy is connected to Kaelgorath.

Initially, they cannot prove this.

The player assists them in collecting evidence.

The Circle eventually confirms that the energy is not simply residual Sundering energy.

Something is actively responding to the Reconnection.

---

# The Fracture

As the investigation continues, tensions between the Dawnbound Concord and Duskward Pact escalate.

Both sides begin competing for control of ancient sites.

Regional factions become involved.

Some settlements fear that the conflict could become another continental war.

The player is forced to navigate political conflicts while continuing the investigation.

Eventually, evidence is uncovered that points toward a single location.

The ruins of **Aethercrest**.

---

# Act II Finale

The player discovers that the original capital was not completely destroyed.

Large portions of Aethercrest survived beneath the earth.

The ruins lie beneath the **Shattered Cairns**.

The discovery changes the political situation immediately.

Both major factions want access to the ruins.

The Ashen Circle warns that the site should not be opened.

The player is sent toward the Shattered Cairns.

The story enters its final act.

---

# Act III — The Sunken Concord

### Levels 40–50 | Shattered Cairns

The final act takes place across the ruins surrounding Aethercrest.

The player discovers the remains of the greatest city of the Age of Concord.

The ruins contain evidence of the final days before the Sundering.

They also contain something far more dangerous.

A fragment of Kaelgorath's influence has survived beneath the city.

---

## The Shattered Cairns

The Shattered Cairns are a massive region of broken terrain surrounding the buried remains of Aethercrest.

Ancient structures emerge from beneath the ground.

Magical energy leaks through cracks in the ruins.

Creatures affected by corruption inhabit the area.

The player must navigate the ruins while competing expeditions attempt to reach the same destination.

---

## The Truth Beneath Aethercrest

The player discovers that Aethercrest was more than a capital.

The city was constructed around enormous magical infrastructure.

The purpose of this infrastructure was connected to the relationship between the mortal world and the Elysian Circle.

The player begins to understand why Kaelgorath attacked the city.

But the full truth remains incomplete.

Some records are missing.

Some have been deliberately destroyed.

Others contradict one another.

---

# The Sunken Concord

The player's investigation eventually reaches the deepest surviving section of Aethercrest.

This becomes the launch raid:

**The Sunken Concord**

See [0107-Raids.md](../0100-World/0107-Raids.md).

The raid represents the culmination of the launch campaign.

Players descend into the buried capital and confront creatures and forces corrupted by Kaelgorath's influence.

The final encounter involves a fragment of Kaelgorath's power.

The player cannot destroy Kaelgorath himself.

Instead, they prevent the fragment from fully manifesting.

---

# Act III Finale

The player succeeds in stopping the immediate threat.

But the victory raises more questions than it answers.

Kaelgorath is still scattered.

The gods remain silent.

The continents continue reconnecting.

Ancient magical structures continue activating.

And the player has discovered evidence suggesting that the accepted history of the Sundering is incomplete.

The campaign ends with Elysium entering a new phase.

The Reclamation is no longer simply a period of rebuilding.

It has become a race to understand what is returning.

# Story Design Rules

### Player Choice

The player's faction choice changes the framing of the story but does not remove the core campaign.

Dawnbound and Duskward characters should experience different dialogue, motivations, rewards, and political perspectives while still reaching the major story milestones.

### Race

Race may influence dialogue, NPC reactions, cultural references, and certain optional quests.

Race should not prevent a player from completing the main campaign.

### Class

Class should influence combat, dialogue flavor, optional interactions, and certain quest approaches.

The main story should remain playable by every class.

### Regional Stories

Regional questlines should introduce the player to the cultures and political conflicts of each area.

They should reinforce the main campaign without being mandatory for understanding it.

### Side Stories

Side stories should expand the world, characters, gods, races, factions, and history.

They should never be required to understand the central plot.

See [0208-Side-Stories.md](../0200-Lore/0208-Side-Stories.md).

### Historical Discovery

The player should learn Elysium's history through the world rather than receiving all of it through exposition.

Important historical information should appear through:

* Ruins
* NPC dialogue
* Ancient documents
* Relics
* Quest objectives
* Environmental storytelling
* Class traditions
* Religious sites
* Regional folklore
* Faction records

Different cultures may provide contradictory versions of the same event.

The player should gradually determine what is reliable.

---

# Core Mysteries

The main campaign introduces several mysteries that can continue into future expansions.

### Why are the continents reconnecting?

The player learns that the phenomenon is connected to ancient magic, but its ultimate cause remains unresolved.

### What really happened during the Sundering?

The player discovers evidence that challenges the simplified historical account.

### What happened to the gods?

The Elysian Circle has remained silent for eight centuries.

The player finds evidence that the silence may have a deeper cause.

### How much of Kaelgorath remains?

The player destroys or suppresses one fragment.

The fate of the remaining fragments is unknown.

### What was Aethercrest actually built for?

The deepest purpose of the ancient capital remains unresolved.

### Why is ancient magic returning now?

The timing of the Reconnection and the return of magic appears too precise to be coincidence.

---

# Campaign Philosophy

The launch campaign should establish one central idea:

**Elysium is not simply recovering its past. It is awakening it.**

The Age of Concord is returning through its ruins, technology, magic, and political ideas.

But the world that existed before the Sundering cannot simply be restored.

The people of modern Elysium have changed.

The continents have changed.

The gods have changed.

And whatever remains of Kaelgorath has changed as well.

The player begins the story believing they are helping rebuild a broken world.

By the end of the launch campaign, they should understand that rebuilding Elysium may also mean confronting everything that was buried with it.
