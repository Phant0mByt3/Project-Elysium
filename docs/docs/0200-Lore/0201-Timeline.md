# Age of Dawn

### Mythic Era

Before recorded history, Elysium was shaped by the gods of the **Elysian Circle**.

Solthar, Nyxara, Terravox, Maelithir, Ignareth, Voranthe, and Aeloria shaped the world's fundamental forces and established the foundations upon which mortal civilization would eventually develop.

The gods created a world capable of sustaining life, but they did not attempt to control every aspect of it.

Mortals were given freedom to develop their own societies, beliefs, traditions, and cultures.

The earliest mortal civilizations emerged during this period.

Human settlements spread across the lands that would eventually become Aurelia.

The ancestors of the high elves established their earliest kingdoms.

Dwarven ancestors settled beneath the mountains.

Orc clans developed in the northern regions.

The ancestors of the Beastkin formed their own independent cultures.

The world was still young, and magic was unstable.

The boundaries between the mortal world and the divine forces that shaped it were far thinner than they would become later.

Many ancient ruins and artifacts found in modern Elysium originate from this era.

### Major Events

* The Elysian Circle shapes Elysium.
* The founding mortal races emerge.
* The earliest civilizations form.
* The first magical traditions develop.
* The gods maintain direct contact with mortals.

# Age of Concord

### ~1,200 – 800 years ago

The scattered civilizations of Elysium gradually establish contact with one another.

Trade routes expand.

Knowledge spreads between cultures.

Diplomatic alliances replace many of the conflicts that had divided early civilizations.

This period becomes known as the **Age of Concord**.

The great capitals are constructed.

Solmere becomes one of the most influential cities in the world.

The Ironpeak Holds expand beneath Vethmoor.

High elven academies become centers of magical study.

Human kingdoms develop complex governments and legal systems.

Dwarven craftsmen reach unprecedented levels of engineering and metallurgy.

Explorers establish routes across the oceans.

The six major class traditions begin to take recognizable form:

* Vanguard
* Arcanist
* Shade
* Warden
* Oathkeeper
* Wayfarer

The traditions are initially cultural and regional rather than standardized worldwide.

### The Elysian Circle

The gods remain present during much of the Age of Concord.

Their temples become major centers of culture and learning.

Divine relics are created.

Religious orders become increasingly influential.

Mortals begin attempting to understand the true nature of the gods rather than simply worshipping them.

### The Rise of Kaelgorath

Kaelgorath, originally an eighth member of the divine order, becomes increasingly opposed to the direction of mortal civilization.

He believes the gods created Elysium and therefore should have authority over its future.

The other gods disagree.

The disagreement becomes increasingly hostile as Kaelgorath begins to view mortal free will as a fundamental flaw in the world.

Kaelgorath is eventually excluded from the Circle's councils.

His resentment grows.

### Major Events

* Mortal civilizations become interconnected.
* The great capitals are established.
* Magic becomes a formal discipline.
* The six major class traditions develop.
* The great kingdoms and regional powers emerge.
* Kaelgorath turns against the Circle.
* Kaelgorath is cast out of the Circle's councils.

---

# The Sundering

### ~800 years ago

The final years of the Age of Concord end with the greatest catastrophe in Elysian history.

Kaelgorath attacks the Elysian Circle.

His goal is not conquest.

He intends to unmake Elysium and rebuild it according to his own vision.

The other gods intervene.

The resulting conflict spreads into the mortal world.

The battle becomes known as the **Sundering**.

The world's original continent is torn apart.

Mountains shift.

Seas flood entire regions.

Cities disappear.

Ancient civilizations collapse.

Magic erupts across the world.

The boundaries between life and death are damaged.

Millions of mortals die.

Entire kingdoms vanish.

The old roads and trade routes become unusable.

The surviving gods ultimately succeed in stopping Kaelgorath, but they cannot destroy him.

Kaelgorath is scattered and weakened.

He becomes known as the **Sundered One**.

The gods survive, but their connection to the mortal world is destroyed.

Their voices disappear.

The Age of Concord ends.

The **Age of Ash** begins.

### Major Events

* Kaelgorath attacks the Circle.
* The divine battle begins.
* Elysium's original continent fractures.
* The great capitals are destroyed or isolated.
* Magic is violently disrupted.
* The boundaries between life and death are damaged.
* The first Revenants appear.
* Kaelgorath is scattered.
* The gods become silent.
* The Age of Concord ends.

---

# Age of Ash

### ~800 – ~100 years ago

The centuries following the Sundering are defined by survival.

The surviving civilizations become isolated.

Long-distance travel becomes extremely dangerous.

Trade collapses.

Ancient roads disappear.

Knowledge is lost as libraries, academies, and cities are destroyed.

The surviving peoples retreat into fortified settlements, mountain holds, isolated kingdoms, clans, and remote communities.

The old world gradually becomes something remembered rather than experienced.

### The Silence

The gods no longer speak directly to mortals.

Temples remain.

Shrines remain.

Relics remain.

But divine communication disappears.

Religion changes from direct interaction with the gods into tradition, interpretation, and faith.

Different cultures begin developing radically different understandings of the same gods.

### The Revenants

The lingering energy of the Sundering begins producing permanent changes in some survivors.

These mortals become known as **Revenants**.

They are not undead.

Their bodies remain alive, but their connection to life and death has been permanently altered.

Many societies fear them.

Others accept them.

Some attempt to study them.

Revenant communities eventually develop their own cultures and traditions.

### The Loss of Knowledge

Much of the old world becomes myth.

The exact locations of ancient cities are forgotten.

Old magical techniques disappear.

Languages change.

Historical records become contradictory.

The Age of Concord becomes increasingly romanticized.

### The Rise of New Powers

As the old kingdoms disappear, new political powers emerge.

Human city-states form throughout Aurelia.

Dwarven holds strengthen their control over the Ironpeak mountains.

Orc clans establish territories throughout the Ashenclaw Tundra.

Beastkin clans spread through isolated regions.

Revenant communities form around areas heavily affected by the Sundering.

The political structure of modern Elysium begins taking shape.

---

# Late Age of Ash

### ~300 – ~100 years ago

Civilization begins recovering from its initial collapse.

Settlements grow into towns.

Towns become cities.

Trade slowly returns.

Exploration resumes on a limited scale.

Fragments of old knowledge are recovered.

The people of Elysium begin debating what should happen next.

Two major philosophies emerge.

---

## The Dawnbound Philosophy

Some believe the old civilization should be restored.

They argue that the Age of Concord was not the cause of Elysium's destruction.

Kaelgorath was.

Their goal becomes the restoration of the old world, its institutions, its cities, and its alliances.

This philosophy eventually develops into the **Dawnbound Concord**.

Its strongest supporters include humans, high elves, and dwarves.

---

## The Duskward Philosophy

Others believe the old world failed long before the Sundering.

They argue that rebuilding the same political structures will eventually create the same problems.

Instead, they want to build new societies based on independence, strength, and alliances that do not depend on the old order.

This philosophy eventually develops into the **Duskward Pact**.

Its strongest supporters include orcs, Beastkin, and Revenants.

---

# Formation of the Dawnbound Concord

### ~150 years ago

The surviving leaders of several human, elven, and dwarven territories begin formal negotiations.

Their goal is to establish cooperation between the surviving civilizations and coordinate the rebuilding of old territories.

The **Dawnbound Concord** is established.

Solmere becomes its political center.

The Concord begins sponsoring reconstruction, exploration, trade, and the recovery of old knowledge.

Its influence expands rapidly.

Regional powers retain their independence while aligning themselves with the Concord.

---

# Formation of the Duskward Pact

### ~120 years ago

Opposition to the Concord's restorationist philosophy grows.

Orc clans, Beastkin groups, Revenant communities, and independent territories begin forming alliances outside the old political structure.

The **Duskward Pact** is eventually established.

Ashka Vor becomes its political center.

The Pact rejects the idea that Elysium should simply return to the world that existed before the Sundering.

Instead, it promotes independence and the construction of new political systems.

---

# The Early Age of Reclamation

### ~100 years ago – present

The world begins changing again.

The separated continents gradually move closer together.

Previously inaccessible regions become reachable.

Ancient roads emerge from the wilderness.

Ruins become accessible.

Old magical sites begin activating.

Ships begin crossing routes that had been impossible for centuries.

The first organized expeditions into forgotten territories begin.

This period becomes known as the **Age of Reclamation**.

---

# The Reconnection

The reconnection of the continents changes Elysium permanently.

Trade becomes possible between cultures that have not interacted for centuries.

Old enemies encounter one another.

Lost languages are heard again.

Ancient technologies are rediscovered.

Territories that had been isolated since the Sundering suddenly become strategically important.

The Dawnbound Concord sees the reconnection as proof that the old world can be restored.

The Duskward Pact sees it as an opportunity to build something entirely new.

Tensions between the two sides increase.

---

# The Return of Magic

As the continents reconnect, ancient magical forces begin resurfacing.

Artifacts thought to be inert become active.

Old spells return.

Magical creatures emerge from previously inaccessible regions.

Corrupted zones begin expanding.

Scholars debate the cause.

Some believe the world's magical structure is repairing itself.

Some believe the gods are returning.

Some believe Kaelgorath is becoming stronger.

The truth remains unknown.

---

# The Present Day

### Current Age of Reclamation

Elysium stands at the beginning of a new era.

The major continents are once again becoming accessible.

The Dawnbound Concord and Duskward Pact compete for influence.

Regional kingdoms and factions attempt to protect their independence.

Neutral organizations operate between the two major powers.

Adventurers explore ruins that have remained untouched since the Sundering.

Scholars search for lost knowledge.

Merchants reopen ancient trade routes.

The old gods remain silent.

Kaelgorath remains scattered.

And the forces that caused the Sundering may not be as distant as the people of Elysium believe.

This is where the player's story begins.

The world is already moving.

The player enters it during a period of political tension, exploration, discovery, and growing supernatural activity.

---

# Current Historical Questions

Several major questions remain unanswered.

### What actually happened during the Sundering?

Most surviving records describe Kaelgorath as the sole cause.

But surviving evidence suggests the final conflict may have been more complicated.

### Why are the continents reconnecting?

No surviving scholar has established a definitive explanation.

### Why is magic returning?

The increase in magical activity may be connected to the reconnection of the continents.

It may also be connected to Kaelgorath.

### Are the gods truly silent?

No confirmed divine communication has occurred since the Sundering.

But unexplained miracles and divine relic activity continue to occur.

### Where are Kaelgorath's fragments?

His influence remains in corrupted regions.

The full extent of his remaining power is unknown.

### What was lost during the Age of Concord?

Ancient ruins continue to reveal technologies, civilizations, and histories that were forgotten during the Age of Ash.

Some discoveries may challenge the accepted history of Elysium.

# Timeline System

Elysium uses historical phases to preserve major events without deleting older versions of the world.

The world can contain locations, characters, factions, and objects that existed differently during different historical periods.

For example:

```text
Aurelia

Age of Dawn
      │
      ▼
Early Civilization
      │
      ▼
Age of Concord
      │
      ▼
The Sundering
      │
      ▼
Age of Ash
      │
      ▼
Age of Reclamation
      │
      ▼
Present Day
```

Historical content may allow players to experience important events from different points in Elysium's history.

Past events should remain consistent with established lore.

When a historical event changes the state of the world, the modern version of that location should reflect the consequences rather than replacing the history entirely.
