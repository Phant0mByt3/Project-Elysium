# 39 — NPCs

## Categories

**Quest Givers** — NPCs offering quests; every quest-giving NPC must have a name, a home location, and at least one line of flavor dialogue beyond the quest text itself, per [1404-NPC-Writing-Guide.md](../1400-Development/1404-NPC-Writing-Guide.md).

**Merchants & Trainers** — vendors, class trainers, and profession trainers. Functional NPCs, but still name-and-flavor-required to avoid breaking immersion (Pillar 1/2, [0002-Core-Pillars.md](../0000-Project/0002-Core-Pillars.md)).

**Rulers & Leaders** — named political figures tied to kingdoms ([0205-Kingdoms.md](0205-Kingdoms.md)) and factions ([0203-Factions.md](0203-Factions.md)), typically appearing in main or side story questlines rather than as static vendors.

**Companions** — recurring named NPCs who travel with or repeatedly assist the player through a questline; central to side stories ([0208-Side-Stories.md](0208-Side-Stories.md)).

**Enemies (Named)** — unique hostile NPCs with names and specific mechanics, distinct from generic mob types; includes dungeon/raid bosses ([0106-Dungeons.md](../0100-World/0106-Dungeons.md), [0107-Raids.md](../0100-World/0107-Raids.md)) and world bosses ([0108-World-Bosses.md](../0100-World/0108-World-Bosses.md)).

## Key Launch NPCs (examples)

**High Councilor Aldwin Rae** — leader of the Concord Council in Solmere, primary Dawnbound-side main story contact.

**Warmother Ushka Dren** — leader of the Ashenclaw Warcouncil, primary Duskward-side main story contact.

**Scholar Peren Vale** — Ashen Circle researcher who guides the player through Act II and III of the main story ([0207-Main-Story.md](0207-Main-Story.md)).

## Standards
Every named NPC introduced in a quest should be added to this document with their role, location, and affiliated questline so writers can track consistency across the growing cast.
