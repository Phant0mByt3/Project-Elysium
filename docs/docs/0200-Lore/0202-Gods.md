# 0202 — Gods

## The Elysian Circle

Seven gods who shaped Elysium and its founding races during the Age of Dawn ([0201-Timeline.md](../0200-Lore/0201-Timeline.md)).

None have spoken directly to mortals since the Sundering, though their influence persists through relics, shrines, religious orders, and the traditions of the six major classes ([0300-Classes.md](../0300-Characters/0300-Classes.md)).

The gods are not simply patrons of individual classes. Their domains influence the philosophies, abilities, cultures, and traditions associated with different classes.

### Class Traditions

The six major classes each draw inspiration from different parts of the Circle.

**Vanguard** — traditionally associated with Solthar, Terravox, and Ignareth. Vanguards represent strength, protection, endurance, and the willingness to stand between civilization and danger.

**Arcanist** — associated with Maelithir, Ignareth, and Voranthe. Arcanists study the forces that shape Elysium, from elemental power to the boundaries between life and death.

**Shade** — associated primarily with Nyxara and Voranthe. Shades study secrecy, shadow, fate, and the unseen forces that influence events from beyond ordinary sight.

**Warden** — primarily associated with Aeloria. Wardens protect the natural world and maintain the balance between civilization and the wilderness.

**Oathkeeper** — primarily associated with Solthar and Terravox. Oathkeepers bind themselves to a cause, creed, or sacred promise and are traditionally viewed as protectors of their people.

**Wayfarer** — associated with Nyxara, Maelithir, and Aeloria. Wayfarers represent exploration, freedom, survival, and the pursuit of places beyond the boundaries of known civilization.

The relationship between a class and its associated gods is cultural rather than mechanical. Players are not required to worship the god associated with their class.

# The Fallen God — Kaelgorath, the Sundered One

Once an eighth member of the Circle, Kaelgorath attempted to unmake Elysium out of resentment for mortal free will, triggering the Sundering.

Kaelgorath is not dead, but scattered and weakened. Fragments of his influence persist throughout corrupted zones and ancient ruins and are the source of many endgame threats.

His influence becomes increasingly important as players uncover the true nature of the Sundering, eventually leading into the climax of the [0107-Raids.md](../0100-World/0107-Raids.md) launch raid.

Kaelgorath has no recognized class tradition. His followers instead distort existing traditions and abilities through corruption.

## Design Use

Class flavor, cathedral and shrine architecture ([0103-Cities.md](../0100-World/0103-Cities.md)), religious orders, relics, dialogue, and holiday events ([0807-Seasons.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0807-Seasons.md)) should draw from this pantheon consistently.

The gods should feel like active forces within Elysium's history even though they have remained silent since the Sundering.

No new gods should be introduced without updating this document.
