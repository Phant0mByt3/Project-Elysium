# 40 — Classes

## Overview
Elysium launches with eight classes, each tied thematically to one or more gods of the Elysian Circle ([32-Gods.md](32-Gods.md)). Every class can fill at least one of three roles — Tank, Healer, or Damage — with specializations ([41-Specializations.md](41-Specializations.md)) determining exactly which.

## Launch Classes

| Class | Role(s) | Patron God | Fantasy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warrior** | Tank / Damage | Ignareth | Frontline melee, weapon mastery |
| **Paladin** | Tank / Healer | Solthar | Holy warrior, protective magic |
| **Rogue** | Damage | Nyxara | Stealth, precision strikes |
| **Ranger** | Damage | Aeloria | Ranged combat, beast companions |
| **Mage** | Damage | — (arcane, not tied to the Circle) | Elemental and arcane spellcasting |
| **Necromancer** | Damage | Voranthe | Death magic, minion summoning |
| **Cleric** | Healer | Solthar / Aeloria | Direct and restorative holy magic |
| **Druid** | Healer / Damage | Aeloria | Shapeshifting, nature magic |

## Design Philosophy
Every class must be viable solo (for questing) and have a clear group role at max level. Class identity should be readable at a glance in combat — see [44-Combat.md](44-Combat.md) for how visual and mechanical identity are kept distinct between classes.

## Progression
Skills unlock through leveling ([42-Skills.md](42-Skills.md)); build customization happens through talent trees ([43-Talent-Trees.md](43-Talent-Trees.md)). Class balance is an ongoing process documented in [49-Balance.md](49-Balance.md).

Race does not restrict class choice — any race may play any class, per [34-Races.md](34-Races.md).
