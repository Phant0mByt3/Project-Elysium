# 994-Ancient-Mysteries.md

## Purpose

This document contains unresolved mysteries, forgotten secrets, hidden locations, and unexplained events within the world of Elysium.

These mysteries may exist in the game for years before their true purpose is revealed. They are designed to create speculation, theories, exploration, and long-term player curiosity.

---

## Mystery Structure

Each mystery should contain:

* Name
* Location
* First Appearance
* Current Known Information
* Hidden Truth
* Future Reveal
* Connected Storylines

---

# Mystery Categories

## Lost Civilizations

Ancient kingdoms and societies that disappeared without explanation.

Examples:

* Forgotten empires
* Destroyed cities
* Missing civilizations
* Ancient technologies

---

## Sealed Locations

Areas that exist but cannot currently be accessed.

Examples:

* Locked temples
* Ancient gates
* Forbidden ruins
* Hidden dimensions

---

## Unknown Entities

Characters, creatures, or forces whose true identities are hidden.

Examples:

* Mysterious travellers
* Unknown gods
* Forgotten heroes
* Ancient beings

---

## Historical Events

Events from the past that are not fully understood.

Examples:

* Ancient wars
* Lost battles
* World-changing disasters
* Forgotten ages

---

# Design Rule

Every mystery should eventually have a purpose.

A mystery can remain unanswered for a long time, but it should never exist without a planned meaning.

# 998-Long-Term-Story.md

## Purpose

This document contains the complete long-term direction of Project Elysium.

It describes where the story can go across years of updates and expansions.

---

# Story Structure

## The First Age

The launch storyline.

Focus:

* Introducing the world
* Establishing factions
* Building player identity

---

## The Second Age

The first major expansion era.

Focus:

* Expanding the known world
* Revealing hidden mysteries
* Introducing larger conflicts

---

## The Third Age

The world begins changing permanently.

Focus:

* Major wars
* Fallen kingdoms
* New civilizations

---

## The Fourth Age

The conflict expands beyond the original world.

Focus:

* Other realms
* Ancient powers
* Unknown civilizations

---

## The Final Age

The conclusion of a major story cycle.

Focus:

* The biggest threats
* The fate of the world
* The legacy of players

---

# Long-Term Principles

* The world should continue evolving.
* Previous events should have consequences.
* Player achievements should remain meaningful.
* Future expansions should connect to past content.
* No story should make the previous world irrelevant.

---

# The Endless Journey

Project Elysium is designed as a living world.

There is no true "end" of the adventure.

Every victory creates a new question.

Every discovery reveals something unknown.

Every legend becomes part of history.
