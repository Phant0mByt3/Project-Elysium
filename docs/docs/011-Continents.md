# 11 — Continents

## Overview

Elysium is made up of five continents, two of which are available at launch. Each continent has a distinct theme, climate, dominant races, and level range.

## Aurelia *(Launch — Levels 1–30)*
The human heartland and the game's starting continent. Temperate plains, rolling farmland, and the capital city of **Solmere** (see [013-Cities.md](013-Cities.md)). Home to the Dawnbound Concord's seat of power. New characters of every race begin their journey in Aurelia's southern shires before the story sends them further afield.

## Vethmoor *(Launch — Levels 25–50)*
A continent of snow-capped highlands, deep mines, and fortified holds, shared by the Dwarven clans and Orcish tribes. Politically split between Dawnbound-aligned dwarven holds and Duskward-aligned orc clans, making it the primary early PvP frontier (see [086-Territory-Control.md](086-Territory-Control.md)).

## Sylvaneth *(Planned — Expansion 1)*
The elven forest continent — ancient, vertical, and half-swallowed by an enormous world-tree canopy. See [150-Expansion-01.md](150-Expansion-01.md).

## Kharzul Wastes *(Planned — Expansion 2)*
A scorched desert continent of beastkin nomad clans and buried pre-Sundering ruins. See [151-Expansion-02.md](151-Expansion-02.md).

## Nightreach *(Planned — Expansion 3, endgame-focused)*
A continent still visibly warped by the Sundering, home to Kaelgorath's remaining corruption and the Revenant race's origin. See [152-Expansion-03.md](152-Expansion-03.md).

## Structure Per Continent
Each continent document (this file's per-continent sections, expanding over time) should define: theme, climate, level range, dominant faction presence, major cities, and its role in the main story. Regional-level detail belongs in [012-Regions.md](012-Regions.md).
