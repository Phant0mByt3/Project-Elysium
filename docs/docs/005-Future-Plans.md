# 05 — Future Plans

A holding area for long-term ideas that are agreed upon in direction but not yet scheduled on the [003-Roadmap.md](003-Roadmap.md). Once an idea here is scoped and prioritized, it graduates into an actual expansion document (see [150-Expansion-01.md](150-Expansion-01.md) and onward). Raw, unscoped brainstorming lives in [999-Ideas.md](999-Ideas.md) instead — this file is for ideas the team has already committed to in principle.

## Confirmed Future Directions

* **Continent 3 — Sylvaneth**: the elven forest continent, planned as the first major post-launch expansion.
* **Continent 4 — Kharzul Wastes**: a desert continent centered on beastkin nomad culture and ancient ruins.
* **Continent 5 — Nightreach**: the corrupted continent tied to Kaelgorath, the Sundered One — intended as an endgame-focused expansion (see [036-History.md](036-History.md)).
* **Player Housing Neighborhoods**: guild-owned housing districts, expanding on [090-Housing.md](090-Housing.md).
* **Cross-Faction Events**: limited-time events where the Dawnbound Concord and Duskward Pact cooperate against a shared threat.
* **A Third Playable Faction**: a neutral, mercenary-aligned faction, gated behind reputation rather than race.
* **Player Ships**: naval travel and ship customization, extending [020-Travel.md](020-Travel.md).

## Review Cadence

This document should be revisited at the start of every expansion planning cycle, alongside [004-Version-History.md](004-Version-History.md), to decide what graduates onto the active roadmap.
