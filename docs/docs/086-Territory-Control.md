# 86 — Territory Control

## Overview
Territory control is the large-scale, open-world PvP system governing contested regions of Vethmoor ([012-Regions.md](012-Regions.md)), where the Dawnbound Concord and Duskward Pact compete for control of strategic points.

## Mechanics
* Contested regions contain capturable objectives (outposts, resource nodes, waypoint shrines — see [021-Fast-Travel.md](021-Fast-Travel.md)).
* Holding an objective grants the controlling faction bonus vendor access, faster local fast-travel, and periodic reputation ([077-Factions-Reputation.md](077-Factions-Reputation.md)) rewards for participants.
* Control can shift through sustained player presence and defeating defending NPC garrisons plus any defending players.

## Design Rules
* Territory shifts should be meaningful but not punishing — losing control of a region should inconvenience a faction (locked shrines, reduced vendor access) without cutting off core progression content.
* Objectives should be spread out to avoid a single dominant guild instantly controlling an entire region; capture timers should encourage sustained presence over hit-and-run tagging.

## Narrative Tie-In
Territory shifts in Frostgate Approach and Ashenclaw Tundra are referenced narratively in the world events system ([019-World-Events.md](019-World-Events.md))'s "Ashenclaw Incursion," giving PvP outcomes a light narrative echo.
