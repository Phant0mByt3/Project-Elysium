# 0604 — Herbalism

**Category:** Professions
**Status:** Living Document

## 1. Overview

Herbalism gathers plant materials feeding primarily into Alchemy ([0605-Alchemy.md](0605-Alchemy.md)), with select herbs also used by Cooking for specialty recipes.

## 2. Gathering Method

Players harvest visible herb nodes scattered through the open world, particularly dense in forest, marsh, and grassland biomes. Certain rare herbs bloom only at specific times of day, tying Herbalism into the day/night cycle ([0115-Day-Night-Cycle.md](../0100-World/0115-Day-Night-Cycle.md)).

## 3. Material Tiers by Region

| Region | Herb Pool |
| --- | --- |
| Southern Shires | Common healing herbs |
| Wildwood Reach | Restorative and mild toxin herbs |
| Greywater Fens | Rare marsh herbs, some tied to undead-resistance potions |
| Sunspire Hills | Sun-blessed herbs used in Concord-aligned recipes |
| Ashenclaw Tundra | Frost-hardy herbs for cold-resistance potions |

## 4. Skill Progression

Herbalism skill increases access to higher-tier herb nodes, mirroring the shared profession leveling curve in [0611-Profession-Progression.md](0611-Profession-Progression.md).

## 5. Night-Blooming Herbs

A subset of rare herbs only spawn during the in-game night cycle, rewarding dedicated herbalists who plan gathering routes around the day/night cycle rather than gathering passively while questing.

## 6. Economic Role

Herbalism is one of the most consistently profitable gathering professions due to Alchemy's steady demand for combat and utility consumables — see [0507-Consumables.md](../0500-Items/0507-Consumables.md) and [1000-Economy.md](../1000-Economy/1000-Economy.md).
