## 1. Overview

Alchemy transforms herbs gathered via Herbalism ([0604-Herbalism.md](0604-Herbalism.md)) into potions, elixirs, and flasks — the primary combat consumable category described in [0507-Consumables.md](../0500-Items/0507-Consumables.md).

## 2. Recipe Categories

* **Healing Potions** — restore health, core leveling and dungeon consumable.
* **Resource Potions** — restore mana/energy/rage depending on class resource type.
* **Combat Elixirs** — temporary stat buffs for a single stat category.
* **Flasks** — longer-duration, stronger buffs typically used before raid pulls.
* **Utility Potions** — resistance potions, cleanse effects, and other non-combat-power effects.

## 3. Crafting Process

Alchemy recipes are brewed at an alchemy station (available in most cities and some villages), consuming herb materials and, for higher-tier recipes, rare byproducts from other professions or drop sources.

## 4. Skill Progression and Recipe Access

Alchemy skill gates access to higher-tier recipes, taught by trainers, discovered through experimentation-style discovery quests, or found as rare drops from specific dungeon and raid bosses — see [0611-Profession-Progression.md](0611-Profession-Progression.md) and [0614-Profession-Recipes.md](0614-Profession-Recipes.md).

## 5. Economic Role

Alchemy is one of the most consistently active professions in the Auction House economy due to the renewable, consumed nature of its products — see [1010-Currency-Sinks.md](../1000-Economy/1010-Currency-Sinks.md).

## 6. Specializations

At higher skill tiers, Alchemists may specialize toward Elixir Mastery (stronger buff potions) or Transmutation (converting materials between types), detailed in [0615-Profession-Specialisations.md](0615-Profession-Specialisations.md).
