# 0603 — Fishing

## 1. Overview

Fishing is the most relaxed, low-stakes gathering profession, feeding almost exclusively into Cooking ([0607-Cooking.md](0607-Cooking.md)) while also offering its own minor collectible and vanity rewards independent of crafting.

## 2. Gathering Method

Players cast a line at any body of water and wait for a bite, represented by a simple timing-based minigame. Different water types (rivers, lakes, coastal ocean, swamp water) yield different fish pools.

## 3. Fishing Locations

| Water Type | Region Examples | Fish Pool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rivers | Southern Shires | Common freshwater fish |
| Marsh Water | Greywater Fens | Fen-adapted, occasionally rare mutated fish |
| Coastal Ocean | Solmere Docks | Saltwater fish, occasional treasure catches |
| Mountain Lakes | Vethmoor highlands | Cold-water fish, rare ingredients for high-tier recipes |

## 4. Treasure Catches

A small chance on any cast yields a "treasure catch" instead of fish — a minor currency reward, a cosmetic fishing pole skin, or rarely a piece of lost equipment, giving Fishing a light exploration-adjacent reward loop even for players not pursuing Cooking seriously.

## 5. Skill Progression

Fishing skill affects catch rate and access to rarer fish pools rather than gating locations outright, keeping the profession approachable and low-pressure relative to combat-adjacent gathering professions — see [0611-Profession-Progression.md](0611-Profession-Progression.md).

## 6. Social and Ambient Role

Fishing spots near cities and villages are deliberately placed as pleasant, low-stakes social areas, supporting casual multiplayer interaction that doesn't require group formation or combat readiness.
