# 0603 — Fishing

## Overview
Fishing is a relaxed, secondary gathering profession available to all characters regardless of their two chosen main professions — a low-stakes activity feeding into Cooking ([0607-Cooking.md](0607-Cooking.md)) and providing occasional unique rewards.

## Core Loop
Cast a line at any body of water → catch fish (and occasionally junk items or rare finds) → use catches as Cooking ingredients or sell on the Auction House.

## Design Notes
* Fishing pools should be placed intentionally near landmarks and scenic overlooks ([0105-Landmarks.md](../0100-World/0105-Landmarks.md)) to reward exploration.
* Rare catches (unique fish, message-in-a-bottle style items, occasional cosmetic rewards) give fishing a light "gacha" appeal without requiring deep system investment.
* Regional fishing pools should reflect local biome theming — Greywater Fens catches should feel different from Ironpeak Hold's underground cave fishing.

## Relationship to Other Systems
Unlike the four other gathering professions, Fishing does not occupy one of a character's two profession slots — see [0600-Professions.md](0600-Professions.md) for the full profession-slot rules.
