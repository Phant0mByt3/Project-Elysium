## 1. Overview

Professions are Elysium's crafting and gathering progression systems, existing alongside — not competing with — combat leveling ([0305-Leveling.md](../0300-Characters/0305-Leveling.md)). Every character may learn up to two professions.

## 2. Categories

* **Gathering Professions** — Mining ([0601-Mining.md](0601-Mining.md)), Woodcutting ([0602-Woodcutting.md](0602-Woodcutting.md)), Fishing ([0603-Fishing.md](0603-Fishing.md)), Herbalism ([0604-Herbalism.md](0604-Herbalism.md)).
* **Production Professions** — Alchemy ([0605-Alchemy.md](0605-Alchemy.md)), Blacksmithing ([0606-Blacksmithing.md](0606-Blacksmithing.md)), Cooking ([0607-Cooking.md](0607-Cooking.md)), Jewelcrafting ([0608-Jewelcrafting.md](0608-Jewelcrafting.md)), Tailoring ([0609-Tailoring.md](0609-Tailoring.md)), Leatherworking ([0610-Leatherworking.md](0610-Leatherworking.md)).
* **Universal Profession** — Enchanting ([0509-Enchanting.md](../0500-Items/0509-Enchanting.md)), which every character can learn as an add-on regardless of their two main profession slots.

## 3. Two-Profession Rule

Most players pair one gathering and one production profession (e.g. Mining + Blacksmithing) for self-sufficiency, though pure-gathering or pure-production pairings are also viable, feeding directly into the Auction House economy ([1003-Auction-House.md](../1000-Economy/1003-Auction-House.md)).

## 5. Progression

Each profession has its own skill level, capped alongside the character level cap ([0305-Leveling.md](../0300-Characters/0305-Leveling.md)), trained through use and recipe/technique unlocks from trainers found in cities and villages ([0103-Cities.md](../0100-World/0103-Cities.md), [0104-Villages.md](../0100-World/0104-Villages.md)). See [0611-Profession-Progression.md](0611-Profession-Progression.md) for the detailed leveling curve.

## 6. Switching Professions

Players may drop and relearn professions at any time through a profession trainer, though skill level and unlocked recipes are lost on drop and must be regained — a deliberate friction to prevent frictionless profession-swapping to chase short-term economic opportunities.

## 7. Design Goals

* Give every player a meaningful non-combat progression track.
* Ensure the player economy is genuinely player-driven rather than vendor-dominated (see [1000-Economy.md](../1000-Economy/1000-Economy.md)).
* Tie profession content into world design — gathering nodes are placed intentionally per region and biome ([0113-Biomes.md](../0100-World/0113-Biomes.md)).

## 8. Post-Launch Expansion

New professions or profession specializations are a confirmed future direction — see [0005-Future-Plans.md](../0000-Project/0005-Future-Plans.md) — expected to accompany new continents as new materials and cultural crafting traditions are introduced.
