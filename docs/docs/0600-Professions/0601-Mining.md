## 1. Overview

Mining is the primary gathering profession feeding metal and gem materials into Blacksmithing ([0606-Blacksmithing.md](0606-Blacksmithing.md)) and Jewelcrafting ([0608-Jewelcrafting.md](0608-Jewelcrafting.md)).

## 2. Gathering Method

Players interact with visible ore node deposits scattered throughout the open world, using a mining tool with no equip requirement beyond having learned the profession. Nodes respawn on a per-region timer to keep supply flowing without allowing a single player to exhaust a zone.

## 3. Material Tiers by Region

| Region | Ore Tier |
| --- | --- |
| Southern Shires / Wildwood Reach | Copper, Tin |
| Greywater Fens / Sunspire Hills | Iron |
| Frostgate Approach / Ember Deeps | Silver, Mithril |
| Ashenclaw Tundra / Ironpeak Holds | Adamant |
| The Shattered Cairns | Sundered Ore (rare, corrupted-touched) |

## 4. Gem Nodes

Rich ore deposits occasionally yield raw gems alongside metal, feeding Jewelcrafting directly and making Mining an attractive pairing for players interested in accessory crafting.

## 5. Skill Progression

Mining skill increases with successful gathering attempts, gating access to higher-tier ore nodes until the appropriate skill threshold is reached — see [0611-Profession-Progression.md](0611-Profession-Progression.md) for the shared profession leveling curve.

## 6. World Design Integration

Ore node density and tier are planned during the region design brief stage ([0102-Regions.md](../0100-World/0102-Regions.md)) and placed during world generation ([0116-World-Generation.md](../0100-World/0116-World-Generation.md)), ensuring miners have a reason to explore every region rather than farming a single optimal zone.

## 7. Specializations

At higher skill tiers, players may specialize toward either Ore Refinement (bonus yield per node) or Prospecting (bonus gem yield), detailed further in [0615-Profession-Specialisations.md](0615-Profession-Specialisations.md).
