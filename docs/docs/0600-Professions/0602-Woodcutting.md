# 0602 — Woodcutting

**Category:** Professions
**Status:** Living Document
**Related:** [0600-Professions.md](0600-Professions.md) · [0609-Tailoring.md](0609-Tailoring.md)

---

## 1. Overview

Woodcutting gathers timber and specialty wood materials used primarily by weapon crafting (bow and staff hafts) and select Tailoring recipes requiring wooden components (loom frames, accessory trim).

## 2. Gathering Method

Players fell marked trees throughout forested regions using a woodcutting tool, yielding raw logs that must be processed before use in most recipes.

## 3. Material Tiers by Region

| Region | Wood Tier |
| --- | --- |
| Southern Shires | Ashwood |
| Wildwood Reach | Ironbark |
| Greywater Fens | Fenwood (water-resistant) |
| Sunspire Hills | Goldenoak |
| Vethmoor highlands | Frostpine |

## 4. Skill Progression

Woodcutting skill increases with successful gathering, gating access to denser forest regions' higher-tier wood — see [0611-Profession-Progression.md](0611-Profession-Progression.md).

## 5. Downstream Use

While no single production profession is dedicated purely to woodworking at launch, wood feeds into ranged weapon crafting recipes owned by Blacksmithing's broader weaponsmithing scope, and into select furniture/housing crafting recipes tied to [0900-Player-Systems/](../0900-Player-Systems/) housing content.

## 6. Future Expansion

Sylvaneth's canopy-forest biome (see [0101-Continents.md](../0100-World/0101-Continents.md)) is expected to introduce significantly deeper Woodcutting content, including its own dedicated Woodworking production profession, once that expansion enters world-building.
