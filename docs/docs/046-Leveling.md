# 46 — Leveling

## Level Range
Launch level cap: **50**. Character progression is intentionally front-loaded with skill unlocks ([042-Skills.md](042-Skills.md)) and back-loaded with talent choices ([043-Talent-Trees.md](043-Talent-Trees.md)), so early levels teach the class and late levels let players build it.

## Experience Sources
* Quest completion (primary source, especially through the 1–30 range).
* Dungeon clears ([016-Dungeons.md](016-Dungeons.md)).
* Exploration and landmark discovery ([015-Landmarks.md](015-Landmarks.md)).
* Minor experience from kills, intentionally secondary to avoid mindless grinding, per Pillar 5 in [002-Core-Pillars.md](002-Core-Pillars.md).

## Pacing
The leveling curve is tuned against the regional level ranges in [012-Regions.md](012-Regions.md) so that a player completing a region's available quests naturally arrives at the next region already at or near its minimum level — no filler grinding required to "catch up."

## Rewards at Key Levels
* **Level 10** — Specialization choice ([041-Specializations.md](041-Specializations.md)).
* **Level 15** — First mount ([091-Mounts.md](091-Mounts.md)).
* **Level 25** — Cross-continent travel unlocked (Aurelia → Vethmoor, per [037-Main-Story.md](037-Main-Story.md) Act I conclusion).
* **Level 50** — Level cap; dungeon Heroic mode, raid, and endgame reputation grinds unlock ([077-Factions-Reputation.md](077-Factions-Reputation.md)).

Future expansions will raise the level cap per their own documents (e.g. [150-Expansion-01.md](150-Expansion-01.md)).
