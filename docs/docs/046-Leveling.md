# 46 — Leveling

## Level Range
Launch level cap: **50**. Character progression is intentionally front-loaded with skill unlocks ([42-Skills.md](42-Skills.md)) and back-loaded with talent choices ([43-Talent-Trees.md](43-Talent-Trees.md)), so early levels teach the class and late levels let players build it.

## Experience Sources
* Quest completion (primary source, especially through the 1–30 range).
* Dungeon clears ([16-Dungeons.md](16-Dungeons.md)).
* Exploration and landmark discovery ([15-Landmarks.md](15-Landmarks.md)).
* Minor experience from kills, intentionally secondary to avoid mindless grinding, per Pillar 5 in [02-Core-Pillars.md](02-Core-Pillars.md).

## Pacing
The leveling curve is tuned against the regional level ranges in [12-Regions.md](12-Regions.md) so that a player completing a region's available quests naturally arrives at the next region already at or near its minimum level — no filler grinding required to "catch up."

## Rewards at Key Levels
* **Level 10** — Specialization choice ([41-Specializations.md](41-Specializations.md)).
* **Level 15** — First mount ([91-Mounts.md](91-Mounts.md)).
* **Level 25** — Cross-continent travel unlocked (Aurelia → Vethmoor, per [37-Main-Story.md](37-Main-Story.md) Act I conclusion).
* **Level 50** — Level cap; dungeon Heroic mode, raid, and endgame reputation grinds unlock ([77-Factions-Reputation.md](77-Factions-Reputation.md)).

Future expansions will raise the level cap per their own documents (e.g. [150-Expansion-01.md](150-Expansion-01.md)).
