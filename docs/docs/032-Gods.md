# 32 — Gods

## The Elysian Circle

Seven gods who shaped Elysium and its founding races during the Age of Dawn ([031-Timeline.md](031-Timeline.md)). None have spoken directly to mortals since the Sundering, though their influence persists through relics, shrines, and the classes tied to them (see [040-Classes.md](040-Classes.md)).

| God | Domain | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Solthar** | Light, the Sun | Patron of the Dawnbound Concord; worshipped in Solmere's Sunspire Cathedral. |
| **Nyxara** | Night, the Moon | Patron of shadow and secrecy; favored by rogues and the Duskward Pact's spies. |
| **Terravox** | Earth, Stone | Patron of dwarves and endurance; the Ironpeak Holds are built in his honor. |
| **Maelithir** | Sea, Storm | Patron of sailors and coastal Aurelia; ties into [018-World-Bosses.md](018-World-Bosses.md)'s Maelith's Herald. |
| **Ignareth** | Fire, the Forge | Patron of blacksmiths and orc clan-fires alike — one of the few gods honored by both factions. |
| **Voranthe** | Death, Fate | Patron of the dying and the Revenant race; associated with necromancer lore, though not necromancy itself. |
| **Aeloria** | Life, Nature | Patron of druids and healers; her silence since the Sundering is a recurring point of grief in regional questlines. |

## The Fallen God — Kaelgorath, the Sundered One

Once an eighth member of the Circle, Kaelgorath attempted to unmake Elysium out of resentment for mortal free will, triggering the Sundering. Not dead, but scattered and weakened — fragments of his influence persist in corrupted zones and are the source of many endgame threats, including the climax of the [017-Raids.md](017-Raids.md) launch raid.

## Design Use
Class flavor, cathedral/shrine architecture ([013-Cities.md](013-Cities.md)), and holiday events ([087-Seasons.md](087-Seasons.md)) should draw from this pantheon consistently. New gods should not be introduced without updating this document.
