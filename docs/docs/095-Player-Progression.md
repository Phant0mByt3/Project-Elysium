# 95 — Player Progression

## Overview
This document ties together the full arc of long-term player progression across systems, serving as a map between the more detailed docs covering each individual layer.

## Progression Layers
1. **Character Level** (1–50 at launch) — [046-Leveling.md](046-Leveling.md).
2. **Specialization & Talents** — [041-Specializations.md](041-Specializations.md), [043-Talent-Trees.md](043-Talent-Trees.md).
3. **Gear / Item Level** — [053-Loot.md](053-Loot.md) through [056-Relics.md](056-Relics.md).
4. **Profession Level** — [060-Professions.md](060-Professions.md).
5. **Reputation** — [076-Reputation.md](076-Reputation.md), [077-Factions-Reputation.md](077-Factions-Reputation.md).
6. **Achievements & Titles** — [074-Achievements.md](074-Achievements.md), [075-Titles.md](075-Titles.md).
7. **Collection Systems** — mounts, pets, cosmetics ([091-Mounts.md](091-Mounts.md), [092-Pets.md](092-Pets.md), [093-Cosmetics.md](093-Cosmetics.md)).
8. **Guild & Social Standing** — [080-Guilds.md](080-Guilds.md).

## Design Philosophy
No single layer should feel mandatory in isolation — a player who cares only about story and exploration should reach level cap satisfied, while a player chasing every layer (Mythic raid clears, Exalted reputation, full mount collection) should have months of goals beyond hitting max level. This layered structure is what allows Elysium to support the multi-year live service vision in [001-Vision.md](001-Vision.md) and [003-Roadmap.md](003-Roadmap.md).
