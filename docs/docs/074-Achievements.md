# 74 — Achievements

## Overview
Achievements are optional, permanently-tracked accomplishments rewarding exploration, mastery, and dedication beyond the core leveling/gearing loop — directly supporting Pillar 1 in [002-Core-Pillars.md](002-Core-Pillars.md).

## Categories
* **Exploration** — discovering every landmark in a region ([015-Landmarks.md](015-Landmarks.md)), fully revealing a continent's map.
* **Quests & Story** — completing every quest chain in a region, finishing the main story ([037-Main-Story.md](037-Main-Story.md)).
* **Combat & PvE** — dungeon/raid clears on specific difficulties, defeating world bosses ([018-World-Bosses.md](018-World-Bosses.md)).
* **PvP** — arena and territory control milestones ([085-Arenas.md](085-Arenas.md), [086-Territory-Control.md](086-Territory-Control.md)).
* **Collection** — mounts, pets, cosmetics collected ([091-Mounts.md](091-Mounts.md), [092-Pets.md](092-Pets.md), [093-Cosmetics.md](093-Cosmetics.md)).
* **Profession** — reaching max skill in a profession, crafting rare recipes.

## Rewards
Achievements should primarily reward titles ([075-Titles.md](075-Titles.md)) and cosmetics rather than power, keeping them a prestige/completionist system rather than a stealth power progression track.

## Design Rules
Every major system added to the game should be reviewed for at least one achievement tie-in as part of its design, rather than achievements being bolted on after the fact.
