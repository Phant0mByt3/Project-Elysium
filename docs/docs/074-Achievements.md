# 74 — Achievements

## Overview
Achievements are optional, permanently-tracked accomplishments rewarding exploration, mastery, and dedication beyond the core leveling/gearing loop — directly supporting Pillar 1 in [02-Core-Pillars.md](02-Core-Pillars.md).

## Categories
* **Exploration** — discovering every landmark in a region ([15-Landmarks.md](15-Landmarks.md)), fully revealing a continent's map.
* **Quests & Story** — completing every quest chain in a region, finishing the main story ([37-Main-Story.md](37-Main-Story.md)).
* **Combat & PvE** — dungeon/raid clears on specific difficulties, defeating world bosses ([18-World-Bosses.md](18-World-Bosses.md)).
* **PvP** — arena and territory control milestones ([85-Arenas.md](85-Arenas.md), [86-Territory-Control.md](86-Territory-Control.md)).
* **Collection** — mounts, pets, cosmetics collected ([91-Mounts.md](91-Mounts.md), [92-Pets.md](92-Pets.md), [93-Cosmetics.md](93-Cosmetics.md)).
* **Profession** — reaching max skill in a profession, crafting rare recipes.

## Rewards
Achievements should primarily reward titles ([75-Titles.md](75-Titles.md)) and cosmetics rather than power, keeping them a prestige/completionist system rather than a stealth power progression track.

## Design Rules
Every major system added to the game should be reviewed for at least one achievement tie-in as part of its design, rather than achievements being bolted on after the fact.
