# 22 — Maps

This document is the staging area for world maps, regional layouts, and navigation planning references used by the world-building team before construction begins.

## Purpose
* Provide a top-down reference for continent and region boundaries before any building starts.
* Track the relative scale and positioning of regions within a continent (see [12-Regions.md](12-Regions.md)).
* Serve as the source for in-game minimap and world map assets, produced by the art team ([130-Art-Style.md](130-Art-Style.md)).

## Current Maps

**Aurelia — Regional Layout (draft)**
A rough compass-oriented sketch: the Southern Shires anchor the south, Wildwood Reach and the Greywater Fens sit centrally west and east of Solmere, and the Sunspire Hills form the northern approach to Vethmoor's ferry crossing.

**Vethmoor — Regional Layout (draft)**
Frostgate Approach forms the southern entry point from Aurelia; the Ember Deeps sit below Ironpeak Hold; Ashenclaw Tundra spans the north; the Shattered Cairns occupy the continent's northern coast, closest to the still-unmapped waters toward Nightreach.

## Process
1. Concept sketch (this document).
2. Scale pass against the leveling curve ([46-Leveling.md](46-Leveling.md)) to confirm region sizing.
3. Handoff to world-building team for construction (Phase 3, [03-Roadmap.md](03-Roadmap.md)).
4. Final in-game map art produced once a region is content-complete.

Actual image/schematic map files, once produced, should be linked from this document rather than duplicated elsewhere.
