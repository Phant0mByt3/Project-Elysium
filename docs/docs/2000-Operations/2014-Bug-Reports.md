# 2014 — Bug Reports

**Category:** Operations
**Status:** Living Document

## 1. Overview

This document covers the live-service-facing side of player-submitted bug reports, complementing the internal development bug tracking process described in [1407-Bug-Tracking.md](../1400-Development/1407-Bug-Tracking.md).

## 2. Submission Channels

Players can submit bug reports through an in-client tool (automatically attaching relevant context like location and recent actions), the support system ([2005-Support-System.md](2005-Support-System.md)), or community channels, all funneling into the same triage pipeline.

## 3. Triage Process

Player-submitted reports are checked for duplicates against the existing tracker, categorized by severity per [1407-Bug-Tracking.md](../1400-Development/1407-Bug-Tracking.md), and prioritized alongside internally discovered issues rather than in a separate queue.

## 4. Player Communication

Where a reported bug is confirmed and scheduled for a fix, the reporting player may receive acknowledgment (without a guaranteed timeline commitment, since fix complexity varies), and confirmed fixes are reflected in patch notes ([2002-Patch-Notes.md](2002-Patch-Notes.md)) once shipped.

## 5. Exploit Reports

Bug reports involving potential exploits (particularly economy-affecting ones) are handled with elevated urgency and confidentiality, avoiding public disclosure of exploit details until a fix has shipped, coordinated with [1206-Security.md](../1200-Technical/1206-Security.md).

## 6. Recognition

Players who report significant, previously-unknown bugs — particularly exploits reported responsibly rather than abused — may be recognized through the community channels described in [2004-Community-Management.md](2004-Community-Management.md), encouraging a healthy, cooperative relationship between the community and the development team around bug discovery.
