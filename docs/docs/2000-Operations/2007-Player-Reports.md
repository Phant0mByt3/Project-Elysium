# 2007 — Player Reports

**Project:** Elysium MMORPG
**Category:** Operations
**Status:** Living Document
**Related:** [2003-Moderation.md](2003-Moderation.md) · [2005-Support-System.md](2005-Support-System.md)

---

## 1. Overview

The in-client player reporting system lets players flag other players for chat violations, inappropriate names, suspected cheating, or exploitative behavior, feeding directly into the moderation pipeline ([2003-Moderation.md](2003-Moderation.md)).

## 2. Report Categories

* **Chat/Harassment** — abusive or inappropriate communication.
* **Inappropriate Name** — character, guild, or title names violating [2017-Community-Guidelines.md](2017-Community-Guidelines.md).
* **Suspected Cheating** — botting, exploiting, or other unfair advantage-seeking behavior.
* **Suspected RMT** — real-money trading solicitation or evidence.

## 3. Report Submission

Reports can be submitted directly from chat, a player's profile, or after a group content session, automatically attaching relevant context (recent chat log excerpt, location, timestamp) to speed up review without requiring the reporting player to manually describe everything.

## 4. Anti-Abuse Safeguards

The reporting system itself includes safeguards against abuse — repeated unfounded reports from a single account are flagged, and report volume alone does not automatically trigger action without human or automated-with-high-confidence review, protecting players from coordinated false-report harassment.

## 5. Reporter Feedback

Reporters receive a general acknowledgment that their report was received and reviewed, without disclosing the specific action taken against the reported player (consistent with the privacy principle in [2003-Moderation.md](2003-Moderation.md)), balancing transparency with the reported player's privacy.

## 6. Integration with Anti-Cheat

Suspected cheating reports are cross-referenced against the automated detection systems in [1207-Anti-Cheat.md](../1200-Technical/1207-Anti-Cheat.md), with player reports serving as a valuable human-sourced signal that complements automated detection.
