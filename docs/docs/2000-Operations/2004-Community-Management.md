## 1. Overview

Community management covers external-facing communication: announcements, social channels, community events, and the general health of the relationship between the development team and the player community.

## 2. Channels

* **In-Client** — news feed, patch notes ([2002-Patch-Notes.md](2002-Patch-Notes.md)), main menu announcements ([1114-Main-Menu.md](../1100-Client/1114-Main-Menu.md)).
* **Forums/Discord** — primary hub for community discussion, feedback threads, and developer communication.
* **Social Media** — broader-reach announcements, marketing beats, and community highlights.
* **Companion Website** — patch note archive, status page ([1216-Monitoring.md](../1200-Technical/1216-Monitoring.md)), leaderboards ([0808-Leaderboards.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0808-Leaderboards.md)).

## 3. Communication Principles

* Timely — significant news (outages, major changes) is communicated as soon as accurate information is available, not delayed for polish.
* Honest — setbacks, delays, and mistakes are acknowledged directly rather than spun, building the long-term trust described in [2000-Live-Service.md](2000-Live-Service.md).
* Two-way — community management actively surfaces player feedback ([2013-Player-Feedback.md](2013-Player-Feedback.md)) back to the development team, not just broadcasts outward.

## 4. Developer Presence

Regular, scheduled touchpoints (developer Q&As, patch preview streams, written dev blogs) give the community direct access to the people building the game, reinforcing the small-team, passion-driven identity described in [0001-Vision.md](../0000-Project/0001-Vision.md).

## 5. Crisis Communication

For significant incidents (extended outages, major exploits, security issues), a documented crisis communication plan ensures consistent, accurate, and prompt messaging across all channels, coordinated with [1206-Security.md](../1200-Technical/1206-Security.md) and [2011-Server-Restarts.md](2011-Server-Restarts.md).

## 6. Community Event Support

Community management coordinates with the events team ([2009-Community-Events.md](2009-Community-Events.md)) to promote and support both developer-run and community-organized events, growing engagement beyond pure gameplay updates.
