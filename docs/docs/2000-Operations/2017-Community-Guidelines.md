# 2017 — Community Guidelines

**Project:** Elysium MMORPG
**Category:** Operations
**Status:** Living Document
**Related:** [2003-Moderation.md](2003-Moderation.md) · [2007-Player-Reports.md](2007-Player-Reports.md)

---

## 1. Overview

Community Guidelines define the standards of conduct expected from every Elysium player, forming the policy foundation that moderation enforcement ([2003-Moderation.md](2003-Moderation.md)) is built on.

## 2. Core Expectations

* Treat other players with basic respect — disagreement is fine, harassment is not.
* No hate speech, discrimination, or targeted harassment of any kind.
* No cheating, exploiting, or real-money trading.
* No inappropriate character, guild, or title names.
* No spam or advertising unrelated to the game.

## 3. Context-Sensitive Application

Guidelines are applied with reasonable judgment for context — heated competitive banter in an Arena match ([0805-Arenas.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0805-Arenas.md)) is treated differently from targeted harassment, though both are subject to the same underlying respect principle.

## 4. Guideline Accessibility

Guidelines are written in plain, direct language, easily accessible in-client and on community channels, and referenced during onboarding so new players understand expectations from their first session ([0409-Tutorial-System.md](../0400-Gameplay/0409-Tutorial-System.md)).

## 5. Guideline Evolution

Guidelines are revisited periodically as the community grows and new situations arise that the original guidelines didn't anticipate, with significant changes communicated clearly rather than silently updated.

## 6. Relationship to Enforcement

These guidelines are the policy; [2003-Moderation.md](2003-Moderation.md) covers how violations are detected and enforced, and [2007-Player-Reports.md](2007-Player-Reports.md) covers how players can report violations they encounter.
