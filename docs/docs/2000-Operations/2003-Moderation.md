## 1. Overview

Moderation covers enforcement of the community guidelines ([2017-Community-Guidelines.md](2017-Community-Guidelines.md)) across chat, names, guild content, and player conduct, working alongside the automated anti-cheat systems in [1207-Anti-Cheat.md](../1200-Technical/1207-Anti-Cheat.md).

## 2. Enforcement Categories

* **Chat/Communication** — harassment, hate speech, spam.
* **Names** — inappropriate character, guild, or title names.
* **Cheating/Exploiting** — handled jointly with the anti-cheat pipeline ([1207-Anti-Cheat.md](../1200-Technical/1207-Anti-Cheat.md)).
* **Real-Money Trading (RMT)** — unauthorized gold/item selling, detected partly through the economic monitoring in [1012-Player-Trading-Rules.md](../1000-Economy/1012-Player-Trading-Rules.md).

## 3. Reporting Pipeline

Player reports ([2007-Player-Reports.md](2007-Player-Reports.md)) feed into a triage queue reviewed by the moderation team, prioritized by severity and report volume, with automated pre-filtering catching the clearest cases before human review.

## 4. Escalation Tiers

| Tier | Example | Typical Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warning | First-time minor chat violation | In-client warning |
| Temporary Restriction | Repeated violations | Chat mute, temporary suspension |
| Extended Suspension | Serious or repeated violations | Multi-day/week account suspension |
| Permanent Ban | Severe violations, RMT, cheating | Permanent account action |

## 5. Staff Tooling

Moderation actions are performed through the admin tools described in [2008-Staff-Tools.md](2008-Staff-Tools.md) and [1214-Admin-Tools.md](../1200-Technical/1214-Admin-Tools.md), with every action logged for accountability.

## 6. Appeals

Players may appeal moderation actions through the support system ([2005-Support-System.md](2005-Support-System.md)), reviewed by a staff member who was not involved in the original action, ensuring a fair, independent second look.

## 7. Transparency

Moderation policy (though not individual case details, which remain private) is communicated openly through [2017-Community-Guidelines.md](2017-Community-Guidelines.md), so players understand what's expected of them and why enforcement happens.
