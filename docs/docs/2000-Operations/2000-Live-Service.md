## 1. Overview

The overarching model for running Elysium as a persistent online game: cadence of updates, support, community, and long-term health.

---

## 2. Principles

- Player trust and clarity come first  
- Processes are documented and repeatable  
- Handoffs between Ops, Support, and Development are explicit  
- Metrics inform decisions without replacing design judgment

## 3. Status

Detailed SOPs are expanded as the project approaches Closed Beta and Live Service. This document establishes ownership and intent for **Live Service**.
