## 1. Overview

Live Service is the overarching operating model for running Elysium as a persistent online game once it reaches Phase 6 (Public Release) and enters Phase 7 (Live Service) on the roadmap ([0003-Roadmap.md](../0000-Project/0003-Roadmap.md)). It ties together update cadence, community management, support, moderation, and analytics into one coherent operating rhythm.

## 2. Principles

* **Player trust and clarity come first** — players should always know what's happening, why, and when, whether that's a patch, an outage, or a moderation action.
* **Processes are documented and repeatable** — live operations should not depend on tribal knowledge held by a single person.
* **Handoffs between Operations, Support, and Development are explicit** — an issue discovered by Support should have a clear path to Development, and a Development fix should have a clear path back to player-facing communication.
* **Metrics inform decisions without replacing design judgment** — analytics ([2015-Game-Metrics.md](2015-Game-Metrics.md)) tell the team what's happening, not automatically what to do about it.

## 3. Live Service Team Structure

Live operations is staffed by a small dedicated team spanning community management ([2004-Community-Management.md](2004-Community-Management.md)), support ([2005-Support-System.md](2005-Support-System.md)), moderation ([2003-Moderation.md](2003-Moderation.md)), and a rotating on-call presence from the development team for technical incidents.

## 4. Weekly Operating Rhythm

A typical live-service week includes: monitoring dashboards and community sentiment daily, a weekly cross-team sync reviewing open issues and upcoming content, and a defined process for escalating anything urgent outside the normal weekly cadence — see [2018-Operations-Checklist.md](2018-Operations-Checklist.md) for the detailed checklist this rhythm is built from.

## 5. Content Cadence

Live service content follows the update structure in [2001-Updates.md](2001-Updates.md), seasonal structure in [2010-Seasonal-Events.md](2010-Seasonal-Events.md), and longer expansion cadence in [1500-Expansions/](../1500-Expansions/), giving players both a short-term and long-term sense of what's coming next.

## 6. Health Monitoring

Overall game health is tracked through a blend of technical metrics ([1216-Monitoring.md](../1200-Technical/1216-Monitoring.md)), player-facing metrics ([2015-Game-Metrics.md](2015-Game-Metrics.md), [2016-Player-Retention.md](2016-Player-Retention.md)), and qualitative community sentiment gathered through [2013-Player-Feedback.md](2013-Player-Feedback.md).

## 7. Status

Detailed standard operating procedures for each area under this umbrella are expanded and stress-tested as the project approaches Closed Beta ([0003-Roadmap.md](../0000-Project/0003-Roadmap.md), Phase 5) and finalized ahead of Public Release. This document establishes the ownership and intent for Live Service as a whole; the documents that follow in this folder cover each specific operational area in detail.
