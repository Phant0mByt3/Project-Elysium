## 1. Overview

Standards for writing clear, complete patch notes for every update ([2001-Updates.md](2001-Updates.md)), kept consistent with the internal changelog in [0004-Version-History.md](../0000-Project/0004-Version-History.md).

## 2. Structure

Every patch note entry follows a consistent structure: a short summary of the update's headline content, followed by categorized sections (New Content, Balance Changes, Bug Fixes, Known Issues), so players can quickly scan for what matters to them.

## 3. Writing Standards

* Plain, direct language — no internal jargon or ticket numbers without context.
* Balance changes state both the change and, briefly, the reasoning, building player trust in the design process rather than presenting changes as arbitrary.
* Known Issues are disclosed honestly rather than omitted, even when they reflect imperfectly on the release.

## 4. Categorization

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| New Content | Dungeons, quests, systems, cosmetics |
| Balance Changes | Class, item, economy tuning |
| Bug Fixes | Resolved issues from [2014-Bug-Reports.md](2014-Bug-Reports.md) |
| Known Issues | Disclosed, unresolved issues players may encounter |

## 5. Publication Channels

Patch notes are published in-client (accessible from the main menu, [1114-Main-Menu.md](../1100-Client/1114-Main-Menu.md)) and across community channels simultaneously with the update's deployment, coordinated through [2004-Community-Management.md](2004-Community-Management.md).

## 6. Archival

All patch notes are archived and cross-referenced against [0004-Version-History.md](../0000-Project/0004-Version-History.md), giving the community (and the team) a complete, searchable history of the game's evolution.
