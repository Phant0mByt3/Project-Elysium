## 1. Overview

This document defines the specific key metrics tracked to assess Elysium's overall health, building on the analytics infrastructure and principles described in [2006-Analytics.md](2006-Analytics.md).

## 2. Core Metric Categories

* **Population** — concurrent players, daily/monthly active users.
* **Engagement** — average session length, content completion rates (dungeons, raids, quests).
* **Progression** — leveling pace distribution, time-to-cap, gearing pace.
* **Economy Health** — currency supply growth, Auction House transaction volume, material prices ([1008-Economic-Balance.md](../1000-Economy/1008-Economic-Balance.md)).
* **Retention** — covered in detail in [2016-Player-Retention.md](2016-Player-Retention.md).

## 3. Metric Review Cadence

Core metrics are reviewed weekly by the live operations team, with a more thorough monthly review examining longer-term trends, feeding into the update planning process described in [2001-Updates.md](2001-Updates.md).

## 4. Content-Specific Metrics

Beyond the core metrics, individual content releases are tracked against launch-specific success criteria — a new dungeon's completion rate and average clear time, a new questline's completion percentage — informing whether similar content should be repeated or adjusted in future releases.

## 5. Metric Interpretation Discipline

Metrics are interpreted in context rather than in isolation — a drop in a specific metric might reflect a genuine problem, a seasonal playtime pattern, or a shift in where players are spending time within the game, and the team investigates before drawing conclusions, consistent with the "metrics inform, not replace, design judgment" principle in [2000-Live-Service.md](2000-Live-Service.md).

## 6. Dashboard Access

Relevant metrics are surfaced through role-appropriate dashboards — the full picture for leadership and analytics-focused roles, and a more focused view for individual discipline leads relevant to their area.
