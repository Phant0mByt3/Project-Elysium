## 1. Overview

This document covers the player-facing operational rhythm of server restarts, complementing the technical maintenance procedures described in [1230-Server-Maintenance.md](../1200-Technical/1230-Server-Maintenance.md).

## 2. Restart Types

* **Scheduled Weekly Restart** — aligned with weekly raid lockout and quest resets ([0107-Raids.md](../0100-World/0107-Raids.md), [0703-Weekly-Quests.md](../0700-Quests/0703-Weekly-Quests.md)), giving players a predictable weekly rhythm.
* **Patch Deployment Restart** — accompanies minor and major content patches ([2001-Updates.md](2001-Updates.md)).
* **Emergency Restart** — unscheduled, for critical stability or security issues.

## 3. Scheduling Philosophy

Scheduled restarts occur during a consistent, historically low-traffic window, communicated well in advance and displayed in-client via a countdown warning as the restart approaches, minimizing disruption to active play sessions.

## 4. Player Warning System

In-client warnings appear at decreasing intervals before a scheduled restart, giving players in dungeons or raids ([0106-Dungeons.md](../0100-World/0106-Dungeons.md)) enough notice to reach a safe stopping point rather than being abruptly disconnected mid-encounter.

## 5. Emergency Restart Protocol

Emergency restarts follow the protocol described in [1230-Server-Maintenance.md](../1200-Technical/1230-Server-Maintenance.md), with as much advance warning as the situation allows and prompt post-restart communication explaining what happened, per the crisis communication principles in [2004-Community-Management.md](2004-Community-Management.md).

## 6. Compensation Policy

Where an emergency restart causes meaningful player disruption (a lost raid lockout attempt, for example), a documented compensation policy determines appropriate remediation, applied consistently rather than case-by-case.
