## 1. Overview

This document covers how qualitative player feedback — forum posts, survey responses, in-client feedback tools — is collected, organized, and fed back into design decisions, complementing the quantitative data described in [2006-Analytics.md](2006-Analytics.md).

## 2. Feedback Channels

* **In-Client Feedback Tool** — a lightweight, always-available way to submit feedback without leaving the game.
* **Forums/Discord** — the primary space for detailed discussion and debate.
* **Surveys** — periodic, targeted surveys on specific systems or overall satisfaction.
* **PTR/Beta Feedback** — structured feedback collection during test periods ([1505-Expansion-Release-Strategy.md](../1500-Expansions/1505-Expansion-Release-Strategy.md)).

## 3. Feedback Categorization

Feedback is tagged by system (combat, professions, economy, etc.) and sentiment, allowing the team to identify recurring themes rather than reacting to the loudest individual voices — a small number of vocal complaints is weighed differently from a broad, consistent pattern of concern.

## 4. Balancing Feedback Against Design Vision

Feedback is one input among several (design pillars, analytics, playtesting) rather than an automatic mandate — a change is made because the team agrees it improves the game against [0002-Core-Pillars.md](../0000-Project/0002-Core-Pillars.md), informed by player feedback, not simply because enough people asked for it.

## 5. Closing the Loop

Where feasible, the team communicates back to the community when feedback directly informed a change, reinforcing that feedback is genuinely heard even when not every request results in an immediate change.

## 6. Sensitive Topic Handling

Feedback related to moderation actions, account issues, or other sensitive individual matters is routed to the support system ([2005-Support-System.md](2005-Support-System.md)) rather than handled through general feedback channels, protecting player privacy.
