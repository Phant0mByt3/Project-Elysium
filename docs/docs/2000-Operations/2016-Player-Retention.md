## 1. Overview

Player retention tracks how successfully Elysium keeps players engaged over time, from their first session through long-term veteran play, a critical health signal for a live-service MMORPG's long-term viability per [0001-Vision.md](../0000-Project/0001-Vision.md).

## 2. Retention Funnel Stages

* **New Player Onboarding** — first session through completing the tutorial ([0409-Tutorial-System.md](../0400-Gameplay/0409-Tutorial-System.md)).
* **Early Retention** — days 1, 7, and 30 return rates, a standard industry benchmark window.
* **Mid-Term Engagement** — leveling completion and first dungeon/raid participation.
* **Long-Term Retention** — sustained play across multiple content updates and seasons.

## 3. Onboarding Optimization

Because early-session experience strongly predicts long-term retention, the tutorial and early leveling experience ([0409-Tutorial-System.md](../0400-Gameplay/0409-Tutorial-System.md)) receives ongoing analytics-driven scrutiny for drop-off points, with iteration prioritized accordingly.

## 4. Return-Player Programs

For lapsed players, the return-player incentives described in [0915-Player-Rewards.md](../0900-Player-Systems/0915-Player-Rewards.md) and targeted "come back" communication (where a player has opted into such communication) aim to re-engage players without being intrusive.

## 5. Churn Signal Monitoring

Declining session frequency or engagement with a specific system for a segment of players is treated as an early churn signal, prompting investigation into whether a specific pain point (a difficulty spike, a confusing system) is driving the drop-off.

## 6. Long-Term Retention Levers

Beyond onboarding, sustained retention relies on the broader content cadence ([2001-Updates.md](2001-Updates.md)), seasonal structure ([0807-Seasons.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0807-Seasons.md)), and the long-term progression systems described in [0905-Player-Progression.md](../0900-Player-Systems/0905-Player-Progression.md) — retention is treated as an outcome of good game design across every system, not a standalone feature to bolt on.
