## 1. Overview

This document defines how content and system updates are planned, communicated, and shipped on a regular cadence once Elysium is live, building on the technical release process in [1408-Release-Process.md](../1400-Development/1408-Release-Process.md).

## 2. Update Categories

* **Hotfixes** — urgent, small-scope fixes for Critical issues ([1407-Bug-Tracking.md](../1400-Development/1407-Bug-Tracking.md)), shipped as soon as they're validated, outside the normal cadence.
* **Minor Patches** — balance changes, bug fixes, and quality-of-life improvements, on a predictable regular cycle.
* **Major Content Patches** — new dungeons, questlines, or systems, less frequent, aligned with the seasonal calendar ([0807-Seasons.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0807-Seasons.md)).
* **Expansions** — the largest update category, covered separately under [1500-Expansions/](../1500-Expansions/).

## 3. Planning Process

Update content is planned several cycles ahead, drawing from the roadmap ([0003-Roadmap.md](../0000-Project/0003-Roadmap.md)), player feedback ([2013-Player-Feedback.md](2013-Player-Feedback.md)), and analytics-driven priorities ([2015-Game-Metrics.md](2015-Game-Metrics.md)), balanced against the bug backlog from [2014-Bug-Reports.md](2014-Bug-Reports.md).

## 4. Communication

Every update, regardless of size, is accompanied by clear patch notes ([2002-Patch-Notes.md](2002-Patch-Notes.md)) and advance notice through community channels ([2004-Community-Management.md](2004-Community-Management.md)) proportional to its scope — a hotfix needs only a brief notice, while a major content patch warrants advance previews.

## 5. Update Cadence Targets

| Category | Target Cadence |
| --- | --- |
| Hotfix | As needed, no fixed schedule |
| Minor Patch | Every 2–3 weeks |
| Major Content Patch | Roughly every season (10–14 weeks) |
| Expansion | Roughly annually |

## 6. Post-Update Review

Every update is followed by a brief post-release review examining metrics ([2015-Game-Metrics.md](2015-Game-Metrics.md)), bug reports ([2014-Bug-Reports.md](2014-Bug-Reports.md)), and community sentiment, feeding lessons learned back into the planning process for the next cycle.
