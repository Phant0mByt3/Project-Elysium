# 30 — Lore

## Overview

Elysium's lore is built around a single defining event — **the Sundering** — that split the world's history into a clear before and after. This document is the entry point into the lore documentation; see [31-Timeline.md](31-Timeline.md) for the full chronology, [32-Gods.md](32-Gods.md) for the pantheon, [33-Factions.md](33-Factions.md) for political groups, [34-Races.md](34-Races.md) for playable and non-playable peoples, [35-Kingdoms.md](35-Kingdoms.md) for nations, and [36-History.md](36-History.md) for detailed historical events.

## The Short Version

Long ago, the seven gods of the **Elysian Circle** shaped the world of Elysium and shared it with mortals in an era remembered as the **Age of Concord** — a golden age of cooperation between races. An eighth god, **Kaelgorath**, grew envious of mortal free will and attempted to unmake the Circle's creation. The resulting battle — the Sundering — tore the world's continents apart, silenced the gods, and plunged civilization into the **Age of Ash**.

Eight centuries later, in the current **Age of Reclamation**, the drifting continents are slowly reconnecting, old magic is resurfacing, and mortal factions are rebuilding — some hoping to restore what was lost, others believing the old order should stay buried.

## Themes
* **Reconstruction vs. nostalgia** — should the world be rebuilt as it was, or into something new?
* **Faith without gods** — the Circle has been silent since the Sundering; religion in Elysium is now more about legacy than active divine contact.
* **Fragmentation and reunion** — mirrored at every scale, from the literal broken continents to broken factions, families, and player-facing storylines.
