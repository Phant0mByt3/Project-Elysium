# 113 — Custom Models

## Overview
Custom 3D models used throughout Elysium's resource pack ([112-Resource-Pack.md](112-Resource-Pack.md)), covering weapons, armor, NPCs, creatures, and world set-dressing beyond what vanilla Minecraft blocks can express.

## Categories
* **Character Models** — race-specific player model adjustments ([034-Races.md](034-Races.md)) and NPC models ([039-NPCs.md](039-NPCs.md)).
* **Creature Models** — enemy and world boss models ([018-World-Bosses.md](018-World-Bosses.md)), designed per-region to match local bestiary themes.
* **Item Models** — weapons, armor, and accessory models ([050-Weapons.md](050-Weapons.md) through [052-Accessories.md](052-Accessories.md)).
* **Environmental Models** — non-block set-dressing (statues, ruins detail, foliage) used to give handcrafted regions a level of detail beyond standard block palettes.

## Pipeline
Models are produced by the art team following the standards in [130-Art-Style.md](130-Art-Style.md) and [136-Models.md](136-Models.md), then integrated into the resource pack for distribution via the launcher.

## Technical Constraints
Model complexity and polycount budgets should be reviewed against the performance targets in [128-Performance.md](128-Performance.md), particularly for large-scale content like raids and world events where many models render simultaneously.
