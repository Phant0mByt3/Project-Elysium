# 150 — Expansion 01: Roots of Sylvaneth

## Overview
The first planned post-launch expansion, introducing the third continent, **Sylvaneth** — an ancient elven forest continent built around a single, colossal world-tree canopy ([11-Continents.md](11-Continents.md)).

## New Content
* **Continent** — Sylvaneth, level range 50–65 (raising the level cap, see [46-Leveling.md](46-Leveling.md)).
* **Race Update** — introduces Wood Elves as a distinct cultural branch of the existing Elf race lore ([34-Races.md](34-Races.md)), native to Sylvaneth.
* **New Dungeons & Raid** — a full new dungeon set and raid tier themed around the world-tree's canopy and roots.
* **New Profession Materials** — unique Sylvaneth timber and herbs extending Woodcutting and Herbalism ([62-Woodcutting.md](62-Woodcutting.md), [64-Herbalism.md](64-Herbalism.md)).
* **Story** — follows directly from the main story's Act III resolution ([37-Main-Story.md](37-Main-Story.md)), with Sylvaneth's elves revealed to hold knowledge about Aeloria's silence since the Sundering ([32-Gods.md](32-Gods.md)).

## Faction Impact
Sylvaneth introduces new faction outposts for both the Dawnbound Concord and Duskward Pact as they compete to form alliances with Sylvaneth's insular elven communities, extending [33-Factions.md](33-Factions.md) and [86-Territory-Control.md](86-Territory-Control.md) into a new continent.

## Status
Planned; not yet scheduled on the active roadmap. See [05-Future-Plans.md](05-Future-Plans.md) for graduation criteria.
