# 85 — Arenas

## Overview
Arenas are structured, instanced PvP matches (2v2 and 3v3 team formats at launch) offering a controlled, ranked alternative to open-world PvP ([084-PvP.md](084-PvP.md)).

## Format
* **2v2** — fast-paced, favors burst and mobility-heavy specs.
* **3v3** — the primary "ranked ladder" format, favoring more balanced team compositions.

## Ranking & Rewards
Seasonal ranked ladders reset periodically (see [087-Seasons.md](087-Seasons.md) for the seasonal calendar this aligns to), rewarding cosmetics ([093-Cosmetics.md](093-Cosmetics.md)) and titles ([075-Titles.md](075-Titles.md)) at top rating brackets rather than raw power items, consistent with [049-Balance.md](049-Balance.md)'s PvP/PvE parity goals.

## Design Rules
* Arena maps should be small, symmetrical, and free of the exploration/lore elements found elsewhere in the world ([015-Landmarks.md](015-Landmarks.md)) — arenas are a pure competitive space.
* Matchmaking should pair teams of similar rating; a placement series determines initial rating for new participants each season.

## Cross-Reference
Territory-based, large-scale PvP is a separate system — see [086-Territory-Control.md](086-Territory-Control.md).
