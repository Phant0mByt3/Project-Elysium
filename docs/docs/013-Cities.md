# 13 — Cities

Major cities are safe hubs containing vendors, trainers, the auction house, bank, and quest hubs. Unlike villages ([014-Villages.md](014-Villages.md)), cities are large, faction-aligned, and serve as respawn/recall points.

## Solmere — Capital of Aurelia
Seat of the Dawnbound Concord and the game's primary starting hub. Built around the **Sunspire Cathedral**, a monument to the god Solthar. Contains the Grand Auction House ([103-Auction-House.md](103-Auction-House.md)), the Adventurer's Guildhall (class trainers), and the Concord Council chambers, a recurring location in the main story ([037-Main-Story.md](037-Main-Story.md)).

## Ashka Vor — Orc Clan Capital, Vethmoor
Duskward Pact stronghold carved into a volcanic caldera. Home to the Warcouncil of Clans and the primary Duskward class trainers for levels 25+.

## Ironpeak Hold — Dwarven Capital, Vethmoor
A fortress-city built into the mountain itself, home to the dwarven forgemasters and the continent's primary blacksmithing and enchanting trainers ([066-Blacksmithing.md](066-Blacksmithing.md), [059-Enchanting.md](059-Enchanting.md)).

## City Design Requirements
* Must contain: bank, auction house access, class trainers for local factions, flight/fast-travel point ([021-Fast-Travel.md](021-Fast-Travel.md)), and a guild hall.
* Must be built with distinct architecture reflecting the [130-Art-Style.md](130-Art-Style.md) guide and the dominant race's culture.
* Must tie into at least one main story or faction questline.

Additional cities are added as continents expand — see [011-Continents.md](011-Continents.md) for the full continent list.
