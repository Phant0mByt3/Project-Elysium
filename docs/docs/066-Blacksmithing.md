# 66 — Blacksmithing

## Overview
Blacksmithing is a production profession converting ore/bars (gathered via [061-Mining.md](061-Mining.md)) into weapons and Plate/Mail armor ([050-Weapons.md](050-Weapons.md), [051-Armour.md](051-Armour.md)).

## Core Loop
Learn a recipe → gather bars and secondary materials → craft at a forge → produce weapons or heavy armor pieces.

## Cultural Tie
Blacksmithing is thematically anchored to the dwarven Ironpeak Holds and their patron god Terravox ([032-Gods.md](032-Gods.md)), with the profession's highest-level trainers located there ([013-Cities.md](013-Cities.md)).

## Recipe Categories
* **Weapons** — one/two-handed melee weapon lines.
* **Plate & Mail Armor** — heavy armor pieces for Warrior/Paladin (plate) and Ranger/Druid secondary (mail).
* **Shields** — the primary crafted source of tank off-hand items.
* **Tool Upgrades** — improved pickaxes/axes for gathering professions, a small but useful cross-profession utility line.

## Endgame Relevance
Blacksmiths should have access to a small number of raid-material-gated recipes, keeping the profession relevant at the level cap alongside dungeon/raid drops ([054-Loot-Tables.md](054-Loot-Tables.md)).
