## 1. Overview

Developer Guidelines are the practical “how we work” companion to formal standards: communication, ownership, definition of done, and expected professionalism.

---

## 2. Expectations

- Document as you build  
- Prefer small, reviewable changes  
- Leave code and systems better than you found them  
- Escalate risk early  
- Respect the vision and pillars when making trade-offs  


---

## Additional Detail: New Contributor Checklist

New contributors follow a documented onboarding checklist covering environment setup ([1410-Developer-Environment.md](1410-Developer-Environment.md)), required reading (this GDD's Project and Core Pillars documents), and a small first task designed to walk them through the full contribution pipeline from branch to review to merge.

## Living Guidelines

These guidelines are revisited whenever the team identifies a recurring point of confusion or friction for new or existing contributors, ensuring onboarding and workflow guidance stays current with how the team actually works rather than how it worked at project inception.
