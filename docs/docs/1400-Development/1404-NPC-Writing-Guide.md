# 1404 — NPC Writing Guide

## Overview
Standards for writing consistent, memorable NPC dialogue and characterization, extending the quest writing guide ([1403-Quest-Writing-Guide.md](1403-Quest-Writing-Guide.md)) to cover characters as a category unto themselves.

## Every NPC Needs
* A name, home location, and role, tracked in [0209-NPCs.md](../0200-Lore/0209-NPCs.md).
* A one-sentence "voice" description (e.g. "gruff but secretly sentimental," "formal to the point of coldness") that any writer can pick up and stay consistent with.
* At least one line of ambient/idle dialogue beyond their quest text, for immersion (Pillar in [0002-Core-Pillars.md](../0000-Project/0002-Core-Pillars.md)).

## Race & Culture Consistency
NPC dialogue should reflect their race and culture as established in [0204-Races.md](../0200-Lore/0204-Races.md) — a dwarven blacksmith and a high-elf scholar should never sound interchangeable.

## Faction Consistency
Dawnbound-aligned NPCs should reflect Concord values (order, cooperative rebuilding); Duskward-aligned NPCs should reflect Pact values (self-reliance, skepticism of the old order) — see [0203-Factions.md](../0200-Lore/0203-Factions.md). Avoid making one faction cartoonishly "evil"; both should read as sincere, defensible worldviews per the evenhanded framing in [0200-Lore.md](../0200-Lore/0200-Lore.md).

## Reuse
Before creating a new NPC, check [0209-NPCs.md](../0200-Lore/0209-NPCs.md) — an existing character may fit the narrative need better than a new one.


## Avoiding Stereotype Pitfalls

Race and culture-consistent dialogue (per [0204-Races.md](../0200-Lore/0204-Races.md)) is written to reflect genuine cultural distinctiveness without leaning on reductive stereotypes — writers are encouraged to ground dialogue in specific, established lore details rather than generic "fantasy race" tropes.

## Recurring Character Continuity

NPCs who reappear across multiple quest chains or acts of the Main Quest ([0708-Main-Quest.md](../0700-Quests/0708-Main-Quest.md)) require a dedicated continuity note tracking their current knowledge, relationships, and emotional state at each point in the story, preventing contradictions as multiple writers may touch the same character over time.
