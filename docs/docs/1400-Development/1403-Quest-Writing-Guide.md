# 1403 — Quest Writing Guide

## Overview
Standards for writing quest text, ensuring every quest ([0700-Quests.md](../0700-Quests/0700-Quests.md)) reads as part of a coherent world rather than generic MMO filler.

## Tone
Grounded high fantasy, consistent with the lore's themes of reconstruction and fragmentation ([0200-Lore.md](../0200-Lore/0200-Lore.md)) — sincere rather than jokey, though individual NPCs may have distinct comedic or dry voices per [1404-NPC-Writing-Guide.md](1404-NPC-Writing-Guide.md).

## Structure
* **Hook** — the first line should establish why the NPC needs the player's help, not just what to do.
* **Objective Text** — clear, concise, in the NPC's voice where possible rather than generic system text.
* **Completion Text** — should acknowledge what the player did, not just hand over a reward blankly.
* **No unmotivated fetch quests** — every "kill X" or "collect Y" objective needs a stated reason tied to the region's local conflict ([0102-Regions.md](../0100-World/0102-Regions.md)).

## Consistency Checks
* Cross-reference [0209-NPCs.md](../0200-Lore/0209-NPCs.md) before introducing a new named character.
* Cross-reference [0200-Lore.md](../0200-Lore/0200-Lore.md) through [0206-History.md](../0200-Lore/0206-History.md) for any historical or mythological claim made in quest text.
* Main story quests must align with the act structure in [0207-Main-Story.md](../0200-Lore/0207-Main-Story.md); side quests should never contradict it.


## Voice Consistency Across Writers

With multiple writers contributing quest content, a shared style reference (tone examples, a list of words/phrases to avoid, sample "good" and "bad" quest text) keeps voice consistent even as different people write different regions — reviewed and maintained by the Narrative Lead.

## Localization-Friendly Writing

Quest text avoids idioms, puns, or wordplay that don't translate well, in anticipation of eventual localization to additional languages, keeping the writing accessible to the broadest possible future audience without sacrificing personality.
