# 33 — Factions

## Player Factions

### The Dawnbound Concord
Founded in the late Age of Ash by survivors who believe Elysium should be rebuilt in the image of the old Age of Concord — order, cooperation, and a return to the old capitals. Aligned with Solthar. Dominant among humans, high elves, and dwarves. Capital: Solmere ([13-Cities.md](13-Cities.md)).

### The Duskward Pact
A rival philosophy formed by those who believe the old order failed and should not be rebuilt — that survival in the new Elysium requires strength, self-reliance, and new alliances rather than nostalgia. Aligned loosely with Nyxara and Ignareth. Dominant among orcs, beastkin, and Revenants. Capital: Ashka Vor.

Faction choice is made at character creation and determines starting continent quests, city access, and PvP allegiance ([84-PvP.md](84-PvP.md)). See [77-Factions-Reputation.md](77-Factions-Reputation.md) for the reputation grind systems tied to each.

## Neutral Organizations

**The Wayfarer's Guild** — a cross-faction merchant and courier organization operating out of neutral trading posts; source of the Auction House's cross-faction listings ([103-Auction-House.md](103-Auction-House.md)).

**The Cartographer's League** — scholars and explorers dedicated to remapping the reconnecting continents; source of exploration-focused achievements ([74-Achievements.md](74-Achievements.md)) and landmark lore ([15-Landmarks.md](15-Landmarks.md)).

**The Ashen Circle** — a secretive scholarly order studying Kaelgorath's remaining influence, appearing in questlines tied to corruption and the Nightreach expansion ([152-Expansion-03.md](152-Expansion-03.md)).

## Enemy Factions
Region-specific hostile factions (bandit crews, undead cults, warbands) are documented alongside their regions in [12-Regions.md](12-Regions.md) rather than here, since they are local rather than world-spanning.
