# 92 — Pets

## Overview
Pets are non-combat cosmetic companions, distinct from Ranger/Necromancer combat pet mechanics (which are class-specific skill features documented under [40-Classes.md](40-Classes.md), not here).

## Acquisition
* Quest rewards, world exploration ([15-Landmarks.md](15-Landmarks.md)), achievements ([74-Achievements.md](74-Achievements.md)), and rare drops.
* Vendor-purchased pets using premium or Aurum currency ([101-Currency.md](101-Currency.md)) as a cosmetic revenue stream.

## Design Rules
* Cosmetic pets have no combat function and no stat impact — purely a follow-you companion for flavor.
* Pet variety should draw from the bestiary of each region as it's built, giving players a themed way to "take home" a piece of a region they enjoyed exploring.

## Relationship to Combat Pets
Ranger Beastmaster and Necromancer minion pets are functionally and visually distinct from cosmetic companion pets to avoid player confusion — see [41-Specializations.md](41-Specializations.md) for those class-specific systems.
