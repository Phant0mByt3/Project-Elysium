## 1. Overview

Iconography is the broader visual language of symbols used across UI, maps, factions, and world signage. It extends the specific icon production standards in [1304-Icons.md](1304-Icons.md).

---

## 2. Principles

- Strong silhouettes and limited detail for small sizes.
- Consistent metaphor language (e.g. how “danger”, “quest”, “vendor” are expressed).
- Faction and elemental symbols remain distinct under colourblind simulation.
- Reuse and systematic variation over one-off invention where possible.


---

## Additional Detail: Symbolic Consistency

Recurring in-world symbols (the Concord's sunburst, the Pact's broken chain motif, each god's personal sigil from [0202-Gods.md](../0200-Lore/0202-Gods.md)) are used consistently across banners, architecture, and UI elements, reinforcing world lore visually without requiring additional text.

## Wayfinding Iconography

Map and UI icons for points of interest use a consistent, quickly scannable icon language (distinct silhouettes for dungeons, landmarks, vendors, quest givers) developed alongside [1304-Icons.md](1304-Icons.md) and the map UI in [1119-Map-UI.md](../1100-Client/1119-Map-UI.md).
