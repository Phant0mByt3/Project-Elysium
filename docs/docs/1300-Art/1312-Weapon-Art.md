## 1. Overview

Weapon Art defines the visual language of weapons across types and rarities: silhouette, ornamentation, material, and how power level is communicated without sacrificing readability.

---

## 2. Principles

- Weapon type is recognisable at a glance (sword vs axe vs staff, etc.).
- Higher rarities gain ornament and effect density carefully so they remain legible.
- Class and culture flavour can appear in weapon design without breaking type identity.
- Models and effects respect performance budgets.


---

## Additional Detail: Legendary Weapon Distinctiveness

Legendary and Relic-tier weapons ([0505-Legendary-Items.md](../0500-Items/0505-Legendary-Items.md), [0506-Relics.md](../0500-Items/0506-Relics.md)) receive bespoke visual treatment — unique particle trails, glow effects, or silhouette details — ensuring they're immediately recognizable across a crowded raid even before checking a tooltip.

## Weapon Scaling with Item Level

Within a weapon type, visual complexity escalates with item level (simple iron sword → ornately detailed, faintly glowing weapon at raid tier), giving players a readable at-a-glance sense of relative gear progression, tying directly into [0500-Weapons.md](../0500-Items/0500-Weapons.md).
