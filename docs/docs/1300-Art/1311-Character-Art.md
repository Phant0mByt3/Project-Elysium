## 1. Overview

Character Art covers the visual design and production of playable races, NPCs, and humanoid entities: proportions, silhouette, material treatment, and customisation ranges.

---

## 2. Principles

- Silhouette readability at combat distances.
- Racial identity preserved under customisation.
- Shared base meshes and texture approaches where possible for efficiency.
- Alignment with the grounded high-fantasy tone.

---

## 3. Scope

Player races, major NPC archetypes, and the customisation options exposed at character creation and through later cosmetics.


## 4. Customization Range

Character creation ([0310-Character-Creation.md](../0300-Characters/0310-Character-Creation.md)) offers a wide but curated range of customization options per race, balancing player self-expression against the production cost of supporting truly unlimited combinations, and against maintaining each race's core visual identity described in [0204-Races.md](../0200-Lore/0204-Races.md).

## 5. Armor Silhouette Consistency

Character art works closely with itemization ([0501-Armour.md](../0500-Items/0501-Armour.md)) to ensure each armor class (Cloth/Leather/Mail/Plate) maintains a consistent, recognizable silhouette across every race's body type, so a player's equipped gear type remains readable regardless of which race is wearing it.
