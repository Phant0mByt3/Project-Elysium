## 1. Overview

Armour Art covers the visual design of cloth, leather, mail, and plate across races and rarities, including set identity and transmog-friendly construction.

---

## 2. Principles

- Armour weight class remains readable from silhouette and material.
- Set pieces feel cohesive when worn together.
- Customisation and transmog work without destroying class or role readability.
- Cultural and faction flavour appears in motifs and colour accents.


---

## Additional Detail: Tier Set Visual Cohesion

Armor pieces from the same dungeon or raid tier set ([0512-Item-Sets.md](../0500-Items/0512-Item-Sets.md)) share a unified visual theme so a fully-equipped set reads as a deliberate, cohesive look rather than mismatched individual pieces.

## Faction and Cultural Armor Variants

Certain craftable and reputation-reward armor sets carry Dawnbound Concord or Duskward Pact visual motifs, giving faction-aligned players armor that visibly signals their allegiance beyond just their character's starting appearance.
