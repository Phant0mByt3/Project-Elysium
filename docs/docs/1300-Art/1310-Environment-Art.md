## 1. Overview

Environment Art defines the visual treatment of terrain, foliage, props, skyboxes, and atmospheric effects that make each region and biome feel distinct and handcrafted.

---

## 2. Principles

- Regional palettes and materials are consistent with the continent and biome design.
- Hero props and landmarks receive extra detail; background elements stay readable at distance.
- Tiling and repetition are broken up intentionally.
- Performance budgets for density and draw calls are respected.

---

## 3. Deliverables

Texture sets, prop models, foliage packs, sky and weather art, and kit pieces for builders to assemble handcrafted spaces.
