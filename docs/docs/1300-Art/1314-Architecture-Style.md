## 1. Overview

Architecture Style defines how buildings, cities, and structures express culture, faction, and region: proportions, materials, motifs, and skyline.

---

## 2. Regional Directions

- **Aurelia / Concord** — pale stone, gold accents, ordered civic geometry, solar motifs
- **Vethmoor Dwarven** — heavy stone, iron, deep halls, geometric runes
- **Vethmoor Orcish** — bone, hide, timber, aggressive silhouettes, clan marks
- Future continents receive their own architectural language when scoped

---

## 3. Principles

- Cities read as places where people live and work, not as prop farms.
- Landmarks and civic buildings are legible from distance.
- Kit pieces support builders while preserving style consistency.
