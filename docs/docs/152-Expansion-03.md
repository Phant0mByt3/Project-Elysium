# 152 — Expansion 03: Nightreach

## Overview
The third planned expansion and the intended narrative climax of Elysium's initial multi-year story arc — introducing **Nightreach**, a continent still visibly warped by the Sundering and home to the strongest remaining concentration of Kaelgorath's influence ([011-Continents.md](011-Continents.md), [032-Gods.md](032-Gods.md)).

## New Content
* **Continent** — Nightreach, level range 80–95, endgame-focused with a higher density of raid and Mythic-tier content relative to leveling zones.
* **Race Origin** — reveals the full origin story of the Revenant race ([034-Races.md](034-Races.md)), whose first sighting was recorded here per [036-History.md](036-History.md).
* **New Systems** — a corruption/exposure mechanic unique to Nightreach, affecting both narrative choices and a light risk/reward gameplay layer for exploring the most corrupted zones.
* **Culminating Raid** — the intended confrontation with a major fragment of Kaelgorath himself, positioned as Elysium's most significant raid encounter to date.

## Narrative Role
Nightreach is intended to resolve the central "should Elysium be rebuilt or remade" tension established in [030-Lore.md](030-Lore.md), giving players a meaningful narrative choice point affecting the tone of subsequent content.

## Status
Planned; not yet scheduled on the active roadmap. See [005-Future-Plans.md](005-Future-Plans.md).
