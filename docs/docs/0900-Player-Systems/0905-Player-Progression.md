## 3. Progression Pacing

| Phase | Primary Progression Layer | Secondary Layers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leveling (1–50) | Character Level | Gear, light reputation |
| Early Endgame | Gear / Item Level | Reputation, professions |
| Established Endgame | Achievements, Collections | Guild standing, PvP rating |
| Long-Term | Account Progression | All layers, at a slower cadence |

## 4. Design Philosophy

No single layer should feel mandatory in isolation — a player who cares only about story and exploration should reach level cap satisfied, while a player chasing every layer (Mythic raid clears, Exalted reputation, full mount collection) should have months of goals beyond hitting max level. This layered structure is what allows Elysium to support the multi-year live service vision in [0001-Vision.md](../0000-Project/0001-Vision.md) and [0003-Roadmap.md](../0000-Project/0003-Roadmap.md).

## 5. Account-Wide vs Character-Specific Progression

Certain layers (collections, achievements, mounts, pets) are account-wide, rewarding players who create alternate characters, while others (character level, gear, professions) remain character-specific, giving both single-character and multi-character playstyles meaningful long-term goals — see [0917-Account-Progression.md](0917-Account-Progression.md).

## 6. Progression Visibility

The character profile screen ([0911-Character-Profile.md](0911-Character-Profile.md)) surfaces a summary view across all progression layers, helping players understand at a glance where they stand and what to pursue next.
