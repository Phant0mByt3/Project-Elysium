# 0902 — Pets

**Category:** Player Systems
**Status:** Living Document
**Related:** [0903-Cosmetics.md](0903-Cosmetics.md) · [0300-Classes.md](../0300-Characters/0300-Classes.md)

---

## 1. Overview

Pets are non-combat cosmetic companions, distinct from Wayfarer's combat pet mechanics (a class-specific specialization feature documented under [0301-Specializations.md](../0300-Characters/0301-Specializations.md), not here).

## 2. Acquisition

* Quest rewards, world exploration ([0105-Landmarks.md](../0100-World/0105-Landmarks.md)), achievements ([0704-Achievements.md](../0700-Quests/0704-Achievements.md)), and rare drops.
* Vendor-purchased pets using Aurum currency ([1001-Currency.md](../1000-Economy/1001-Currency.md)) as a cosmetic revenue stream.

## 3. Pet Behaviors

Cosmetic pets follow the player passively and may occasionally perform small idle animations or reactive behaviors (perking up near combat, reacting to emotes), giving them a bit of personality without any mechanical function.

## 4. Design Rules

* Cosmetic pets have no combat function and no stat impact — purely a follow-you companion for flavor.
* Pet variety should draw from the bestiary of each region as it's built, giving players a themed way to "take home" a piece of a region they enjoyed exploring.

## 5. Regional Pet Examples

* **Fenling** (Greywater Fens) — a small marsh-adapted creature earned through the Fenwick Reckoning quest chain ([0701-Quest-Chains.md](../0700-Quests/0701-Quest-Chains.md)).
* **Ember Pup** (Ember Deeps) — a rare drop from the Ember Foundry dungeon.

## 6. Relationship to Combat Pets

Wayfarer's Beastmaster specialization pets are functionally and visually distinct from cosmetic companion pets to avoid player confusion — see [0301-Specializations.md](../0300-Characters/0301-Specializations.md) for that class-specific system, which involves an actively controlled, combat-participating companion rather than a passive cosmetic follower.

## 7. Collection Tracking

Pet collection progress is tracked through the account-wide Collections system ([0907-Collections.md](0907-Collections.md)), alongside mounts and transmog appearances.
