# 0903 — Cosmetics

**Status:** Living Document

## 1. Overview

Cosmetics are the umbrella system for all non-power visual customization: transmogrification, dyes, cosmetic pets ([0902-Pets.md](0902-Pets.md)), mounts ([0901-Mounts.md](0901-Mounts.md)), housing decor ([0900-Housing.md](0900-Housing.md)), and emotes ([0904-Emotes.md](0904-Emotes.md)).

## 2. Transmogrification

Players may apply the visual appearance of any previously-owned gear piece to their current gear, decoupling stats from appearance and letting players maintain a consistent look through gear upgrades — see [0513-Transmog-System.md](../0500-Items/0513-Transmog-System.md) for the full itemization rules.

## 3. Dyes

A color-customization system for eligible armor pieces, acquired through professions ([0508-Crafting.md](../0500-Items/0508-Crafting.md)) and vendors, allowing players to further personalize a transmog appearance beyond its default coloring.

## 4. Acquisition Philosophy

Cosmetics should be earned primarily through play — achievements, raids, seasonal events ([0807-Seasons.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0807-Seasons.md)) — with any premium-currency cosmetic offerings kept clearly separate from gameplay-affecting rewards, preserving the "quality over quantity" and immersion-first pillars in [0002-Core-Pillars.md](../0000-Project/0002-Core-Pillars.md).

## 5. Historical Titles

**Examples:**

* First Fleet Member
* First Guild to Conquer The Sunken Concord
* Ancient Archivist
* Reclaimer of Aethercrest

These are identity rewards, not power rewards — see [0705-Titles.md](../0700-Quests/0705-Titles.md) for the full title system.

## 6. Faction and Cultural Cosmetics

Certain cosmetic sets are tied to faction reputation or regional culture (Dawnbound Concord ceremonial gear, Duskward Pact war-paint appearances), giving cosmetic collecting a way to express identity within the world's political and cultural landscape rather than being purely arbitrary skins.

## 7. Premium Currency Boundaries

If a premium currency store is introduced post-launch, it is restricted to cosmetic-only content by policy — no gameplay-affecting items, boosts, or convenience items that create a power gap, a boundary treated as non-negotiable per [0001-Vision.md](../0000-Project/0001-Vision.md).

## 8. Design Rules

No cosmetic item should ever carry a stat or gameplay effect. This is a hard rule, not a guideline, to keep character power entirely separate from character appearance.
