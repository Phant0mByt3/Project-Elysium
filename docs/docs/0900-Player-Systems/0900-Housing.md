# 0900 — Housing

**Category:** Player Systems
**Status:** Living Document
**Related:** [0903-Cosmetics.md](0903-Cosmetics.md) · [0815-Guild-Halls.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0815-Guild-Halls.md)

---

## 1. Overview

Player housing gives every character a personal, instanced plot to decorate and customize, using Unreal Engine's modular building-piece system as a genuine gameplay feature rather than fighting against it.

## 2. Core Features

* **Instanced Plots** — each character receives a personal housing instance, accessible via a portal item or city housing district entrance.
* **Decoration** — a curated palette of placeable furniture and decor items, sourced from crafting professions ([0508-Crafting.md](../0500-Items/0508-Crafting.md)), quest rewards, and cosmetic vendors ([0903-Cosmetics.md](0903-Cosmetics.md)).
* **Trophy Display** — special decor items unlocked from raid ([0107-Raids.md](../0100-World/0107-Raids.md)) and achievement ([0704-Achievements.md](../0700-Quests/0704-Achievements.md)) milestones.

## 3. Housing Themes

Plots are offered in a small set of base layouts reflecting each launch culture (a Solmere townhouse, an Ironpeak Hold stone dwelling, an Ashka Vor longhouse), giving players a thematically appropriate starting point regardless of their character's race or faction.

## 4. Design Rules

* Housing should never grant combat power — pure self-expression and prestige, in line with [0903-Cosmetics.md](0903-Cosmetics.md)'s philosophy.
* Decoration should use the curated palette of housing pieces rather than full unrestricted free-building, to keep housing visually consistent with [1300-Art-Style.md](../1300-Art/1300-Art-Style.md) rather than reintroducing "generic sandbox-build" aesthetics that undermine the game's immersion, per [0001-Vision.md](../0000-Project/0001-Vision.md).
* Housing plots should not require constant upkeep or decay mechanics — decoration is a one-time or occasional creative act, not a chore.

## 5. Social Visitation

Players can open their housing plot to friends, party members, or guildmates for visits, giving housing a light social dimension beyond purely personal decoration.

## 6. Acquisition and Cost

A basic housing plot is available at no cost once a player reaches a modest level threshold, with additional plot size or premium locations available through Aurum or achievement-gated unlocks, keeping baseline housing accessible to every player.

## 7. Future: Guild Neighborhoods

Shared guild-owned housing districts are a confirmed future direction — see [0800-Guilds.md](../0800-Multiplayer/0800-Guilds.md) and [0005-Future-Plans.md](../0000-Project/0005-Future-Plans.md).
