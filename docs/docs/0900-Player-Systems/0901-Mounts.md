# 91 — Mounts

## Overview
Mounts are the primary speed-boosting travel tool, unlocked starting at level 15 ([0305-Leveling.md](../0300-Characters/0305-Leveling.md)), and a major collection/cosmetic goal at all levels ([0704-Achievements.md](../0700-Quests/0704-Achievements.md)).

## Acquisition
* **Starter Mounts** — purchasable from any city stable once level-eligible.
* **Faction Mounts** — reputation-gated, Revered-tier rewards ([0707-Factions-Reputation.md](../0700-Quests/0707-Factions-Reputation.md)).
* **Rare/World Boss Mounts** — low-drop-chance rewards from world bosses ([0108-World-Bosses.md](../0100-World/0108-World-Bosses.md)) and raid bosses ([0107-Raids.md](../0100-World/0107-Raids.md)).
* **Achievement Mounts** — earned through exploration or completionist achievements ([0704-Achievements.md](../0700-Quests/0704-Achievements.md)).

## Mount Types
Ground mounts only at launch (see [0110-Travel.md](../0100-World/0110-Travel.md)); flying mounts are explicitly out of scope for launch to preserve the value of hand-built ground travel and world exploration, revisited as a future direction ([0005-Future-Plans.md](../0000-Project/0005-Future-Plans.md)).

## Design Rules
Mount speed should be standardized (no pay-for-speed or grind-for-speed disparity) so that mount collecting remains purely cosmetic/prestige, never a power or convenience treadmill — consistent with [0903-Cosmetics.md](0903-Cosmetics.md)'s philosophy.
