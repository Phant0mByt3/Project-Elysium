# 0901 — Mounts

**Category:** Player Systems
**Status:** Living Document
**Related:** [0900-Housing.md](0900-Housing.md) · [0110-Travel.md](../0100-World/0110-Travel.md)

---

## 1. Overview

Mounts are the primary speed-boosting travel tool, unlocked starting at level 15 ([0305-Leveling.md](../0300-Characters/0305-Leveling.md)), and a major collection/cosmetic goal at all levels ([0704-Achievements.md](../0700-Quests/0704-Achievements.md)).

## 2. Acquisition

* **Starter Mounts** — purchasable from any city stable once level-eligible.
* **Faction Mounts** — reputation-gated, Revered-tier rewards ([0707-Factions-Reputation.md](../0700-Quests/0707-Factions-Reputation.md)).
* **Rare/World Boss Mounts** — low-drop-chance rewards from world bosses ([0108-World-Bosses.md](../0100-World/0108-World-Bosses.md)) and raid bosses ([0107-Raids.md](../0100-World/0107-Raids.md)).
* **Achievement Mounts** — earned through exploration or completionist achievements ([0704-Achievements.md](../0700-Quests/0704-Achievements.md)).

## 3. Mount Types

Ground mounts only at launch (see [0110-Travel.md](../0100-World/0110-Travel.md)); flying mounts are explicitly out of scope for launch to preserve the value of hand-built ground travel and world exploration, revisited as a future direction ([0005-Future-Plans.md](../0000-Project/0005-Future-Plans.md)).

## 4. Regional and Cultural Mounts

Mount appearances draw from each region's cultural identity — sturdy plains horses in Aurelia, mountain goats and war-boars in Vethmoor — giving mount collecting a way to reflect the world's geography and culture rather than being a generic stable of interchangeable horses.

## 5. Mount Speed Tiers

| Tier | Speed Bonus | Unlock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | Standard | Level 15, city vendor |
| Enhanced | Moderate | Faction reputation, quest reward |
| Prestige | Cosmetic only, same speed as Enhanced | Achievement, raid, world boss |

## 6. Design Rules

Mount speed should be standardized within a tier (no pay-for-speed or grind-for-speed disparity beyond the basic-to-enhanced unlock) so that mount collecting remains purely cosmetic/prestige beyond that single practical upgrade, consistent with [0903-Cosmetics.md](0903-Cosmetics.md)'s philosophy.

## 7. Guild and Group Mounts

Select rare mounts support multiple riders (a two-seat option for select large mounts), offering a niche social/roleplay use case for guild events and screenshots without affecting standard solo travel balance.
