# 57 — Consumables

## Categories

**Potions** — instant or duration effects (healing, mana restore, temporary stat buffs); crafted via Alchemy ([65-Alchemy.md](65-Alchemy.md)).

**Food & Drink** — longer-duration, smaller buffs, typically consumed before a dungeon/raid pull; crafted via Cooking ([67-Cooking.md](67-Cooking.md)).

**Scrolls** — one-time utility effects (temporary teleport, buff duration extension); sourced from vendors or rare quest rewards.

**Bandages/Field Kits** — self-healing tools with a cast time, giving non-healer players a limited out-of-combat sustain option.

## Design Rules
* Combat consumables (potions, scrolls) should share a limited number of cooldown categories to prevent stacking too many effects at once trivializing difficulty.
* Food and potion buffs should never be mandatory for normal-difficulty content, only for Heroic/Mythic dungeons and raids ([16-Dungeons.md](16-Dungeons.md), [17-Raids.md](17-Raids.md)), to keep the leveling and casual experience approachable.
* Consumables should be a meaningful Aurum sink and profession revenue source — see [100-Economy.md](100-Economy.md).

## Acquisition
Primarily player-crafted (Alchemy, Cooking) or vendor-purchased with basic recipes; rare/powerful consumables may be quest or drop-gated.
