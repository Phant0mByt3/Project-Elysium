# 114 — Soundtrack

## Overview
An original musical score, distinct from Minecraft's default soundtrack entirely, reinforcing each continent, faction, and major story moment with its own musical identity.

## Structure
* **Continent Themes** — a distinct musical identity per continent ([0101-Continents.md](../0100-World/0101-Continents.md)): Aurelia's pastoral, hopeful themes vs. Vethmoor's weightier, percussive mountain themes.
* **Faction Themes** — motifs for the Dawnbound Concord (choral, bright) and Duskward Pact (rhythmic, low-brass) woven into faction-specific content ([0203-Factions.md](../0200-Lore/0203-Factions.md)).
* **Combat Music** — dynamic layers that intensify for world bosses, dungeon bosses, and raid encounters ([0106-Dungeons.md](../0100-World/0106-Dungeons.md), [0107-Raids.md](../0100-World/0107-Raids.md)).
* **Story Themes** — a recurring main theme for the central narrative ([0207-Main-Story.md](../0200-Lore/0207-Main-Story.md)), reprised and varied at key story beats.

## Delivery
Music files are bundled into the resource pack ([1102-Resource-Pack.md](1102-Resource-Pack.md)) and distributed via the launcher.

## Design Rules
Music should never loop jarringly or interrupt combat music transitions — smooth crossfades are a requirement, not a nice-to-have, for maintaining immersion per [0001-Vision.md](../0000-Project/0001-Vision.md).
